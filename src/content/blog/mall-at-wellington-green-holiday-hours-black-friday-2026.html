Planning holiday shopping at The Mall at Wellington Green? The mall is closed on Thanksgiving, opens at 8 a.m. on Black Friday (Nov. 27) and stays open until 9 p.m., then stretches its hours through Christmas Eve. Here's the full 2026 holiday hours schedule, plus Black Friday and Small Business Saturday events, Santa photo times, Sensory Santa, pet photos with Santa, the Hanukkah celebration and tips for beating the crowds.

Black Friday at The Mall at Wellington Green

On Friday, Nov. 27, the mall opens early at 8 a.m. and stays open until 9 p.m., two hours earlier and one hour later than a normal Friday.

Best Friend Friday: from 10 a.m. to noon in District Court, next to H&M, the mall is hosting a morning event with mimosas, giveaways, swag and a fashion show featuring holiday looks. Bring a shopping friend.

Anchor stores at the mall include Macy's and Dillard's, along with Apple, H&M, Brighton, Chico's, Helzberg Diamonds and Tommy Bahama, according to the mall. For a full store list and map, see our Mall at Wellington Green directory guide.

Small Business Saturday, Nov. 28

The mall says it's home to more than 50 small businesses, and on Small Business Saturday it's encouraging shoppers to look for a Small Business Saturday sign displayed in participating storefronts. Hours are 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.

Want to shop small beyond the mall? Wellington has local boutiques, cafes and service businesses in shopping centers like the Courtyard Shops on Wellington Trace and along State Road 7. Browse the Wellington List directory for local shops and restaurants.

Photos with Santa: 2026 schedule

Santa is at the mall from Nov. 13 through Christmas Eve. Walk-ins are welcome, but the mall strongly encourages reservations. Santa takes a break from 3 to 4 p.m. on longer days.

  • Nov. 13-25: weekdays 2 to 7 p.m.; Saturdays 11 a.m. to 7 p.m.; Sundays noon to 5 p.m.
  • Nov. 26: mall closed.
  • Black Friday, Nov. 27: 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.
  • Nov. 28: 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. Nov. 29: noon to 5 p.m.
  • Nov. 30-Dec. 4: 2 to 7 p.m.
  • Dec. 5-12: Monday through Saturday 11 a.m. to 7 p.m.; Sunday noon to 5 p.m.
  • Dec. 13: 9 a.m. to 8 p.m.

Check the mall's Santa page for the schedule from Dec. 14 through Christmas Eve, which often has the longest lines. The mall was offering an online-only early-bird discount code on an Elite photo package for reservations made by Nov. 23.

Sensory Santa and pet photos

  • Sensory Santa, Sunday, Dec. 6, 10 to 11 a.m.: before the mall opens, families of children with autism and other special needs can visit Santa in a calmer, quieter setting. Reservations are highly encouraged.
  • Pet Photos with Santa, Monday, Nov. 30 and Monday, Dec. 7, 5 to 7 p.m.: bring your dog for a photo with Santa. All pets must stay leashed. Reserve through the mall's website. More dog ideas are in our dog-friendly Wellington guide.

More holiday events at the mall

  • Santa's Arrival Pajama Party, Saturday, Nov. 14, 5 to 7 p.m.: a Kids Club event in District Court with a bubble show, swag and milk and cookies while supplies last. Wear festive PJs.
  • Tea Time and Tall Hats, Wednesday, Nov. 18, 10:30 a.m.: a Mommy and Me storytime with tea and giveaways.
  • Giving Tree, Nov. 13-Dec. 13: pick up a tag at the Giving Tree in front of the Santa set, buy the requested gift and return it unwrapped with the tag to help a local veteran family, in partnership with Stand Down.
  • Musicthon, Dec. 5 and 6, 11 a.m. to 6 p.m.: students of the Palm Beach County Music Teachers Association perform.
  • Hanukkah Celebration, Sunday, Dec. 6, 3 to 6 p.m.: with Chabad of Wellington, in District Court across from H&M, featuring treats, face painting and live entertainment.
  • Holiday Gift Workshop, Saturday, Dec. 12, 11 a.m. to 1 p.m.: kids 12 and under make and wrap a handmade gift.
  • Storytime with Mrs. Claus, Wednesday, Dec. 16, 10:30 a.m.: for children 5 and under, with Cinnabon decorating.
  • Free Zumba: every Saturday from 10 to 11 a.m. in the District Studio across from H&M.

Tips for holiday shopping in Wellington

  • Plan Black Friday. The mall opens at 8 a.m., but individual stores may open at different times, so check your must-visit stores first.
  • Book Santa ahead. Reservations cut the wait, especially on weekends and the week before Christmas.
  • Make it a night out. The mall's upper level is home to the Palm Beach Improv, and nearby Lotis Wellington has restaurants. See our Lotis guide and date night restaurants.
  • Plan around Thanksgiving closures. Village facilities and the library close on Thanksgiving too. See our Thanksgiving in Wellington guide.
  • More holiday fun: the village tree lighting is Dec. 3. See our Wellington holiday events and holiday lights guides.
