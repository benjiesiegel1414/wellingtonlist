We picked these from the burger joints, pubs and grills on Wellington List, covering quick lunches, family dinners and late nights. Every spot below links to its full Wellington List listing. 1. Hausmash

Hausmash opened in Wellington Green Square in October 2025, and it's the village's go-to for smash burgers. Thin, crisp-edged patties of certified Angus beef go on brioche buns, the fries are cooked in beef tallow, and the whole place is decked out in 1980s style. The menu keeps it tight: six burgers, six milkshakes and five kinds of fries.

What to try: The Rockin' Bacon Burger with truffle and pecorino fries and a Nutella milkshake.

Tip: It's open until 11 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays.

  • Address: 10660 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 170, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 284-6400
  • Hours: Sun-Thu 11:30 a.m.-10 p.m.; Fri-Sat 11:30 a.m.-11 p.m.
  • See the full Hausmash listing

2. Lindburgers

Lindburgers in the Wellington Trace plaza is part of a small Palm Beach County group with locations in Delray Beach and Jupiter. The menu lists roughly 50 gourmet burgers cooked to order, plus wings, hot dogs and milkshakes.

What to try: One of the specialty burgers, plus wings to share.

Tip: Happy hour runs daily from 2 to 7 p.m., and kids eat free on Mondays with an adult meal.

  • Address: 13860 Wellington Trace, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 753-0555
  • Hours: Sun-Wed 11 a.m.-9 p.m.; Thu 11 a.m.-9:30 p.m.; Fri-Sat 11 a.m.-10 p.m.
  • See the full Lindburgers listing

3. BurgerFi

BurgerFi in Wellington Plaza on West Forest Hill Blvd makes its burgers with natural Angus beef that the company says is raised without steroids, antibiotics or added hormones. It also has the VegeFi veggie burger, chicken and falafel sliders, fresh-cut fries and onion rings.

What to try: A burger with onion rings and a frozen custard shake. It also pours draft beer and wine.

  • Address: 12836 W Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 612-7853
  • Hours: Daily 11 a.m.-10 p.m.
  • See the full BurgerFi listing

4. Five Guys

Five Guys at The Pointe at Wellington Green cooks every burger to order with any of its free toppings. Fries are fresh-cut and cooked in peanut oil, and milkshakes can be blended with mix-ins.

What to try: A cheeseburger "all the way" and a regular order of fries, which is plenty to share.

Tip: Grab a handful of the free peanuts while you wait.

  • Address: 10200 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 150, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 790-7500
  • Hours: Daily 11 a.m.-10 p.m.
  • See the full Five Guys listing

5. Flanigan's

Flanigan's on State Road 7 is part of a family-run South Florida group that has been serving food and drinks since 1959. It's known for baby back ribs, burgers and fresh fish, with a full bar and sports on TV, and it's open every day until 2 a.m.

What to try: A burger and a cold drink after a late game.

  • Address: 2335 S State Road 7, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 422-0988
  • Hours: Daily 11 a.m.-2 a.m.
  • See the full Flanigan's listing

6. Kickback Neighborhood Tavern

Kickback Neighborhood Tavern took over the former Backstreets space in Wellington Plaza and opened in February 2021, with the motto "Eat Local, Drink Local, Stay Local." The kitchen serves elevated tavern food with house-made sauces and weekly chalkboard specials.

What to try: A tavern burger on the covered patio, which is dog friendly.

Tip: Look out for trivia nights, live music on weekends and the monthly car show.

  • Address: 12771 W Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 795-0100
  • See the full Kickback Neighborhood Tavern listing

7. WOB Bar & Kitchen

WOB Bar & Kitchen, formerly World of Beer, is on Southern Blvd in Royal Palm Beach. It carries about 300 beers with 40 rotating taps, plus wine and spirits, and the food menu is built to pair with beer.

What to try: A burger with a local craft beer.

Tip: It has two Topgolf Swing Suites, a private room and a pet-friendly patio, so it works for groups.

  • Address: 11121 Southern Blvd, Royal Palm Beach, FL 33411
  • See the full WOB Bar & Kitchen listing

8. Park Avenue BBQ Grille

Park Avenue BBQ Grille on Wellington Trace is best known for its baby back ribs, but the menu also includes burgers alongside barbecue chicken, fried fish and Southern sides.

What to try: A burger with a side of mac and cheese or collard greens.

Tip: It's open daily from 11 a.m. to 9:30 p.m.

  • Address: 13897 Wellington Trace, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Hours: Daily 11 a.m.-9:30 p.m.
  • See the full Park Avenue BBQ Grille listing

9. Cooper's Hawk Winery and Restaurant

For a sit-down burger with a glass of wine, Cooper's Hawk Winery and Restaurant at Lotis Wellington has burgers on its scratch-kitchen menu along with steaks, seafood and pasta, plus a wine tasting room.

What to try: A burger paired with a Cooper's Hawk wine from the tasting room.

Tip: It also serves weekend brunch and has a kids' menu.

  • Address: 1387 S State Road 7, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 922-9463
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 11 a.m.-9 p.m.; Fri 11 a.m.-10 p.m.; Sat 10 a.m.-10 p.m.; Sun 10 a.m.-9 p.m.
  • See the full Cooper's Hawk Winery and Restaurant listing

10. Wild Burger

Wild Burger is a delivery-first burger brand from Buffalo Wild Wings, cooked in the Buffalo Wild Wings kitchen on State Road 7 and sold mainly through delivery apps. It's open late, until 1:30 a.m. on Fridays and Saturdays.

What to try: The bacon smashed Hatch chile burger with tater tots.

Tip: Order it on Uber Eats, Postmates or DoorDash.

  • Address: 2837 S State Road 7, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 11 a.m.-12:30 a.m.; Fri-Sat 11 a.m.-1:30 a.m.; Sun 11 a.m.-11:30 p.m.
  • See the full Wild Burger listing

Tips for burger night in Wellington

  • Happy hours: Lindburgers runs happy hour daily from 2 to 7 p.m., and Kickback Tavern has a daily happy hour too.
  • Kids: Kids eat free at Lindburgers on Mondays with an adult meal.
  • Late night: Flanigan's is open until 2 a.m. every day, and Wild Burger delivers until 1:30 a.m. on weekends.
  • Veggie options: BurgerFi has the VegeFi veggie burger and falafel sliders, and Wild Burger has a black bean burger.
