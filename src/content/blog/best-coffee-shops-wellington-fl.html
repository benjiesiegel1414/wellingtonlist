Local coffee shops in Wellington

Candid Coffee & Anna Bakes

Two local businesses teamed up to create Candid Coffee & Anna Bakes, pairing locally roasted coffee with fresh-baked pastries. It is the go-to for Wellington locals who want specialty coffee without driving to West Palm Beach.

Address: 10120 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 160, Wellington, FL 33414

Carmela Coffee

Carmela Coffee near Wellington Green is a cozy cafe for espresso drinks, cold brew and a light bite, and a comfortable spot to meet a friend or get some work done.

Address: 10630 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414

Foxtail Coffee

Florida-based specialty roaster Foxtail Coffee anchors the coffee scene at Lotis Wellington on State Road 7, with espresso drinks, seasonal lattes and pastries. Grab a cup and walk the Lotis promenade.

More local cafes worth trying

Wellington also has a handful of smaller cafes, including Premier Cafe and Roasters on the State Road 7 side of town, Taylor Made Cafe off South Shore Blvd near the equestrian district, Cumbres Coffee Roasters and Ixkanul Coffee for specialty beans, and Lemon Drop Play Cafe, which combines coffee for parents with a play space for little kids. Hours at small cafes change often, so check before you go.

Reliable chains in Wellington

  • Starbucks, 2891 S State Road 7, plus other locations around the village.
  • Dunkin', 13501 S Shore Blvd, handy on show days near Wellington International.
  • Panera Bread, 10540 Forest Hill Blvd, with plenty of seating, Wi-Fi and food for longer work sessions.

Best coffee shops for working remotely

For a laptop session, look for places with plenty of seating and outlets. Panera and the larger cafes near Wellington Green are your best bets on weekdays. Avoid peak brunch hours on weekends, and in season (January through April), expect every coffee shop near the showgrounds to be busy on show days.

Tips for coffee in Wellington

  • Go early on weekends. Popular local spots get crowded by mid-morning.
  • Pair coffee with a walk. Grab a cup and head to the Town Center promenade along Lake Wellington.
  • Support local. Independent cafes rely on regulars, and they often have the best pastries.
