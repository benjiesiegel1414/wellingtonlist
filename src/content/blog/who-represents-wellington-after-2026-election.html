The Nov. 3, 2026 election changed who represents Wellington at nearly every level of government above the village. Palm Beach County's official results, posted Nov. 16, confirm new faces on the County Commission, in the Florida House and Florida Senate, and in Congress, and several take office on Tuesday, Nov. 17. Here's who your new officials are, what they've said they'll focus on, what the outgoing officials said, and how to find out exactly which districts your home is in.

County Commission District 6: Katherine Waldron

District 6 covers the western communities, including Wellington. Katherine Waldron, a Democrat who lives in Wellington, won 58.22% (50,913 votes) to 41.78% (36,542) for Republican incumbent Sara Baxter, an Acreage resident first elected in 2022. Waldron was sworn in on Nov. 17, and in her first votes she joined a unanimous decision to create a data center advisory committee (see our Project Tango update).

Background: Waldron served two terms on the Port of Palm Beach Commission (2017-2022) and represented Florida House District 93 from 2022 to 2024. She holds an economics degree from the University of Virginia and an MBA from Palm Beach Atlantic University. Her stated priorities include affordability, workforce housing, transportation and environmentally responsible development.

"It's an honor to return to elected service and to represent communities I have served for nearly a decade," Waldron said in a statement, thanking Baxter for "a hard-fought campaign" and promising a smooth transition.

The outgoing commissioner: Baxter said she was proud of securing road and infrastructure funding for the district and more efficient county government. She told the Town-Crier the national mood "had a huge impact," saying people "were feeling the crunch of affordability." Her advice to her successor: "Stand up for what you believe in. You're never going to make everyone happy."

Issues to watch: the proposed Project Tango AI data center on 202 acres off Southern Boulevard near Arden, drainage and growth in The Acreage, and county transportation spending. See our coverage of the county transportation plan and Acreage drainage.

Florida House District 93: August Mangeney

District 93 includes most of Wellington, plus parts of Greenacres and unincorporated Lake Worth. Democrat August Mangeney, an attorney and first-time candidate, won 56.21% (40,695 votes) to 43.79% (31,699) over Republican incumbent Anne Gerwig, a former Wellington mayor first elected to the House in 2024.

Mangeney said cost of living and education resonated with voters, and he plans to focus on property insurance, housing costs and public school funding. He said implementing Amendment 3's new homestead exemptions will be a major issue in the coming session, and he's planning a bill on "surveillance pricing" in grocery stores. "I represent everyone equally in the district," he said. "We can disagree on what is the right thing for Florida, but I will always be doing what I genuinely believe is the best thing."

Gerwig said, "While I am disappointed in the results, I appreciated all of the kind words, dedicated volunteers and contributors."

Florida House District 94: Elizabeth Holmes

District 94 covers Loxahatchee Groves, Westlake, The Acreage/Loxahatchee, parts of Royal Palm Beach and Palm Beach Gardens, and the Glades. Democrat Elizabeth Holmes, a public school teacher, won 50.36% (38,414 votes) to 47.96% (36,582) for Republican incumbent Meg Weinberger; independent Doug Barrow received 1.68%.

Holmes plans to focus on teacher pay, school funding, insurance and health care costs. She said she'll approach bills "through research and collaboration." Weinberger, who was seeking a second term, said the outcome reflected the national mood, adding, "I'm grateful to have served." She said she was most proud of passing animal protection legislation.

Florida Senate District 26: David Silvers

Former State Rep. David Silvers, a Democrat, won the open District 26 seat with 58.06% (135,408 votes) over former State Rep. Rick Roth, a Republican, with 41.94% (97,826). He replaces retiring Sen. Lori Berman. Use the Florida Senate's lookup to confirm whether your address is in District 26.

School Board District 6: John McGovern

Former Wellington Councilman John McGovern was installed as the District 6 member of the Palm Beach County School Board at the board's reorganizational meeting on Nov. 17, 2026, the Town-Crier reported. McGovern won the seat in August and replaces Marcia Andrews, who retired after four terms. For more on the schools that serve Wellington, see our Westlake boundary plan story.

U.S. House District 22: Pia Dandiya

In Palm Beach County's portion of Congressional District 22, Democrat Pia Dandiya received 56.24% (46,537 votes) to Republican Casey Askar's 43.76% (36,205). The Town-Crier reported Dandiya was elected to represent most of the western communities. The district also includes parts of Broward, Hendry and Collier counties.

The bigger picture

The local results were part of a statewide shift in legislative races. Democrats flipped 16 Republican-held seats in the Florida House, raising their number to 50 of 120 seats and ending the Republican supermajority, the Town-Crier reported, while Republicans narrowly held Florida's statewide offices. Republicans still hold a majority in the Florida House, and both new local legislators said they want to work across party lines.

Not everything changed: Wellington's Village Council isn't on the November ballot. Wellington's municipal elections are held in March, with the next one in March 2027.

How to find your officials

  • Check your districts: your voter information card, or the Palm Beach County Supervisor of Elections' voter lookup, lists your congressional, state House, state Senate and county commission districts.
  • Florida House and Senate: both chambers have "find your representative" tools on their websites.
  • County Commission: the Palm Beach County website lists commissioners and district maps.
  • Village of Wellington: Village Council meetings are held at Village Hall, 12300 Forest Hill Blvd., and public comment is open to residents.

Key dates

  • Nov. 16, 2026: Palm Beach County posts official results.
  • Nov. 17, 2026: Waldron was sworn in on the County Commission at its reorganizational meeting, along with Erica Whitfield (District 2). The board chose Marci Woodward as county mayor and Joel Flores as vice mayor. New state legislators were sworn in at the Legislature's organizational session.
  • March 2027: Wellington and other municipal elections.
