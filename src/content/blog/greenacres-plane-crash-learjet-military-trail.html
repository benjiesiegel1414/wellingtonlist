On Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, a Learjet 60 business jet crashed near Military Trail and 10th Avenue North in the Greenacres area of Palm Beach County, east of Wellington. All nine people on board were killed. Here are the key facts.

Key facts

Date
Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, shortly before 1 p.m.
Location
Near Military Trail and 10th Avenue North in the Greenacres area of Palm Beach County, Florida
Aircraft
A Learjet 60 business jet
Flight
From Austin-Bergstrom International Airport in Texas to North Eleuthera in the Bahamas
On board
Nine people: two crew members and seven passengers
Fatalities
All nine people on board were killed
Investigation
The National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB), with the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)

What happened

The jet was flying from Austin, Texas, to the Bahamas when it went down in a residential area along Military Trail, according to the FAA and the Associated Press. The crash caused a large fire and left a crater in the roadway. All nine people on board were killed.

At a news conference the next day, the NTSB said the crew had reported ice and turbulence to air traffic controllers shortly before the plane rapidly descended, CBS12 and WPTV reported.

Right after the crash

  • Military Trail near the crash site was closed to traffic.
  • About 600 residents of a nearby mobile home community were evacuated.

The investigation

The NTSB is the federal agency that investigates civil aviation crashes and determines their probable cause, with help from the FAA. For official updates on the investigation, road closures and the neighborhood, check the NTSB and Palm Beach County.
