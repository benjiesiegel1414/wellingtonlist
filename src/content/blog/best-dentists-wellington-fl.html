How to choose a dentist in Wellington

  • Check insurance first. Confirm the office is in network for your plan before booking.
  • Ask about new patient specials. Many Wellington practices offer discounted first exams and cleanings.
  • Ask how emergencies are handled. Find out whether they hold same-day slots and what happens after hours.
  • Pick a convenient location. Most dentists cluster along Forest Hill Blvd and State Road 7.
  • Read recent reviews, paying attention to comments about wait times and billing.
