How to choose a dentist in Wellington

  • Check insurance first. Call and confirm the office is in network for your plan before booking.
  • Ask about new patient specials. Many Wellington practices offer discounted first exams and cleanings.
  • Ask about emergencies. Find out whether the office sees same-day emergencies and what to do after hours.
  • Think about location. Most dentists cluster along Forest Hill Blvd and State Road 7, so pick one near home, work or school.
  • Read recent reviews and pay attention to comments about wait times and billing.

Dental emergencies

For a knocked-out tooth, severe pain or swelling, call your dentist right away; many keep after-hours lines. For facial swelling with fever or trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the ER at Wellington Regional Medical Center. See our guide to hospitals and urgent care in Wellington.
