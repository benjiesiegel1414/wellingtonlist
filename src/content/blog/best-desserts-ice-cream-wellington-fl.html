1. Whit's Frozen Custard

Whit's Frozen Custard in the Courtyard Shops at Wellington Trace is a franchise of the Ohio-based custard chain started in 2003. Husband-and-wife owners Chris and Natalie Mass opened the Wellington shop in 2019. Vanilla and chocolate are always on, alongside a flavor of the day.

What to try: A sundae or a blended Whitser, the shop's top sellers.

Tip: There are vegan coconut milk options, and you can order custom custard cakes and hand-packed quarts.

  • Address: 13860 Wellington Trace, Suite 43, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 855-2500
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 1-9 p.m.; Fri-Sat 1-10 p.m.; Sun 1-9 p.m.
  • See the full Whit's Frozen Custard listing

2. Kobosko Kreamery

Kobosko Kreamery is an independent ice cream shop on Forest Hill Blvd just east of Wellington, in the 33411 area. It has a wide range of flavors, gluten-free options and shaded outdoor seating.

What to try: A scoop of whatever new flavor is in the case.

Tip: It stays open until 10 p.m. most nights and 10:30 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays.

  • Address: 9102 Forest Hill Blvd, West Palm Beach, FL 33411
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 10 a.m.-10 p.m.; Fri-Sat 10 a.m.-10:30 p.m.; Sun 10:30 a.m.-10 p.m.
  • See the full Kobosko Kreamery listing

3. Cold Stone Creamery

Cold Stone Creamery near The Mall at Wellington Green mixes ice cream to order with candy, fruit, nuts or baked goods on a frozen granite stone, served in a cup or waffle cone.

What to try: A signature creation in a waffle cone.

Tip: Order a custom ice cream cake ahead for pickup. It's open until 11 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays.

  • Address: 10240 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 110, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 798-8737
  • Hours: Sun-Thu 9:30 a.m.-10 p.m.; Fri-Sat 9:30 a.m.-11 p.m.
  • See the full Cold Stone Creamery listing

4. Anna Bakes

Anna Bakes on Forest Hill Blvd runs together with Cumbres Coffee Roasters. The pastry case has croissants, scones, cookies, cupcakes and coffee cake, with several vegan and gluten-free options.

What to try: A croissant or cupcake with an espresso drink.

Tip: It closes mid-afternoon, so go in the morning.

  • Address: 10120 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 160, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 288-2457
  • Hours: Mon-Fri 7 a.m.-3:45 p.m.; Sat-Sun 8 a.m.-2:45 p.m.
  • See the full Anna Bakes listing

5. Christy's Bakery

Christy's Bakery on Fairlane Farms Road is a small bakery making homemade cakes, cheesecakes, fruit pies such as apple and cherry, cookies and pastries.

What to try: A slice of cheesecake or a whole fruit pie for the holidays.

Tip: It takes orders for custom and decorated cakes for birthdays and other occasions.

  • Address: 3220 Fairlane Farms Rd, Suite 8, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 422-0028
  • Hours: Mon-Fri 9 a.m.-5:30 p.m.; Sat 9 a.m.-3 p.m.
  • See the full Christy's Bakery listing

6. Wonder Sweets Bakery

Wonder Sweets Bakery is a custom cake shop on the second level of The Mall at Wellington Green, across from CMX Cinemas. Owner Careli Bello, a former architect from Venezuela who learned to bake from her grandmother, opened the mall shop in November 2022.

What to try: Tres leches cake, or a custom letter cake for a party.

Tip: It designs cakes for birthdays, weddings and Sweet 16s, so order ahead.

  • Address: 10300 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 855-6947
  • See the full Wonder Sweets Bakery listing

7. Keywa's

Keywa's is a creperie in the food court at The Mall at Wellington Green. The menu focuses on sweet crepes, plus waffles, milkshakes, smoothies and boba tea.

What to try: A sweet crepe and a boba tea.

Tip: It's a good stop before or after a movie at the mall.

  • Address: 10300 Forest Hill Blvd, K106, Wellington, FL 33414
  • See the full Keywa's listing

8. Delicias Cuban Bakery and Ice Cream Shop

Delicias Cuban Bakery and Ice Cream Shop is a small counter inside a gas station at Lake Worth Road and State Road 7, at the southern edge of Wellington. It makes pastelitos, flan and other Cuban pastries, plus ice cream and Cuban coffee.

What to try: Pastelitos with a cafe con leche, or flan.

Tip: Order larger quantities in advance.

  • Address: 9890 Lake Worth Rd, Lake Worth, FL 33467
  • See the full Delicias Cuban Bakery and Ice Cream Shop listing

9. Hausmash

For a dessert-style milkshake, Hausmash in Wellington Green Square hand-spins six milkshakes, including a Nutella shake, to go with its smash burgers.

What to try: The Nutella milkshake.

Tip: It's open until 11 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays.

  • Address: 10660 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 170, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 284-6400
  • Hours: Sun-Thu 11:30 a.m.-10 p.m.; Fri-Sat 11:30 a.m.-11 p.m.
  • See the full Hausmash listing

Tips for dessert in Wellington

  • Ordering a cake? Whit's, Cold Stone, Christy's and Wonder Sweets all make custom cakes. Order several days ahead for birthdays and holidays.
  • Dietary needs: Whit's has vegan coconut milk custard, Kobosko has gluten-free options, and Anna Bakes has vegan and gluten-free pastries.
  • Late-night sweets: Cold Stone and Kobosko stay open past 10 p.m. on weekends.
  • Holiday treats: many bakeries take holiday pie and cake orders in November and December, so call early. For more, see our breakfast and brunch guide, the best coffee shops and all Wellington restaurants.
