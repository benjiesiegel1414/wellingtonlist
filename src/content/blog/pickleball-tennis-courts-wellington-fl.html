Looking for pickleball or tennis courts in Wellington, Florida? Wellington has two free public open-play spots for pickleball, a village-run tennis center with 27 courts, and lighted courts at the Wellington High School sports complex. Here's where to play, how many courts each place has, the hours, rules and costs, plus free beginner pickleball clinics and courts just outside the village.

1. Greenbriar Park: Wellington's pickleball hub

Greenbriar Park at 2975 Greenbriar Blvd. has six dedicated outdoor pickleball courts open for free drop-in play, first come, first served, from sunrise to sunset daily. Bring your own paddle and balls. The park also has restrooms, a pavilion, a playground, sand volleyball and the Wellington Dog Park.

Court rules posted by the village:

  • Use the paddle racks to set the rotation.
  • Games are played to 11, win by 2.
  • There's a 30-minute limit when players are waiting, and you should leave the court after your game if others are waiting.
  • No unauthorized tournaments, league play or paid lessons.
  • No bikes, skateboards, alcohol, smoking, food or drink on the courts, and no dogs except service animals.

Tip: the courts have no lights, so after-work players in winter should head to Greenview Shores instead.

Free Learn to Play Pickleball clinics

The village runs free beginner clinics at the Greenbriar Park courts covering rules, basic strategy and serving. No equipment is needed, but registration is required through the village's CivicRec catalog, and space is limited. Clinics are open to ages 10 and up.

  • Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, 9 a.m. to noon
  • Saturday, Jan. 16, 2027, 9 a.m. to noon
  • Saturday, Feb. 13, 2027, 9 a.m. to noon
  • Saturday, March 13, 2027, 9 a.m. to noon
  • Saturday, April 10, 2027, 9 a.m. to noon

For players who want more, the village also offers Pickleball Fundamentals with Love Serving Autism, a monthly class on Sundays from 9 to 10:30 a.m. at Greenbriar Park. It's open to all levels and abilities, including players with a disability or who need accommodations.

2. Greenview Shores Park: lighted courts for evening play

Greenview Shores Park at 2163 Greenview Shores Blvd. is the sports complex at Wellington Community High School, completed in June 2021. It has 8 lighted tennis courts, and 4 of them have lines for recreational pickleball. Play is free, first come, first served.

  • School year hours: Monday through Friday 6 to 10 p.m.; Saturday and Sunday 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.
  • Summer hours: Monday through Friday 8 a.m. to 10 p.m.; Saturday and Sunday 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.
  • Rules: limit play to 1 hour when others are waiting; no leagues, tournaments or paid lessons; no dogs except service animals.
  • Parking: enter at the high school's main entrance off Greenview Shores Blvd. at the traffic signal, turn left at the stop sign, then right into the back lot by the stadium.

The complex also has 3 outdoor basketball courts and a track.

3. Wellington Tennis Center

The village-run Wellington Tennis Center at 3100 Lyons Rd. opened June 9, 2015, and now has 24 self-irrigated Har-Tru clay courts and 3 hard courts, a pro shop and a canvas-covered rooftop balcony. Six of those courts (three clay and three hard) were added in 2026 with help from a $200,000 Florida Recreation Development Assistance Program grant, USTA Florida reported. Eight tennis pros on staff offer private and group lessons.

  • Hours: Monday through Thursday 7:30 a.m. to 10 p.m.; Friday through Sunday 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Thanksgiving: 7:30 a.m. to 2 p.m. Wednesday, Nov. 25; closed Nov. 26 and 27.
  • Court fees: call the pro shop at (561) 791-4775 or email tennis@wellingtonfl.gov

Kids and family programs (current village prices):

  • Junior Aces (ages 5-18): Saturday drop-in drills and games, 10 to 11:30 a.m., $30 per clinic. No advance registration.
  • Family Time Clinic (ages 3-4): kids learn alongside a parent, $30 per clinic. Mondays 11 a.m., Wednesdays and Saturdays 8 a.m. Pre-register 24 hours ahead.
  • Youth clinics (ages 5-18): after school Monday through Thursday, grouped by red, orange, green and yellow ball levels. The Nov. 30 to Dec. 17 session is $220, or $40 drop-in.
  • Holiday camps: 9 a.m. to noon, $150 per session or $80 a day. Thanksgiving camp runs Nov. 23-25; winter break camp runs Dec. 21-23 and Dec. 28-30.

4. Olympia Park

Olympia Park at 9830 Stribling Way has tennis courts available for open play, along with outdoor basketball courts, a track, ballfields, batting cages and restrooms. The park is open 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. on weekdays and 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekends. It's a good option for residents on the west side of Wellington.

5. Diadem Pickleball Complex (coming soon)

Diadem is building an air-conditioned indoor complex with 11 pickleball courts, pickleball simulators, a golf simulator and the Cat 5 restaurant and bar at 10570 W Forest Hill Blvd., near The Mall at Wellington Green. An opening date and court prices haven't been officially announced. Details are in our Diadem Wellington guide.

Courts just outside Wellington

  • Okeeheelee Park, 7715 Forest Hill Blvd.: 6 pickleball courts and 6 lighted tennis courts, with lighted courts open until 10 p.m., according to Palm Beach County Parks.
  • Crestwood Park North, Royal Palm Beach, 900 Crestwood Blvd. N: 2 pickleball and 2 tennis courts, opened in 2024, the Town-Crier reported.
  • Royal Palm Beach Recreation Center and Preservation Park: outdoor lighted and indoor pickleball courts, with a day or annual fee, according to a 2024 Palm Beach Daily News roundup. Check with the Village of Royal Palm Beach for current times.
  • John Prince Park, Lake Worth Beach: 6 lighted outdoor pickleball courts, free, according to the same 2024 roundup.

Tips for playing in Wellington

  • Beat the heat: outdoor courts are busiest on winter mornings. In summer, play early or after dark at Greenview Shores.
  • Check for rain closures: wet courts close; the village posts status updates online.
  • Get your gear: paddles and balls aren't provided at open play.
