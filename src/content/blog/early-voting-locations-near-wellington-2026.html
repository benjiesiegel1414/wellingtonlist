Early voting for the Nov. 3, 2026 general election runs through Sunday, Nov. 1 in Palm Beach County, from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. every day. Any registered Palm Beach County voter can use any of the county's 28 early voting sites, and several are a short drive from Wellington. Here's where to go, what to bring, and how vote-by-mail and Election Day work, using information from the Palm Beach County Supervisor of Elections.

Early voting at a glance

Dates
Monday, Oct. 19 through Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026
Hours
7 a.m. to 7 p.m. each day
Where
Any of 28 early voting sites in Palm Beach County
Appointments
Optional 15-minute time slots; walk-ins are always welcome
Election Day
Tuesday, Nov. 3, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., at your assigned polling place only

You don't have to use the site nearest your home. The Supervisor of Elections' early voting locations map lists all 28 sites and shows estimated wait times.

What to bring

Bring one or two forms of valid, current ID that together show your photo and signature. If your photo ID doesn't include your signature, you'll be asked for another ID that does. If you forget your ID, you can still vote, but you'll cast a provisional ballot that the Canvassing Board reviews later.

Skip the line with an appointment

The Supervisor of Elections offers optional early voting appointments in 15-minute time slots at any site during early voting hours. Appointments aren't required, and walk-in voters are always welcome.

If you have a vote-by-mail ballot

  • Return deadline: mail ballots must be received by the Supervisor of Elections Main Office by 7 p.m. on Election Day to be counted.
  • Drop it off: you can drop your completed mail ballot at any early voting site during early voting hours.
  • Track it: the Supervisor of Elections offers online ballot tracking so you can confirm it arrived.
  • Changed your mind? If you vote in person instead, your mail ballot is cancelled and you'll receive a regular ballot.

Voting on Election Day, Nov. 3

On Election Day, polls are open from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., but you must vote at the polling place assigned to your precinct. You can find it on your Voter Information Card or sample ballot, with the Supervisor of Elections' online Precinct Finder, or by calling (561) 656-6200.

Know what's on your ballot

Ballots in the Wellington area include several local questions. For a neutral rundown, see our explainer on Palm Beach County's ballot questions on schools, parks and libraries. Your personal sample ballot is available through the Supervisor of Elections website.

Questions?

The Palm Beach County Supervisor of Elections Main Office is at 4301 Cherry Road in West Palm Beach and can be reached at (561) 656-6200. Hours and details can change, so check votepalmbeach.gov before you go.
