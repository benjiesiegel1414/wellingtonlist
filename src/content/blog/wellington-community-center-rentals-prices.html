Planning a wedding, shower, birthday or meeting in Wellington? The village rents out rooms at the Wellington Community Center on Lake Wellington, from a 15-person conference room at $50 an hour to a 4,350-square-foot ballroom for 200 guests at $200 an hour for residents on weekends. It also rents a lakefront pavilion, park pavilions and party space at the Aquatics Center. We pulled every current room, capacity, hourly rate and deposit into one place, along with how to book.

Grande Ballroom

The Grande Ballroom is the Community Center's largest space, built for weddings, receptions, banquets and large celebrations. It seats 200 guests at banquet tables or 300 in theater seating. A rental includes a stage, kitchen access (ice machine, warming ovens, refrigerator, freezer, sink and prep counter), tables and chairs, a customizable setup and exclusive use of the second-floor waterfront balcony, with sunset views over Lake Wellington. The balcony has no cover, so plan a backup for rain. Add-ons include banquet rounds, a bar, a podium, buffet tables and high tops.

Example: a five-hour Saturday reception (including setup and cleanup) at the resident rate would be $1,000 in room fees plus tax, with a $1,000 refundable security deposit.

Outdoor Promenade

The 2,500-square-foot open-air Promenade behind the Community Center faces Lake Wellington and is popular for outdoor ceremonies and receptions. It has no cover for weather. Wedding ceremony chairs can be added for $3 per chair, including a white chair cover. Rates and the deposit match the Grande Ballroom.

Lakeview Room

The 2,900-square-foot Lakeview Room on the lower level holds up to 120 guests and can be split into two smaller rooms, Lakeview East and Lakeview West. It's a good fit for showers, celebrations, receptions and meetings. A rental includes access to the first-floor covered patio, kitchen access, tables and chairs, and add-ons like high tops, bars, a podium and banquet rounds.

Small rooms for parties, classes and meetings

  • Panther Room (927 sq. ft., 40 guests): suited to small showers, private parties and meetings.
  • Sailfish Meeting Room (715 sq. ft., 30 guests): a carpeted room for meetings, seminars and small gatherings.
  • Everglades Arts and Crafts Room (686 sq. ft., 20 guests): has an in-room sink and counter space, which makes it handy for kids' craft parties and workshops.
  • Wi-Fi Conference Room (517 sq. ft., 15 guests): conference-style seating with Wi-Fi and a large TV display, good for trainings and presentations.

The small rooms have one rate for all days, and none include kitchen access.

Community Center Pavilion

For an outdoor party by the lake, the Community Center Pavilion on the Town Center Promenade holds up to 50 people.

  • Rates: $100 for a half day (8 a.m. to noon or 1 to 5 p.m.) or $200 for a full day (8 a.m. to 5 p.m.).
  • Includes: eight picnic tables, three trash cans and access to restrooms inside the Community Center. The pavilion is ADA accessible.
  • Good to know: alcohol is permitted, and there's a designated bounce house area nearby, subject to size and staff approval. There's no electricity at the pavilion.
  • Booking: up to 90 days in advance by calling (561) 753-5262.

It's steps from Scott's Place playground and the Wellington Amphitheater.

How to book a Community Center room

  1. Check dates: fill out the village's online Room Rental Information Request form, and staff will follow up about availability.
  2. Prove residency: residency is confirmed at booking, and you'll need proof of Wellington residency for the resident rate.
  3. Count your hours: book enough consecutive hours to cover setup, the event and cleanup.
  4. Pay the deposit: security deposits run $100 to $1,000 and are refundable if you stay within your time and there's no damage.

Nonprofits: nonprofit groups pay the Wellington resident rate. All other organizations pay the non-resident rate.

Community Center hours

The Community Center is open Monday through Thursday 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m. to 1 p.m., and is closed Sundays. For Thanksgiving it's open 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. on Nov. 25 and closed Nov. 26-27. Besides rentals, it hosts classes and programs in the arts, fitness, health and wellness for kids, adults and seniors.

How it compares

For many Wellington events, the Community Center is a budget-friendly, lakefront alternative to a private venue: all rates include tables, chairs and setup, and the village notes that "the sunsets are free." The trade-offs: you must book every hour of setup and cleanup, the outdoor spaces have no weather cover, and you'll need to arrange your own catering.
