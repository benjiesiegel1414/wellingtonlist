Looking for dog-friendly places in Wellington, Florida? The village runs a free off-leash dog park at Greenbriar Park with separate areas for small, medium and large dogs, and Palm Beach County runs two more dog parks a short drive east. Here's how they compare, including hours, maintenance closures, wash stations and rules, plus where dogs aren't allowed, dog-friendly patios and local pet services.

1. Wellington Dog Park at Greenbriar Park

The Wellington Dog Park is a large fenced, off-leash area separated into sections for small, medium and large dogs, inside Greenbriar Park on the west side of the village. It has bathrooms, pavilions for shade, walking paths, dog wash stations, waste stations throughout and water fountains for dogs and owners. The rest of Greenbriar Park has a playground, 4 sand volleyball courts and six pickleball courts, so it works for the whole family.

  • Hours: dawn to dusk daily; closed Thursdays until 3 p.m. for maintenance.
  • Cost: free.

Key rules posted by the village:

  • Dogs must be licensed and vaccinated and wear a visible license.
  • No dogs under 4 months old and no female dogs in heat. Aggressive dogs must be removed immediately.
  • Handlers must be 16 or older and carry a leash; children under 16 must be with an adult and closely supervised.
  • Maximum of three dogs per handler. Dogs can only be off-leash inside the fenced area.
  • No personal items like toys or pools, and no food, alcohol or glass containers.
  • Pick up after your dog and fill any holes your dog digs.

Tip: the village warns that dog parks can have fleas, ticks and other pests, so keep your dog's flea and tick prevention up to date.

2. Pooch Pines at Okeeheelee Park

Palm Beach County's Pooch Pines is inside Okeeheelee Park on Forest Hill Boulevard, just east of Wellington. It has two large-dog sections that rotate for rest and maintenance, a small-dog section, double-gated entry, a paved dog wash station, water fountains with pet fountains, restrooms, shaded seating and benches.

  • Hours: sunrise to sunset; closed Wednesdays from noon to 3 p.m. for maintenance.
  • Make a day of it: Okeeheelee also has 8 miles of walking paths and a nature center. See our trails guide.

3. Lake Woof at John Prince Park

Lake Woof at John Prince Memorial Park, 2520 Lake Worth Rd. in Lake Worth, is the county's newest and largest dog park, with separate small, medium and large sections plus two more that rotate, five gazebos with benches, a two-dog wash station, pet water fountains and restrooms. Hours are sunrise to sunset, and it closes Thursdays from noon to 3 p.m. for maintenance.

County dog park rules

At Pooch Pines and Lake Woof, Palm Beach County limits each person to 2 dogs, requires dogs to be leashed on the way in and out, and bans food of any kind inside the dog park and smoking in the fenced areas. Large dogs aren't allowed in the small-dog area, puppies under 4 months and female dogs in heat are prohibited, and children 10 and younger must be closely supervised. Report incidents to the county at (561) 252-1714.

Where dogs aren't allowed in Wellington

  • Peaceful Waters Sanctuary: no dogs except service animals.
  • Village pickleball and tennis courts at Greenbriar Park and Greenview Shores Park: no dogs except service animals.
  • Wellington Aquatics Center: no animals in the pool or on the pool deck.
  • The off-leash rule: at Greenbriar, dogs can be off-leash only inside the fenced dog park.

Rules for other parks and preserves can differ, so check the posted signs at the entrance.

Dog-friendly patios

  • Juan's Marketplace, 11198 Polo Club Rd., is an open-air cafe, bar and market with covered outdoor seating where dogs are welcome. Open daily 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Lazy Dog Restaurant and Bar has been announced for The Square at Lotis Wellington on State Road 7. The chain's patios welcome leashed dogs and have a dog menu with $7 grilled chicken or burger bowls and free water, but dogs must stay on the ground, not in laps or on chairs. Until the Wellington location opens, the closest one is in Boca Raton.

Patio policies are up to each restaurant and the health department, and dogs are generally not allowed inside restaurants except service animals. Call ahead before bringing your dog anywhere new.

Pet services in Wellington

  • Vets: see every veterinarian in Wellington, plus Petfolk Veterinary and Urgent Care in Royal Palm Beach, open daily 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., for non-life-threatening problems.
  • Grooming: Scenthound in the Courtyard Shops on Wellington Trace focuses on baths, nail trims, ear cleaning and teeth brushing. Petco on State Road 7 has a grooming salon, an in-store vet hospital and training classes.
  • Daycare and boarding: K9 Resorts Luxury Pet Hotel on Fairlane Farms Road offers cage-free suites, daily playgroups and small-dog daycare.
  • Training: Dog Training Elite serves Wellington with obedience, puppy and behavior programs.

Tips for dog owners in Wellington

  • Watch the heat. Pavement and artificial surfaces get hot. Walk early or after sunset and bring water.
  • Stay away from the water's edge. Alligators live in Wellington's canals and lakes, and small dogs are at real risk.
  • Plan around closures: Wellington Dog Park on Thursday mornings, Pooch Pines on Wednesday midday.
  • Keep tags current. Both the village and county parks require visible licenses and up-to-date vaccines.
