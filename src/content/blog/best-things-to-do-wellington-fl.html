Wondering what to do in Wellington, Florida? Wellington is known worldwide as the winter equestrian capital, but there's a lot more here: free boardwalks through the wetlands, an observation tower over the Everglades, free concerts at the amphitheater, a brand-new aquatics center and a comedy club at the mall. Here are 15 of the best things to do in and around Wellington, with addresses, hours and tips.

We picked these from the attractions, parks and venues on Wellington List, mixing Wellington's famous equestrian events with everyday outings for families and visitors. Every spot below links to its full Wellington List listing. No business paid to be included, and the list is in no particular order of quality. Hours were last checked in November 2026 and can change, especially around holidays, so call ahead.

1. Wellington International

Wellington International on Equestrian Club Drive is home to the Winter Equestrian Festival, a hunter and jumper circuit that began in 1977. Riders and horses come from all 50 states and more than 55 countries. The 2027 festival starts Jan. 6.

What to try: Saturday Night Lights during the festival. Outside food, drinks and coolers aren't allowed on the grounds.

Tip: See our Winter Equestrian Festival 2027 guide for dates and tickets.

  • Address: 3400 Equestrian Club Dr, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 793-5867
  • See the full Wellington International listing

2. National Polo Center

The National Polo Center, owned by the United States Polo Association, has six championship fields. Each winter it hosts the Gauntlet of Polo from February to April, ending with the U.S. Open Polo Championship.

What to try: A ticketed Sunday match on Field One (some tickets include brunch at the Pavilion). Many other games during the season are free to watch.

Tip: See our 2027 polo season guide.

  • Address: 3667 120th Ave S, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 204-5687
  • See the full National Polo Center listing

3. Equestrian Village (Global Dressage Festival)

Equestrian Village on South Shore Blvd hosts the Adequan Global Dressage Festival each winter, where you can watch top dressage horses and riders up close.

What to try: Friday Night Stars, when top international classes compete under the lights. The arena stands are covered.

Tip: See our Global Dressage Festival guide.

  • Address: 13500 South Shore Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 793-5867
  • See the full Equestrian Village (Global Dressage Festival) listing

4. Peaceful Waters Sanctuary

Peaceful Waters Sanctuary is a free, 30-acre wetland at the back of Village Park with about 1,500 feet of raised boardwalk and a one-mile walking loop. Birders come for roseate spoonbills, white pelicans, herons and egrets.

What to try: A sunrise or late-afternoon walk with binoculars.

Tip: Winter is the busiest season for birds.

  • Address: 11676 Pierson Rd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 791-4000
  • Hours: Daily, dawn to dusk; closed holidays
  • See the full Peaceful Waters Sanctuary listing

5. Wellington Environmental Preserve

The Wellington Environmental Preserve on the western edge of the village is a free nature park with a paved loop of nearly 3 miles, wetland boardwalks and a six-story observation tower over the Everglades landscape.

What to try: Climb the tower, then walk or bike the paved loop.

Tip: Shade is limited, so bring water and sun protection. Dogs aren't allowed on the trails.

  • Address: 3491 Flying Cow Ranch Rd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Hours: Daily 8 a.m.-8 p.m.
  • See the full Wellington Environmental Preserve listing

6. Wellington Amphitheater

The village-owned Wellington Amphitheater at Town Center holds up to about 1,500 people and hosts free concerts, food truck nights, movie nights and holiday events.

What to try: A Thursday night concert with a picnic blanket.

Tip: See our amphitheater events guide and holiday events.

  • Address: 12100 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 753-2484
  • See the full Wellington Amphitheater listing

7. Wellington Town Center and Lake Wellington

Town Center on Lake Wellington brings together Village Hall, the Community Center, the amphitheater, a 9/11 memorial with a piece of the World Trade Center, and Scott's Place, a barrier-free playground.

What to try: Walk the promenade along Lake Wellington and visit the 9/11 memorial.

  • Address: 12150 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 753-2484
  • Hours: Community Center: Mon-Thu 7 a.m.-9 p.m.; Fri closed; Sat 9 a.m.-6 p.m.; Sun 10 a.m.-5 p.m.
  • See the full Wellington Town Center and Lake Wellington listing

8. Wellington Aquatics Center

The Wellington Aquatics Center at Village Park opened in January 2026, with a heated Olympic competition pool, diving boards, a zero-depth recreation pool and Florida's first NinjaCross obstacle course over the water.

What to try: The NinjaCross course and the recreation pool with the kids.

Tip: See our aquatics center guide for prices.

  • Address: 3410 120th Ave S, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 791-4770
  • Hours: Labor Day to Memorial Day: Mon-Sat 9 a.m.-7 p.m., Sun noon-7 p.m.; Memorial Day to Labor Day: Mon-Sat 8 a.m.-8 p.m., Sun noon-8 p.m.
  • See the full Wellington Aquatics Center listing

9. Village Park

Village Park on Pierson Road is Wellington's largest park, about 114 acres, with sports fields, gyms, pickleball courts, a skate park, a playground and a walking trail of about 2.5 miles with exercise stations.

What to try: The walking trail, then a stop at Peaceful Waters next door.

  • Address: 11700 Pierson Rd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 791-4005
  • See the full Village Park listing

10. Palm Beach Improv

Palm Beach Improv moved to The Mall at Wellington Green in September 2026 after about two decades in West Palm Beach. The club seats about 450 and books touring stand-up comedians most weekends.

What to try: A weekend headliner show.

Tip: Each guest must order at least two drinks (nonalcoholic counts), mall parking is free, and tickets should be bought on the club's own website.

  • Address: 10300 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 833-1812
  • See the full Palm Beach Improv listing

11. The Mall at Wellington Green

The Mall at Wellington Green on Forest Hill Blvd opened in 2001 and is the largest retail center in Wellington, with more than 170 stores, a CMX Cinemas movie theater, restaurants, the Improv and Kids Empire.

What to try: A movie at CMX Cinemas and dinner nearby.

Tip: See our mall directory and hours.

  • Address: 10300 W Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 227-6900
  • Hours: Mon to Sat 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.; Sun 11 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • See the full The Mall at Wellington Green listing

12. Kids Empire Wellington

Kids Empire inside The Mall at Wellington Green is an indoor playground for ages 1 to 12, with a multi-level maze, slides, ball pits and a separate toddler zone.

What to try: A rainy-day or hot-afternoon play session.

Tip: Socks and a signed waiver are required, and admission is good all day with re-entry.

  • Address: 10300 W Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 268, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 621-9677
  • See the full Kids Empire Wellington listing

13. Lion Country Safari

Lion Country Safari in Loxahatchee, a short drive north of Wellington, opened in 1967 and bills itself as the first cageless zoo in the U.S. You drive your own car along a 4-mile preserve past free-roaming animals.

What to try: The drive-through safari, then the walk-through area.

  • Address: 2003 Lion Country Safari Rd, Loxahatchee, FL 33470
  • Phone: (561) 793-1084
  • See the full Lion Country Safari listing

14. South Florida Fairgrounds and Yesteryear Village

The South Florida Fairgrounds on Southern Blvd, just north of Wellington, hosts the South Florida Fair for about 17 days each January, plus shows and events year round. It's also home to Yesteryear Village, a 10-acre living history park with buildings from about 1895 to 1945 and costumed interpreters.

What to try: The fair in January, or Yesteryear Village Thursday through Saturday.

  • Address: 9067 Southern Blvd, West Palm Beach, FL 33411
  • Phone: (561) 795-3110
  • Hours: Yesteryear Village: Thu-Sat 10 a.m.-4 p.m.; Sun-Wed closed (varies during events)
  • See the full South Florida Fairgrounds and Yesteryear Village listing

15. Okeeheelee Park

Okeeheelee Park, just east of Wellington on Forest Hill Blvd, is a free Palm Beach County park of more than 1,700 acres with two lakes, mountain bike trails, a BMX track, a golf course, a dog park and the Okeeheelee Nature Center.

What to try: The Nature Center trails and live animal exhibits, or the mountain bike trails.

  • Address: 7715 Forest Hill Blvd, West Palm Beach, FL 33413
  • Phone: (561) 966-6600
  • Hours: Daily sunrise to sunset
  • See the full Okeeheelee Park listing

Tips for visiting Wellington

  • Come in winter for the horses. Wellington's biggest events, including the Winter Equestrian Festival, dressage and polo, run from about January through April.
  • Lots is free. Peaceful Waters, the Environmental Preserve, Town Center and most amphitheater events cost nothing.
  • Plan for heat. In summer, do outdoor trails early in the morning and save afternoons for the pool or indoor spots.
