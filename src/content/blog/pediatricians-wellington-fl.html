Pediatric specialists in Wellington

The Nicklaus Children's Wellington Outpatient Center on Forest Hill Boulevard brings pediatric specialty care closer to home, so many families do not need to drive to Miami for specialist visits. Your pediatrician can refer you.

After-hours and urgent care for kids

When your pediatrician's office is closed, call their after-hours line first; many practices have a nurse or on-call doctor. For minor illnesses and injuries, Wellington's urgent care centers see children, including Baptist Health Urgent Care Express and Premier Family Health Urgent Care. For emergencies, call 911 or go to the emergency room at Wellington Regional Medical Center. See our full guide to hospitals and urgent care in Wellington.

How to choose a pediatrician in Wellington

  • Check insurance first. Call the office to confirm they take your plan and are accepting new patients.
  • Ask about sick visits. Find out whether they hold same-day appointments and how the after-hours line works.
  • Consider location. A practice near your child's school or on your commute makes frequent visits easier.
  • Plan for physicals. Back-to-school and sports physicals book up in July and August, so schedule early.
  • Meet before baby arrives. Many practices offer prenatal meet-and-greet visits for expecting parents.
