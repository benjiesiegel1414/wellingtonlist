Palm Beach County students are off for Thanksgiving break Nov. 23-27 and winter break Dec. 21, 2026 through Jan. 1, 2027, and Wellington's Parks and Recreation Department has a lineup of short camps to fill those days, from tennis and soccer to volleyball, lacrosse and gymnastics for preschoolers. We pulled every Thanksgiving and winter break camp from the village's online recreation catalog, with dates, ages, hours and prices, so you can compare them in one place and register before spots fill. Spring break camps for March 2027 are already listed too.

Tennis camps at the Wellington Tennis Center

The Wellington Tennis Center at 3100 Lyons Rd. runs morning camps for ages 5 to 18, all levels welcome, with instructional drills and games. Each three-day session is $150, and the catalog also lists flexible-priced drop-in options (the village's tennis page lists $80 a day). The village says tennis camps run regardless of weather, moving to indoor activities in the second-floor player's lounge if it rains.

Holiday Soccer Camp

The winter break soccer camp is run by Wellington Soccer Club's director of coaching and staff, according to the catalog, which describes it as an education-based skills camp. It's the longest day of the village's winter camps, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., at Village Park, for ages 6 to 18. Each three-day session is $140.

Volleyball camps

The village's volleyball camps teach skills and basics in a fun, organized setting for ages 9 to 15, mornings at Village Park. The Thanksgiving camp is held indoors in Gyms 2 and 3, and costs $125 for residents or $156 for non-residents. A winter break camp runs Dec. 21-23.

Lacrosse Winter Break Camp

This speed lacrosse camp at Greenview Shores Park is for new and experienced players ages 8 to 14. It focuses on skill-building and speed training through non-contact play, from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., for $150. Check the catalog for the exact days, since the listing shows a start date of Dec. 21.

TumbleKids Kamp for ages 3 to 5

For preschoolers, TumbleKids Kamp at Village Park mixes basic gymnastics, sports, rhythm activities and socialization, and the village pitches it as a good starter before recreation and competitive sports. It's a drop-off program. Thanksgiving and the first winter session are $125 for three mornings; the second winter session, Dec. 28-31, runs four days until 1 p.m. for $165.

How to register

  1. Set up a CivicRec account with Wellington Parks and Recreation, with proof of residency for resident rates.
  2. Find the camp under the Camps and Academies tab of the catalog at wellingtonfl.gov/CivicRec.
  3. Register early. Camps have minimum and maximum enrollment; the Thanksgiving volleyball camp caps at 50.
  4. Ask questions at the Village Park front desk, (561) 791-4005, or the Tennis Center pro shop, (561) 791-4775.
