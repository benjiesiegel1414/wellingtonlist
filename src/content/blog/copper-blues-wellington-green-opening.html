What is Copper Blues?

Copper Blues is a live music pub and restaurant with locations in Phoenix and Doral, and Wellington is its Palm Beach County home. The company says it books live music seven nights a week, with acoustic performers during happy hour and cover bands on weekends, usually with no cover charge. Some locations also host DJs, tribute bands and themed nights like country and salsa. Expect a full bar with a cocktail focus and a pub-style kitchen menu.

Hours

Copper Blues lists these hours for its Wellington location, which make it one of the latest-open spots in town:

  • Monday to Wednesday: 4 p.m. to midnight
  • Thursday: 4 p.m. to 1 a.m.
  • Friday: 4 p.m. to 3 a.m.
  • Saturday: 11 a.m. to 3 a.m.
  • Sunday: 11 a.m. to midnight

Opening-week hours can differ, so call (561) 404-4101 or check copperblueslive.com before you head over.

Comedy next door: Palm Beach Improv

Copper Blues opens alongside the Palm Beach Improv, the comedy club that spent years at CityPlace and the Kravis Center before moving into the mall. The two venues were announced together in 2025 as part of the mall's push toward dining and entertainment, and the Improv suggests grabbing a drink and some live music at Copper Blues before your show. Improv tickets are sold on palmbeachimprov.com; the box office is (561) 833-1812.
