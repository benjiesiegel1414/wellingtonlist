Private schools in Wellington

St. David's-in-the-Pines Episcopal School

An Episcopal school on Forest Hill Blvd serving preschool and the early elementary grades in a small, nurturing Christian setting.

Wellington Collegiate Academy

An independent private school in Wellington offering a college-prep focus with small classes.

The Wellington Preparatory School and Paolo Prep Academy

Smaller private schools on the Lake Worth Road side of the village, worth a visit if you want an independent school close to home.

Wellington also has many private preschools and early learning centers, including several faith-based programs attached to local churches.

Christian schools near Wellington

The King's Academy

One of the largest Christian schools in Palm Beach County, The King's Academy in West Palm Beach serves PreK through 12th grade with strong academics, arts and athletics. Many Wellington families make the drive.

Berean Christian School

Founded in 1964, Berean Christian School at 8350 Okeechobee Blvd in West Palm Beach is a PreK-12 Christian school with a biblically based curriculum and accreditation through Christian school associations. It is one of the closer Christian K-12 options for Wellington families.

Other Christian options

Atlantic Christian Academy and Palm Beach Christian Academy in West Palm Beach are smaller Christian schools that also draw students from the western communities.

Catholic schools near Wellington

Most Catholic schools serving Wellington families are in West Palm Beach, about 25 to 35 minutes away, and are part of the Diocese of Palm Beach:

  • Rosarian Academy, an independent Catholic school for students through 8th grade.
  • St. Juliana School, a Catholic PreK-8 parish school.
  • Cardinal Newman High School, the area's Catholic high school.

Local parishes in and around Wellington can tell you which Catholic schools families in your area attend and whether parish discounts apply.

Paying for private school in Florida

Florida's school choice program lets every K-12 student apply for a state-funded scholarship that can be used toward private school tuition at participating schools, including most Christian and Catholic schools. Amounts vary and lower-income families get priority. Ask each school whether it participates and how much of tuition the scholarship typically covers.

How to choose

  • Visit in person and sit in on a class if you can.
  • Ask about total cost: tuition, registration, uniforms, books, transportation and fundraising expectations.
  • Plan the commute. Most larger private schools are 25 to 40 minutes from Wellington, so ask about buses or carpools.
  • Apply early. Popular schools fill and many have waitlists, with applications often opening in the fall for the next year.
