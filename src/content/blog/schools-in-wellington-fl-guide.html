Elementary schools

Six of Wellington's seven elementary schools earned an A in 2026. Wellington Elementary runs a choice arts program in visual, performing and communication arts, and New Horizons Elementary offers an International Spanish Academy for students who want a dual-language program. Most elementary schools offer Voluntary Pre-K (VPK) for 4-year-olds, which is free in Florida.

Phone numbers: Binks Forest (561) 904-9800, Elbridge Gale (561) 422-9300, Equestrian Trails (561) 904-9600, New Horizons (561) 651-0500, Panther Run (561) 804-3900, Wellington Elementary (561) 651-0600, Discovery Key (561) 491-8200.

Middle schools

All three Wellington middle schools earned an A. Each offers technology-focused choice programs: Polo Park Middle has Pre-Engineering (Gateway to Technology) and Pre-Information Technology, Wellington Landings Middle pairs Fine Arts with Pre-Information Technology, and Emerald Cove Middle offers Pre-Information Technology.

Phone numbers: Emerald Cove (561) 803-8000, Polo Park (561) 333-5500, Wellington Landings (561) 792-8100.

High schools

Wellington Community High School

Wellington's namesake high school earned an A and offers choice academies that fit the village well, including an Equine and Pre-Veterinary academy, plus Fine Arts, Fire Science and Marketing. It also has in-house programs in drafting and communications design and information technology, and an Air Force JROTC unit. 2101 Greenview Shores Blvd, (561) 795-4900.

Palm Beach Central High School

Palm Beach Central, on the east side of Wellington, also earned an A. Its academies include Culinary Arts, Cybersecurity, Digital Design, Engineering and Technology (Project Lead the Way) and Medical Sciences (for zoned students), and it offers the Cambridge AICE program. 8499 W Forest Hill Blvd, (561) 304-1000.

How school zoning works

Your home address determines your zoned elementary, middle and high school. Boundaries don't always follow neighborhood lines, and they can change when new schools open, so check the exact address with the district's Find My School map tool, found in the district's GIS Map Catalog, before you buy or rent.

Choice and magnet programs: dates to know

Students can apply to choice programs outside their zone through the district's online application at mypbchoiceapp.com (you'll need a district student ID). For the 2026-27 school year:

  • Arts school applications were due December 5, 2025
  • All other choice programs were due January 30, 2026
  • Assignment notices went out April 2, 2026
  • A late application window ran March 30 to May 1, 2026

Expect a similar timeline for 2027-28, with applications opening in the fall. Many programs use a lottery, so applying on time matters more than applying first.

Charter schools

Somerset Academy Wellington runs a K-8 school and a high school at 1000 Wellington Trace. Charter schools are free public schools run independently from the district; they admit by application, with a lottery when demand exceeds seats, so apply early.

Florida school choice scholarships for 2026-27

Every Florida student eligible for public school can apply for a state scholarship to use at a private school, regardless of household income. Priority goes to renewing students, then lower-income families. For Palm Beach County in 2026-27, Step Up For Students lists award amounts of about $8,890 for grades K-3, $8,288 for grades 4-8 and $8,091 for grades 9-12. New applications for the 2026-27 school year are due November 15, 2026. Amounts are set by the Legislature each year, so check stepupforstudents.org for current figures.

How to compare schools

  • Start with your address. Confirm your zoned schools before anything else.
  • Look past the letter grade. Every Wellington district school but one earned an A, so programs, size and commute matter more.
  • Tour. Visits tell you more than rankings, especially for private and charter schools.
  • Mark the deadlines. Choice applications close in winter for the following fall.
  • Plan for traffic. Drop-off lines on Forest Hill Blvd, State Road 7 and Lake Worth Rd get heavy, especially in season.
