    Frequently asked questions

    Is the free listing really free?

    Yes. A basic listing with your name, address, phone, hours and a short description is free. Fill out the form on this page and, if it passes our checks, it usually goes live within the hour.

    What do I get with a Featured listing?

    Your business is pinned to the top of its category and service pages with a Featured badge, and your page gets your logo, up to 10 photos, a deals box, a booking button, social links, a longer write-up and mentions in related Wellington guides.

    How does Lifetime work?

    Lifetime is a single $49 payment at our founding price. Your listing stays Featured for as long as Wellington List runs, with no monthly bill. The founding price will go up as the site grows.

    Can I cancel the monthly plan?

    Yes, anytime. Your listing goes back to a free listing at the end of the billing period, and nothing else changes.

    What happens after I pay?

    Stripe takes you straight to a setup page where you upload your logo and photos and fill in your details. Your Featured listing goes through quick automatic checks and usually goes live within the hour.

    My business is already listed. Can I upgrade it?

    Yes. Choose Featured, and on the setup page paste the link to your current listing. We upgrade that page instead of creating a new one.

    Which businesses qualify?

    Businesses located in Wellington, or service businesses that regularly serve Wellington homes and companies, including nearby Royal Palm Beach, Loxahatchee, Lake Worth, Greenacres and the rest of western Palm Beach County. We do not list adult businesses, gambling, or anything misleading.

    Want to stand out?

    Featured businesses are pinned above every free listing in their category, with photos, a logo and a booking button.
