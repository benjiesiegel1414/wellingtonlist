Equestrian venue

Wellington International

The showgrounds at the center of Wellington's winter season and home of the Winter Equestrian Festival. For thirteen weeks each winter, show jumpers, hunters and equitation riders from across the United States and dozens of countries compete here, and spectators can watch much of the action.

Polo venue

National Polo Center

The United States Polo Association's flagship venue, set on roughly 161 acres with seven tournament fields. Sunday polo during the winter season is a Wellington tradition, and the clubhouse hosts dining and private events.

WellingtonDetails
Nature preserve

Peaceful Waters Sanctuary

A roughly 30 acre wetland preserve tucked into the back of Village Park, with about 1,500 feet of elevated boardwalk and a one mile trail. It is one of the best free birdwatching spots in Palm Beach County, with herons, wading birds and the occasional alligator.

Inside Village ParkDetails
Nature preserve

Wellington Environmental Preserve

A 365 acre preserve at the Marjory Stoneman Douglas Everglades Habitat on the western edge of the village, with paved trails and a six story observation tower overlooking the Everglades landscape. Wide open and quiet, it is great for long walks and family bike rides.

Western WellingtonDetails
Outdoor venue

Wellington Amphitheater

The Wellington Amphitheater is the village's free outdoor stage at Town Center, 12100 Forest Hill Blvd, next to Scott's Place playground and Lake Wellington. It hosts free tribute band concerts, food truck nights, movie nights and seasonal festivals for up to about 1,500 people.

Town CenterDetails
Shopping mall

The Mall at Wellington Green

Wellington's regional shopping mall on State Road 7, with department stores, national retailers, restaurants and entertainment under one roof. The surrounding Wellington Green area is one of the busiest dining corridors in the village.

State Road 7Details
Drive-through safari park

Lion Country Safari

A drive-through safari park a short drive north of Wellington, where you tour animal habitats from your own car, then park for a walk-through area with rides, animal encounters and a water play zone. A classic day out for families.

Loxahatchee (nearby)Details
New American

Kaluz

An upscale New American restaurant near Wellington Green with a full bar and outdoor seating, and one of the most talked about dinner spots in the village.

Wellington GreenDetails
Italian

Agliolio Fresh Pasta and Wine Bar

A family owned Italian spot known for fresh pasta and a lively, busy dining room. A local go-to for date nights and family dinners alike.
