Looking for the best auto repair shops and tire stores in Wellington, Florida? Here are 12 local providers for auto repair, tires, maintenance and dealer service in and around Wellington, with addresses and phone numbers where available.

Auto repair in Wellington: what to know

Most of Wellington's independent repair shops sit in the light-industrial areas off Fortune Way and Fairlane Farms Road, just south of Forest Hill Boulevard. Several specialize in European and luxury vehicles, which is useful in a town full of them. Quick-lube and chain shops are a few minutes north on State Road 7 in Royal Palm Beach.

South Florida heat is hard on batteries, tires and air conditioning, so many drivers have AC systems checked before summer. Ask for a written estimate before work begins, and ask whether the shop uses OEM or aftermarket parts.

Tips for hiring auto repair shops and tire stores in Wellington

  • Ask for a written estimate before repairs begin
  • Ask whether the shop works on your make, including classic or European cars
  • Horse trailer owners should ask about trailer repair and maintenance

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best auto repair shops and tire stores in Wellington, FL?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Wellington, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 12 options for auto repair, tires, maintenance and dealer service.

Where are auto repair shops in Wellington?

Most independent shops are in the Fortune Way and Fairlane Farms Road business areas off Forest Hill Blvd. Chain quick-lube and brake shops are nearby on State Road 7 in Royal Palm Beach.

Are there shops in Wellington that work on European cars?

Yes. Several Wellington shops, including WBA Performance and Wellington Auto Service, service European and specialty vehicles. Call ahead to confirm they work on your make.

Do these auto repair shops and tire stores serve all of Wellington?

Most serve all Wellington ZIP codes, including 33414, 33449 and 33467, plus nearby Royal Palm Beach, Loxahatchee and Lake Worth. Call to confirm they cover your address.
