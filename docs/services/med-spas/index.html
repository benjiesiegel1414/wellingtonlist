Choosing a med spa in Wellington

Wellington's med spas cluster along Forest Hill Boulevard, South Shore Boulevard and State Road 7. Common treatments include neurotoxins like Botox and Dysport, dermal fillers, laser hair removal, IPL and BBL photofacials, microneedling, HydraFacials and body contouring. Many also offer wellness services such as IV therapy and medical weight loss.

In Florida, injectables should be performed by or under the supervision of a licensed physician, physician assistant or nurse. Ask who will perform your treatment, what training they have and which devices they use, and book a consultation before committing to a package.

Tips for hiring med spas and beauty spas in Wellington

  • Ask who performs injectables and their credentials
  • Book a consultation before committing to a treatment plan
  • Ask about package pricing for laser treatments

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best med spas and beauty spas in Wellington, FL?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Wellington, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 12 options for facials, injectables, laser treatments and skin care.

What treatments do Wellington med spas offer?

Wellington med spas commonly offer Botox and Dysport, dermal fillers, laser hair removal, IPL and BBL photofacials, microneedling, Morpheus8, HydraFacials, CoolSculpting and wellness services like IV therapy.

How do I choose a safe med spa?

Ask who performs injectables and lasers and what their medical license and training are, confirm a physician oversees treatments, and start with a consultation before buying a package.

Do these med spas and beauty spas serve all of Wellington?

Most serve all Wellington ZIP codes, including 33414, 33449 and 33467, plus nearby Royal Palm Beach, Loxahatchee and Lake Worth. Call to confirm they cover your address.
