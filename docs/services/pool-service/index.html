Pool service in Wellington: what to know

Wellington's heat, summer storms and heavy rain mean pools need steady care year round. Most homeowners use a weekly service that balances chemicals, skims and brushes, empties baskets and checks equipment. Bigger jobs, such as pump and heater replacement, leak detection, acid washing and full resurfacing, usually go to a renovation contractor.

Pool resurfacing (plaster, quartz or pebble finishes) is typically needed every 10 to 15 years, or sooner if you see rough patches, stains or chipping. It's a good time to update tile and coping too. Wellington pools in HOA communities may need approval before changes to decks, screens or enclosures.

Tips for hiring pool service companies in Wellington

  • Ask what weekly service includes, such as chemicals and filter cleaning
  • Get equipment checked before summer
  • Ask about storm prep for pools during hurricane season
  • Pool resurfacing (plaster, pebble or quartz finishes) is typically needed every 10 to 15 years; get at least two quotes and ask about tile and coping work at the same time

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best pool service companies in Wellington, FL?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Wellington, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 11 options for weekly pool cleaning, equipment repair, pool resurfacing, supplies and renovations.

How often should a pool be resurfaced in Wellington?

Most plaster, quartz and pebble pool finishes last about 10 to 15 years. Rough surfaces, staining, chipping or frequent algae are signs it's time to call a renovation contractor for a resurfacing quote.

What does weekly pool service include?

Weekly service usually covers chemical testing and balancing, skimming, brushing, vacuuming, emptying baskets and a quick equipment check. Confirm whether chemicals and filter cleaning are included in the price.

Do these pool service companies serve all of Wellington?
