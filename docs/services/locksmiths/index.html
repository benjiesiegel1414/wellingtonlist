Locksmiths in Wellington: what to know

Florida has no state locksmith license, and many "Wellington locksmith" search results are out-of-area call centers. Save a vetted local number before you need it, ask for the legal business name and the full price before anyone is dispatched, and expect a real locksmith to ask for proof that you live there or own the car.

Locked out right now? See our guide to 24-hour emergency locksmiths in Wellington. If a child or pet is locked in a car, call 911 first.

Tips for hiring locksmiths in Wellington

  • Save a vetted locksmith number before you need one
  • Get the total price, including the service call and after-hours fees, before anyone is dispatched
  • Rekey your locks when you move into a new home

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best locksmiths in Wellington, FL?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Wellington, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 8 options for home and car lockouts, rekeying, lock changes, car keys and safes.

Is there a 24-hour locksmith in Wellington, FL?

Yes. Pop-A-Lock of Palm Beach, A Local Locksmith and South Shore Locksmiths advertise 24/7 service for home and car lockouts in Wellington. See our emergency locksmith guide for phone numbers.

Do locksmiths need a license in Florida?

No. Florida has no professional licensing for locksmiths, so check reviews, ask for ID and the legal business name, and get the full price in writing before work starts.

Do these locksmiths serve all of Wellington?

Most serve all Wellington ZIP codes, including 33414, 33449 and 33467, plus nearby Royal Palm Beach, Loxahatchee and Lake Worth. Call to confirm they cover your address.
