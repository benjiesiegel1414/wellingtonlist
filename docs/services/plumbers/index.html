Plumbing in Wellington: what to know

Most Wellington homes sit on concrete slabs with water lines underneath, so slab leaks, water heater failures in the garage and main line clogs are the calls local plumbers get most. For a leak, shut off the main water valve first, then call. If the problem is at the meter or in the street, call Wellington Utilities at (561) 791-4010, or (561) 791-4037 after hours.

Need help right now? See our guide to 24-hour emergency plumbers in Wellington. Before hiring, look up the plumber's license on myfloridalicense.com; state-certified plumbing contractor licenses start with CFC.

Tips for hiring plumbers in Wellington

  • Confirm the plumber is licensed in Florida and carries insurance
  • Ask whether they charge a trip fee and whether emergency or after-hours rates apply
  • For older Wellington homes, ask about repiping and water heater replacement options

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best plumbers in Wellington, FL?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Wellington, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 21 options for drain cleaning, leaks, water heaters, backflow testing and emergency repairs.

Is there a 24-hour plumber in Wellington, FL?

Yes. Good Boy Plumbing on Forest Hill Blvd offers 24/7 emergency dispatch, and Roto-Rooter Wellington, Benjamin Franklin Plumbing and Copperhead Plumbing also advertise 24/7 service. See our emergency plumbers guide for phone numbers.

Who do I call for a water main break in Wellington?

Call Wellington Utilities at (561) 791-4010 on weekdays or (561) 791-4037 after hours. Buena Vida, Olympia and Villagewalk are served by Palm Beach County Water Utilities at (561) 740-4600, option 1.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater in Wellington?

Yes. Wellington lists water heater replacement on its permit fee schedule, and a licensed plumber normally pulls the permit.

Do these plumbers serve all of Wellington?

Most serve all Wellington ZIP codes, including 33414, 33449 and 33467, plus nearby Royal Palm Beach, Loxahatchee and Lake Worth. Call to confirm they cover your address.
