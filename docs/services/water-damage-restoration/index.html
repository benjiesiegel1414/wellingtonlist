Tips for hiring water damage and mold restoration companies in Wellington

  • Stop the water at the main shutoff and take photos before cleanup starts, for your insurance claim
  • Ask whether the company is IICRC certified and whether it bills your insurer directly
  • Drying should start within 24 to 48 hours to limit mold growth
