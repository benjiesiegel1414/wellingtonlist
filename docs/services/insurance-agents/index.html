Insurance in Wellington: what to know

Florida home insurance has changed a lot in recent years, so it pays to shop around. Captive agents represent one company, such as State Farm, Allstate or Florida Farm Bureau, while independent agencies can quote several carriers at once. Many Wellington homeowners also need separate flood coverage, since standard homeowners policies don't cover flood damage.

A wind mitigation inspection can lower Florida home premiums by documenting your roof, shutters and other storm features. Equestrian property owners should ask about farm, horse mortality and equine liability coverage, which not every agent writes.

Tips for hiring insurance agents in Wellington

  • Compare quotes from more than one agent, since Florida home insurance rates vary widely
  • Ask about wind mitigation inspections, which can lower premiums
  • Review your flood and hurricane coverage before June 1

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best insurance agents in Wellington, FL?

Start with providers based in or regularly serving Wellington, check licensing and reviews, and get more than one quote for bigger jobs. This page lists 12 options for home, auto, flood, life and business insurance agents.

Do I need flood insurance in Wellington, FL?

Standard homeowners policies don't cover flood damage, so many Wellington homeowners buy separate flood insurance through the NFIP or a private carrier. Your lender may require it depending on your flood zone.

What is a wind mitigation inspection?

It's an inspection that documents storm-resistant features like roof straps, shutters and impact windows. Florida insurers offer premium discounts based on the report, so ask your agent whether one could lower your rate.
