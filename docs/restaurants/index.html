The best restaurants in Wellington, FL

Where Wellington eats, from date night favorites to breakfast spots and late night tacos.

Wellington's dining scene stretches from the Wellington Green area near State Road 7 to the plazas along Wellington Trace and the restaurants near the showgrounds on South Shore Boulevard. Below are the spots locals talk about most, grouped so you can filter by type. With Village Landing adding about 100,000 square feet of restaurant space by 2028, expect this list to grow.
