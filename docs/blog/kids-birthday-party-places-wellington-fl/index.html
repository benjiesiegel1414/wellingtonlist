Kids Birthday Party Places in Wellington, FL: 10 Venues, Prices Compared

By Ben SiegelPublished November 13, 20267 min read

Planning a kids birthday party in Wellington, Florida? Prices are all over the map: a weekend pavilion at Tiger Shark Cove costs $106 for the day, while a princess party at a play cafe can top $1,000. We pulled the current packages, prices and fine print from 10 party spots in and around Wellington, from trampolines and ice skating to bowling, a safari and the village's new aquatics center, and put them side by side so you can compare.

1. Village pavilions at Tiger Shark Cove and Village Park

The cheapest way to throw a party in Wellington is a village pavilion next to a free playground. Tiger Shark Cove has an ocean-themed playground with a pirate ship and a giant shark sculpture, and Village Park has a playground plus fields and a skate park.

  • Price (includes sales tax): small pavilions $53 for a half day or $106 for a full day; large pavilions $80 or $160.
  • Hours: a half day is 8 a.m. to noon or 1 to 5 p.m.; a full day is 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • What you get: Village Park's large Pavilion 3 has 12 picnic tables and a big grill; Tiger Shark Cove's large Pavilion 3 has 10 tables and is connected to the restrooms. No pavilion has electricity.
  • Booking: up to 60 days ahead through the village's CivicRec catalog or at the Village Park front desk; within 7 days, in person only. Rentals are only needed on weekends; on weekdays pavilions are free, first come, first served.

Watch out for: the village's rental page lists no bounce houses or inflatables for the Tiger Shark Cove pavilions. Compare every village playground in our Wellington playgrounds guide.

2. Kids Empire Wellington

Kids Empire at The Mall at Wellington Green (10300 W Forest Hill Blvd., Suite 268) is an indoor playground for ages 1 to 12 with a multi-level maze, slides, ball pits, a dance floor and a padded toddler zone for ages 1 to 3.

  • Packages: "Play Your Way" starts at $32.90 per kid and can be customized with add-ons; "Play Big" is the all-inclusive option at $55.90 per kid.
  • Included: a dedicated party host, 2.5 hours of reserved seating, a dance party with the Huggy dragon mascot, drinks and goodies, plus all-day play.
  • Deposit: $150 at booking.
  • Hours: 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. Monday through Thursday; 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. Friday through Sunday. Phone (561) 621-9677.

Watch out for: parties are at reserved tables in the open play area, not private rooms. Socks are required, waivers are required for every guest and the park is card-only.

3. Greenacres Bowl

Greenacres Bowl on Lake Worth Road has bowling, a new arcade and three weekend birthday packages. All include 2 hours of bowling with shoe rentals, a party attendant, arcade cards and a free game pass per guest for a future visit.

  • Splitzville: from $194.98 for 2 lanes and up to 10 guests; extra lanes $94.99.
  • Lucky Strikes: from $244.98, adds 2 pizzas and 2 pitchers of soda; extra lanes $119.99.
  • Extra Frame: from $319.98, with bigger arcade cards and an autographed bowling pin.
  • When: Saturdays and Sundays at 12:30 p.m. and 3 p.m.

Watch out for: there's a 10-guest minimum, a two-week booking notice, a 50% deposit and a 21% event fee added to all parties, plus tax. Call (561) 968-0100.

4. Urban Air Trampoline and Adventure Park

Urban Air at 10560 Forest Hill Blvd. is the go-to for high-energy parties: an indoor zipline, ropes course, warrior course, climbing walls, dodgeball, trampolines and a tubes playground.

  • Small Squad: $199.99 for 5 guests, at a shared table.
  • Unlimited Play (most popular): $399.99 for 10 guests, with all attractions, Urban Air socks, a shared party host, setup and cleanup, waters, tableware and a T-shirt for the birthday child.
  • Private room: add $100 for a 2-hour private room, which also includes 2 large one-topping pizzas and 2 drink pitchers.
  • Extras: cookie cake $29.99, party favor bags $5.99 per guest, Dippin' Dots $4.99 per guest.

Why families like it: after the 2-hour party, guests keep playing until the park closes. Watch out for: no outside food or drink, height requirements vary by attraction and prices don't include tax.

5. Wellington Aquatics Center

The village's new Wellington Aquatics Center at 3410 120th Ave. S has a zero-depth recreation pool with a play structure and Florida's first retractable NinjaCross obstacle course.

  • Party room: $250 for 2 hours for Wellington residents, $300 for non-residents. It holds up to 35 guests and includes pool admission for 15; extra guests pay daily admission.
  • Cabana: $75 for 4 hours for residents, $100 for non-residents, up to 10 guests. Book at least 48 hours ahead online.
  • Private pool: the whole recreation pool with the play structure and NinjaCross is $450 an hour for residents, $560 for non-residents.

Watch out for: NinjaCross and the high dive require a 48-inch minimum height and a 25-yard swim test, there are no rain checks for weather closures, and coolers and outside food aren't allowed. Questions: (561) 791-4770. Hours and admission are in our Aquatics Center guide.

6. Lemon Drop Play Cafe

Lemon Drop Play Cafe at 4095 S State Road 7, Unit Q2, is a coffee shop with two separate enclosed play areas and no climbing structures, which makes it a calm pick for babies, toddlers and preschoolers. The owner is a certified event planner, and parties rent the whole space.

  • Little Lemon Bash: $350 for 3 hours of private use of the playground and cafe.
  • Zesty Fun: $550, adds party packs and a platter.
  • Ultimate Lemon Drop Party: $750, adds catering, a balloon arch and cafe credit.
  • Princess parties (2.5 hours): The Royal Sparkle $650, The Enchanted Celebration $850 and The Ultimate Royal Affair $1,150 with two princesses, live singing and a mini themed cake.

The website showed $50 off its packages when we checked, with the final price calculated at checkout.

7. Palm Beach Skate Zone

Palm Beach Skate Zone at 8125 Lake Worth Rd., just east of Wellington in suburban Lake Worth, calls itself Palm Beach County's only three-rink ice facility. An ice skating party is weatherproof and feels different from the usual trampoline party.

  • Classic Party: from $425, during public skating sessions, with 10 admissions and skate rentals, 2 lobby tables, a party host, 2 large cheese pizzas, popcorn, drinks, goodie bags with a free skate pass and 3 skate-assist walkers. Extra guests are $29.95 plus tax.
  • Ultimate Party: from $675, with 1 hour of private ice and 2 hours in the lobby, a dedicated host, 10 skate-assist walkers and the same food.
  • Classic party times: Friday 8-11 p.m., Saturday 12:30-3 p.m. and 8-11 p.m., Sunday 12:30-3 p.m. and 6-8 p.m.

Watch out for: no outside food or drinks except the birthday dessert, and bring your own candles, lighter and cake server. The rink recommends booking at least 4 weeks ahead. Phone (561) 963-5900.

8. Lion Country Safari

Lion Country Safari in Loxahatchee is a short drive north of Wellington, and its "Safari Birthday Celebration" turns a park visit into a party.

  • Price: $50 plus tax per guest, with a 20 paid-guest minimum. The birthday child gets in free.
  • Included: admission to the drive-through safari and the walk-through park, a giraffe feeding ticket and a Dippin' Dots voucher for every guest, plus printable invitations. The birthday child also gets a goodie bag.
  • Booking: Saturdays and Sundays only, at least 10 days ahead, through Group Sales at (561) 793-1084 ext. 2270.

9. Goldfish Swim School Royal Palm Beach

Goldfish Swim School at 10329 Southern Blvd. has a 90-degree indoor pool and lists party hours on Saturday and Sunday from 4:30 to 6:30 p.m. Pricing isn't posted online, so call (954) 945-7890 for details. It's a natural fit for kids already in lessons.

10. Saito's Japanese Steakhouse

For a smaller celebration, a hibachi dinner at Saito's near The Mall at Wellington Green lets the chef put on a show at the table. There's no package, just menu prices. Note that a gratuity is added to the bill automatically. More ideas are in our kid-friendly restaurants guide.

Birthday party planning tips

  • Read the guest math. A package for "10 guests" usually means 10 kids, and the birthday child often counts as one. Ask how adults are counted.
  • Add up the fees. Tax, event fees (21% at Greenacres Bowl) and per-guest add-ons can push a $200 package past $300.
  • Book early for weekends. Saturday slots fill first. Several venues require 10 days to 4 weeks of notice.
  • Have everyone sign waivers ahead. Kids Empire, Urban Air and Skate Zone all require them, and online waivers save time at check-in.
  • Order the cake: see bakeries in our desserts guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest place to have a kids birthday party in Wellington?

A village pavilion at Tiger Shark Cove or Village Park costs $53 for a half day or $106 for a full day for a small pavilion on weekends, including tax, next to a free playground.

How much is an Urban Air birthday party in Wellington?

Urban Air Wellington's Small Squad party is $199.99 for 5 guests and its Unlimited Play package is $399.99 for 10 guests, plus tax. A private room adds $100 and includes two pizzas and two drink pitchers.

Can you have a birthday party at the Wellington Aquatics Center?

Yes. The party room is $250 for 2 hours for residents and $300 for non-residents, holds up to 35 guests and includes admission for 15.

How much is a Kids Empire birthday party in Wellington?

Kids Empire Wellington's Play Your Way package starts at $32.90 per kid and the all-inclusive Play Big package is $55.90 per kid, with a $150 deposit.

Where can toddlers have a birthday party in Wellington?

Lemon Drop Play Cafe on State Road 7 rents its enclosed play areas and cafe for private parties starting at $350 for 3 hours, and Kids Empire has a padded toddler zone for ages 1 to 3.
