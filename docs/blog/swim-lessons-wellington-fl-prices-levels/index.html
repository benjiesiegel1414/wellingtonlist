Swim Lessons in Wellington, FL: Levels, Schedule and Prices

By Ben SiegelPublished November 16, 20264 min read

Looking for swim lessons in Wellington, Florida? The village's Wellington Aquatics Center teaches group lessons with American Red Cross certified instructors for $60 per two-week session for residents, and two private swim schools offer year-round indoor lessons nearby. Here's how the village program works, which level fits your child, the full schedule and prices, adult and adaptive lessons, junior lifeguard and lifeguard certification, and how the private options compare.

How village swim lessons work

Lessons at the Aquatics Center run Monday through Thursday for two weeks, for a total of eight classes per session, taught by American Red Cross certified instructors. The fall 2026 schedule ran six sessions from Aug. 24 through Nov. 19, with no classes during Labor Day week. Check the village's Swim Lessons page or the CivicRec catalog for the next session dates.

The recreation pool is kept at 83 to 85 degrees, so lessons continue through winter. The facility closes if the water drops below 75 degrees or for bad weather.

Which level is right for your child?

The village publishes a quick placement check. Getting the level wrong can mean your child is removed from the roster (with a refund), so it's worth a minute.

Ages 3 to 5

  • Can't float on their back or put their face in the water comfortably: Preschool 1.
  • Can float and put their face in, but can't glide in a streamlined position or swim forward three body lengths: Preschool 2.
  • Can do all that and combines arm and leg movements: Preschool 3.

Ages 6 to 13

  • Not comfortable putting their face in or can't float front and back without help: Level 1.
  • Can float, but can't glide streamlined or doesn't know front crawl arms: Level 2.
  • Can swim front crawl 10 yards, take a breath and keep going: Level 3, which introduces breaststroke, butterfly kick and proper breathing.

Not sure? Call the Aquatics Center at (561) 791-4770 and staff will help place your child.

Adult, adaptive and one-on-one lessons

  • Adult (14+): a 30-minute class for novice swimmers covering floating, submerging, kicking and arm motions, with up to four students per instructor.
  • Adaptive Aquatics (3-12): 15 minutes one-on-one with an instructor for children with physical limitations, working at their own pace.
  • In-Between (3-13): 15-minute one-on-one lessons. $105 for residents, $120 for non-residents.

How to register

  1. Get a CivicRec account and add your child to it before registration opens.
  2. Bring proof: two forms of ID for residency (such as a driver's license and utility bill) and a birth certificate for the child.
  3. Register by the Friday before a session starts, online or in person at the Aquatics Center.
  4. Using a Drowning Prevention Coalition of Palm Beach County certificate? You must register in person.

Refunds: request before the second lesson. Injury or illness refunds need a doctor's note, and there's a $10 fee per participant per program for refunds or transfers.

Swim team, passes and lifeguard training

  • Monthly swim team: the village catalog lists monthly swim team options from $85 to $180.
  • Practice swims: daily admission is $5 for resident kids (3-17), $7 for adults and $4 for seniors 55+; non-residents pay $8, $10 and $7. Annual passes start at $150 for resident children and $200 for resident adults, and a two-person resident family pass is $350. Passes are sold in person.
  • Junior Lifeguard (ages 10-14): summer sessions Monday through Thursday, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m., covering lifeguarding basics and surveillance. $90 for residents, $113 for non-residents.
  • Lifeguard certification (15+): $290, including CPR/AED, first aid and lifeguarding certification. Candidates must swim 300 yards continuously, swim 25 yards, retrieve a 10-pound weight from 10 feet deep and return it within 1 minute 40 seconds, and tread water for two minutes using legs only.

Private swim schools near Wellington

If you want year-round weekly lessons or a different schedule, two indoor swim schools serve Wellington families:

  • British Swim School at Wellington, 3141 Fortune Way: year-round small-group lessons for babies, kids, teens and adults in an indoor pool. Call (561) 855-7043 for pricing.
  • Goldfish Swim School Royal Palm Beach, 10329 Southern Blvd.: indoor 90-degree pool, weekly 30-minute lessons with a 4-to-1 student-teacher ratio for ages 4 months to 12 years, starting at $128 a month according to its website, with up to 12 free make-up lessons.

Water safety tips for Wellington families

  • Lessons aren't a substitute for supervision. The Aquatics Center requires parents to stay within arm's reach of children who can't swim, even in life jackets.
  • Watch the canals and lakes. Wellington's neighborhoods are full of water, and alligators live in many of them.
  • Start early. Village preschool levels begin at age 3; private schools start as young as 4 months.

Frequently asked questions

How much are swim lessons at the Wellington Aquatics Center?

Group lessons are $60 per session for Wellington residents and $75 for non-residents. Each session is eight classes, Monday through Thursday for two weeks. One-on-one In-Between lessons are $105 for residents and $120 for non-residents.

What ages can take swim lessons in Wellington?

Preschool levels serve ages 3 to 10, Levels 1-3 serve ages 6 to 13, there's an adult class for ages 14 and up, and Adaptive Aquatics serves ages 3 to 12.

How do I register for Wellington swim lessons?

Register online through CivicRec or in person at the Aquatics Center by the Friday before a session starts. You need two forms of ID for residency and a birth certificate for the child.

Does Wellington offer lifeguard certification?

Yes. Lifeguard certification for ages 15 and up costs $290, and summer Junior Lifeguard sessions for ages 10 to 14 cost $90 for residents and $113 for non-residents.
