Family and general dentists

Molar Bear Dental

Molar Bear Dental sees kids and adults, so the whole family can book in one place. It's open until 7 p.m. on Mondays and Wednesdays and handles weekend emergencies by appointment.

Address: 11924 W Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 10 · Phone: (561) 721-2688

Sage Dental of Wellington

Sage Dental in the Wellington Trace plaza offers general, cosmetic and emergency care with same-day emergency appointments.

Address: 13889 Wellington Trace, Suite A5-A6 · Phone: (561) 795-8900 · Mon to Fri 8 to 5

Wellington Smiles (Dr. Steven Miller)

A long-standing family practice on the west end of Forest Hill Blvd. Wellington Smiles offers same-day visits for dental pain and an after-hours answering service.

Address: 12788 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 2001 · Phone: (561) 798-8023

City Dental of Wellington

City Dental on State Road 7 is one of the few Wellington offices open on Saturdays (8 a.m. to 2 p.m.) and stays open until 6 p.m. Monday through Thursday.

Address: 2803 S State Road 7, Suite 100 · Phone: (561) 249-3225

Mohip Dental & Associates

Drs. Vikram Mohip and Adam Walters offer general and cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, same-day crowns and laser dentistry, with Saturday hours from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Mohip Dental.

Address: 250 Professional Way · Phone: (561) 798-1600

How to choose a dentist in Wellington

  • Check insurance first. Confirm the office is in network for your plan before booking.
  • Ask about new patient specials. Many Wellington practices offer discounted first exams and cleanings.
  • Ask how emergencies are handled. Find out whether they hold same-day slots and what happens after hours.
  • Pick a convenient location. Most dentists cluster along Forest Hill Blvd and State Road 7.
  • Read recent reviews, paying attention to comments about wait times and billing.

Hours change, so call to confirm before your visit. In a hurry? See our guide to emergency and 24-hour dentists in Wellington, or browse all dentists in Wellington.

Frequently asked questions

Which Wellington dentists are open on Saturday?

City Dental of Wellington (8 a.m. to 2 p.m.), Mohip Dental & Associates (9 a.m. to 1 p.m.) and OrthoMike Orthodontics are open Saturdays, and KA Dental in nearby Royal Palm Beach is open Saturday 9 to 3.

Who is a good pediatric dentist in Wellington?

Pediatric options include Children & Pediatric Dentistry of Wellington on Forest Hill Blvd, POP Wellington and Pediatric Dentistry & Family Orthodontics on State Road 7, and Palm Beach Children's Dentistry.

Do any Wellington dentists accept Medicaid?

Children & Pediatric Dentistry of Wellington advertises Medicaid acceptance for kids. Always call to confirm your specific plan.

Where can I find an emergency dentist in Wellington?

Sage Dental, Soft Hands Dental, Wellington Smiles and POP Wellington offer same-day emergency visits, and Molar Bear Dental sees weekend emergencies by appointment. See our emergency dentist guide for details.
