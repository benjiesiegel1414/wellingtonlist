    Fishing in Wellington, FL: Children's Holiday Fishing Classic, Where to Fish and License Rules

    By Ben SiegelPublished November 17, 20264 min read

    Wellington's free 36th annual Children's Holiday Fishing Classic is Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026, from 8 to 11:30 a.m. at Village Park, and registration opens Monday, Nov. 23. Here's everything families need for the tournament: age groups, rules, prizes and what to bring. Plus where else to fish in and around Wellington, the boat ramp permit rule, and who needs a Florida fishing license.

    About the Children's Holiday Fishing Classic

    Held every December, the Children's Holiday Fishing Classic is a free tournament for kids 15 and younger, presented by the village with Building Up Sports Academy and the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC). It's open to all local children and guests, not just Wellington residents.

    Prizes go to first, second and third place in each age group, based on a point system for fish caught and released. All species count.

    Rules parents should know

    • Kids fish on their own. Parents are encouraged to watch, but no one over 15 may fish or help, except to bait hooks and take fish off.
    • Catch and release. Points are for fish caught and released at the tournament site.
    • No boats.
    • Bait: provided. You can bring your own bait, but no live shiners.
    • Rods: bring your own if you can. Only a very limited number of loaner rods will be available.

    How to register

    Registration is free and opens Monday, Nov. 23. Sign up online through the village's CivicRec recreation catalog at wellingtonfl.gov/CivicRec or in person at Village Park. Need an account? See our youth sports guide for how CivicRec sign-up works.

    Make a day of it: the same evening, decorated boats light up Lake Wellington in the Holiday Boat Parade from 6:30 to 8 p.m. See our Wellington holiday events guide.

    Okeeheelee Park: the best bet for families

    Palm Beach County's Okeeheelee Park at 7715 Forest Hill Blvd., just east of Wellington, has freshwater fishing in a stocked lake, a fishing pier, picnic shelters and restrooms, according to Palm Beach County Parks. There's also a boat launch on the freshwater fishing lake for non-motorized boats, plus kayak and canoe access. The park is open sunrise to sunset, and its nature center and trails make it easy to turn a fishing trip into a full morning. Details are in our trails guide.

    Lake Wellington and the Forest Hill Boat Ramp

    Wellington's public boat ramp at 12500 Forest Hill Blvd. has a pavilion and picnic tables and is open sunrise to sunset. Use of the ramp is by permit only; contact the village's Planning and Zoning Department to request one. Lake Wellington is also home to the Florida Rowing Center's boathouse and its 2,000-meter rowing course, so watch for rowers.

    Where fishing isn't allowed

    Fishing and feeding wildlife are prohibited at Peaceful Waters Sanctuary, which is also home to alligators. For neighborhood lakes and canals, rules depend on who owns or manages the water, such as your HOA or the drainage district, so check before you cast.

    Do you need a Florida fishing license?

    Kids in the Holiday Fishing Classic don't need one. Under FWC's 2026 freshwater rules:

    • Children under 16 don't need a fishing license.
    • Florida residents 65 and older are exempt with proof of age and residency, such as a Florida driver license or ID.
    • Residents fishing with a cane pole (live or natural bait, no reel) in their home county are exempt, except in fish management areas.
    • Everyone else 16 to 64 needs a license. A resident annual freshwater license is $17, and a resident five-year license is $79, plus handling fees when bought online or by phone.
    • Free fishing days: FWC lists April 3-4 and June 12-13, 2027 as license-free freshwater fishing weekends.

    Buy a license at GoOutdoorsFlorida.com, by phone at 888-347-4356, through the FWC Fish-Hunt-FL app or at the Palm Beach County Tax Collector's office.

    Tips for fishing with kids

    • Start simple. A light rod, small hooks and bread or worms work well for bluegill and other panfish.
    • Bring a towel and pliers for safe catch-and-release.
    • Watch for alligators. Keep kids and pets back from the water's edge and never feed wildlife.
    • Sun protection matters, even in December. Hats, sunscreen and water are a must.

    Frequently asked questions

    When is Wellington's Children's Holiday Fishing Classic?

    Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026, from 8 to 11:30 a.m. at Village Park, 11700 Pierson Rd. The tournament itself runs 9 to 11 a.m.

    How do I register for the Children's Holiday Fishing Classic?

    Registration is free and opens Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, online through the village's CivicRec catalog or in person at Village Park. It's for children 15 and younger.

    Do kids need a fishing license in Florida?

    No. Children under 16 don't need a Florida fishing license. Florida residents 65 and older are also exempt with proof of age and residency.

    Can you use the Wellington boat ramp?

    The Forest Hill Boat Ramp at 12500 Forest Hill Blvd. is open sunrise to sunset, but use is by permit only from the village's Planning and Zoning Department.
