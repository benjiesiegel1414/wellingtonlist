The Best Parks and Nature Spots in Wellington, FL

By Wellington List staffPublished October 3, 20264 min read

One of the best things about Wellington, Florida is how much green space there is. Between village parks, a lakefront Town Center and wild preserves full of birds and alligators, there is somewhere to walk, play or explore within a few minutes of almost every neighborhood. Here are the best parks and nature spots in and around Wellington.

1. Village Park

Village Park on Pierson Road is Wellington's biggest sports and recreation park, with fields and courts for just about every youth sport, a gym, walking paths and playgrounds. If your kids play rec or travel sports in Wellington, you will get to know this place well.

Address: 11700 Pierson Rd, Wellington, FL 33414

2. Peaceful Waters Sanctuary

Right next to Village Park, Peaceful Waters Sanctuary is a restored wetland with a boardwalk over the water. It is one of the best places in Palm Beach County for easy bird watching, with herons, egrets, ibis and anhingas, and yes, the occasional alligator. Go early in the morning for the best wildlife and the coolest temperatures.

Address: 11676 Pierson Rd, Wellington, FL 33414

3. Wellington Environmental Preserve

For a wilder experience, the Wellington Environmental Preserve on Flying Cow Ranch Road protects a large stretch of marsh and wetlands on the western edge of the village. It has trails for walking, biking and horseback riding, plus observation spots overlooking the water. It is quiet, open and feels miles from the suburbs.

Address: 3491 Flying Cow Ranch Rd, Wellington, FL 33414

4. Wellington Town Center and the Amphitheater

Town Center on Forest Hill Blvd is the village's front lawn, with a promenade along Lake Wellington, playgrounds, the Wellington Aquatics Complex and the Wellington Amphitheater, which hosts free concerts, movie nights and food truck events throughout the year. It is the easiest place to spend an evening outside with kids.

Address: 12100 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414

5. Neighborhood parks

Beyond the big four, Wellington has dozens of smaller neighborhood parks with playgrounds, courts and lake views tucked into residential areas. Check the Village of Wellington's parks and recreation pages for the closest one to you, along with facility rentals and program schedules.

Great parks a short drive away

Okeeheelee Park

Okeeheelee Park in suburban West Palm Beach is one of the largest county parks in the area, with lakes, trails, a golf course, a nature center and water sports. It is about 15 minutes east of Wellington on Forest Hill Blvd.

Mounts Botanical Garden

Mounts Botanical Garden on Military Trail is the oldest and largest botanical garden in Palm Beach County, with themed gardens and a popular elevated walkway.

Lion Country Safari

Not a park exactly, but Lion Country Safari in Loxahatchee is a drive-through safari a few minutes north of Wellington and a favorite day out for local families.

Tips for enjoying Wellington parks

  • Go early or late. From May through October, mornings and evenings are far more comfortable, and wildlife is more active.
  • Bring bug spray and water. Especially at the preserves after summer rains.
  • Respect the wildlife. Keep a safe distance from alligators and never feed them.
  • Check event calendars. Town Center and the Amphitheater have frequent free events. See our Wellington events calendar.

Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest park in Wellington, FL?

Village Park on Pierson Road is Wellington's largest sports and recreation park, with fields, courts, a gym, walking paths and playgrounds.

Where can you see wildlife in Wellington, FL?

Peaceful Waters Sanctuary, with its boardwalk over a restored wetland, and the Wellington Environmental Preserve on Flying Cow Ranch Road are the best spots for birds and other wildlife.

Are there free events at Wellington parks?

Yes. The Wellington Amphitheater at Town Center hosts free concerts, movie nights and food truck events throughout the year.
