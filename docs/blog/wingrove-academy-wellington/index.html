Wingrove Academy: Everything We Know About Wellington's New Private School

By Wellington List staffPublished October 2, 20265 min read
Advertisement Sponsor WellingtonList.comPromote your local business or service here, in front of Wellington residents every day. Become a sponsor

Wellington is getting a new private school, and it is a big one. Wingrove Academy, a nonprofit PreK through 12 independent school, is being built on the K-Park land along Stribling Way as part of the same project that will bring Village Landing to State Road 7. Here is what we know so far about the campus, the opening timeline, tuition and scholarships for Wellington families.

Wingrove Academy at a glance

Type
Nonprofit, independent PreK through 12 school
Location
Back portion of the K-Park site off Stribling Way, west of State Road 7
Developer
ElevateED, in partnership with Related Ross
Planned size
About 45 acres, with plans to eventually serve around 1,700 students
Opening
First phase targeted for August 2028
Tuition
Not yet announced
Website
wingroveacademy.org

Who is behind Wingrove Academy?

The school is being developed by ElevateED, an education management organization founded by Karen Yung. Yung is a co-founder of Education in Motion, an international school group that has opened private schools in cities including London, Singapore and Bali. Wingrove is ElevateED's first project and is planned as its flagship school.

The school is also backed by Stephen Ross, whose company Related Ross is building the Village Landing town center on the front portion of the same property. In a statement, Ross tied the school to Palm Beach County's ability to attract growing businesses and families.

Where did the name come from?

For much of 2025, planning documents referred to the project with the placeholder "New School in Wellington." The official name, Wingrove Academy, was announced on August 18, 2025, along with the school's mission of combining rigorous academics with visual and performing arts, competitive athletics and whole-child development on a purpose-built, sustainable campus.

When does Wingrove Academy open?

The current timetable calls for the first phase of the school to open in August 2028, around the same time Village Landing is expected to open next door. Reports have indicated the high school program will follow, with a first high school class planned for 2029. A national search for a founding head of school has been led by education search firm RG175.

How much will tuition cost?

Tuition has not been announced. Early reporting said tuition is expected to be competitive with other independent schools in the area, where top schools can exceed $40,000 a year. We will update this page as soon as Wingrove publishes official tuition figures.

Scholarships for Wellington residents

Scholarships became a major topic at Village Council meetings, with council and education committee members pushing for clear commitments to local students. In February 2026, ElevateED returned with specifics: about $10 million in need-based scholarships aimed at roughly 100 Wellington students. To qualify, a student must:

  • Live in Wellington at the time of application
  • Meet the school's admission standards
  • Demonstrate financial need

Earlier presentations also described a founders scholarship program equal to 20 full scholarships per year for the school's first five years.

Why Wellington?

School organizers have pointed out that hundreds of Wellington students already attend private schools outside the village, and some families spend hours a day commuting. Organizers estimated that 400 to 500 Wellington students currently attend private schools elsewhere, and the school expects to draw students from across the region as well. Early descriptions of the curriculum also mentioned flexible pathways for students pursuing serious commitments outside the classroom, which in Wellington often means equestrian sport.

Campus access and traffic

The school will sit on the north and back side of the K-Park land. As part of the approvals, a new roundabout is planned on the north side of the property to serve as the school's entrance, along with lane additions around Stribling Way, State Road 7 and the Fairlane Farms Road roundabout. Developers have said these road improvements need to be finished before the site opens. You can read more about the traffic plan in our Village Landing guide.

Our take

For Wellington families, a full PreK through 12 independent school inside the village is a genuine new option, and the local scholarship commitment is meaningful. The open questions are tuition, admissions details and how traffic on Stribling Way holds up once both the school and Village Landing are running. We will keep this guide updated as Wingrove shares more.

Frequently asked questions

When does Wingrove Academy open?

Wingrove Academy is targeting August 2028 for the opening of its first phase, with a first high school class reported for 2029.

Where is Wingrove Academy located?

The school is being built on the back portion of Wellington's K-Park property off Stribling Way, west of State Road 7, next to the Village Landing town center.

How much is tuition at Wingrove Academy?

Tuition has not been announced yet. Early reports said it is expected to be competitive with other independent schools in Palm Beach County.

Does Wingrove Academy offer scholarships for Wellington residents?

Yes. ElevateED presented about $10 million in need-based scholarships for roughly 100 Wellington students. Applicants must live in Wellington, meet admission standards and demonstrate financial need.

Who is building Wingrove Academy?

The school is being developed by ElevateED, founded by Education in Motion co-founder Karen Yung, in partnership with Stephen Ross and Related Ross.
