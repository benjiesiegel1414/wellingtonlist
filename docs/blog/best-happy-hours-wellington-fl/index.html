12 Best Happy Hours in Wellington, FL

By Ben SiegelPublished November 10, 20266 min read

Looking for the best happy hour in Wellington, Florida? Wellington has happy hours that start as early as 2 p.m., Greek and Asian spots with afternoon specials, a steakhouse "social hour," margarita bars and a rock pub that keeps happy hour going until 7. Here are 12 happy hours in Wellington with their times, addresses and what to order.

We picked these from the restaurants and bars on Wellington List that run a regular happy hour, and we list the times each one publishes. Every spot below links to its full Wellington List listing. No business paid to be included, and the list is in no particular order of quality. Hours were last checked in November 2026 and can change, especially around holidays, so call ahead.

1. Lindburgers

Lindburgers on Wellington Trace has the longest happy hour on this list, running daily from 2 to 7 p.m. The menu has roughly 50 gourmet burgers, plus wings and hot dogs.

What to try: Wings and a burger to share during happy hour.

Tip: Kids eat free on Mondays with an adult meal, so it works for a family happy hour too.

  • Address: 13860 Wellington Trace, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 753-0555
  • Hours: Sun-Wed 11 a.m.-9 p.m.; Thu 11 a.m.-9:30 p.m.; Fri-Sat 11 a.m.-10 p.m.
  • See the full Lindburgers listing

2. Spiro's Taverna of Wellington

Spiro's Taverna in the Shoppes at Chancellor on South Shore Blvd opened in April 2026 as the Treasure Coast group's first Palm Beach County location. Happy hour runs daily from 3 to 6 p.m., and the menu has more than 90 Greek and American dishes.

What to try: Spanakopita and dolmades to share, or a gyro.

  • Address: 12020 S Shore Blvd, Suite 900, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Hours: Daily 11 a.m.-9 p.m.
  • See the full Spiro's Taverna of Wellington listing

3. Lemongrass Asian Bistro

Lemongrass Asian Bistro at The Mall at Wellington Green runs a daily happy hour from 3 to 6 p.m., with sushi rolls, Thai dishes and ramen on the menu.

What to try: Sushi rolls and Thai appetizers.

Tip: It's a good stop before a movie at CMX Cinemas in the mall.

  • Address: 10312 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 202, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 459-1598
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 11:30 a.m.-9 p.m.; Fri 11:30 a.m.-10 p.m.; Sat noon-10 p.m.; Sun noon-9 p.m.
  • See the full Lemongrass Asian Bistro listing

4. Chili's Grill & Bar

Chili's on State Road 7 runs a weekday happy hour from 3 to 6 p.m. at its full bar, which pours margaritas.

What to try: A margarita with chips and salsa or an appetizer.

Tip: The kitchen stays open until midnight on Fridays and Saturdays.

  • Address: 2525 S State Road 7, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 790-0062
  • Hours: Sun-Thu 11 a.m.-11 p.m.; Fri-Sat 11 a.m.-midnight
  • See the full Chili's Grill & Bar listing

5. Copper Blues Rock Pub & Kitchen

Copper Blues Rock Pub & Kitchen on the upper level of The Mall at Wellington Green runs happy hour daily from 4 to 7 p.m. It serves pub food, shareable plates and craft cocktails, and after dark it turns into a live music spot.

What to try: Shareable plates and craft cocktails before a show at the Palm Beach Improv next door.

  • Address: 10300 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 404-4101
  • Hours: Mon to Wed 4 p.m. to midnight, Thu 4 p.m. to 1 a.m., Fri 4 p.m. to 3 a.m., Sat 11 a.m. to 3 a.m., Sun 11 a.m. to midnight (as listed; confirm after opening)
  • See the full Copper Blues Rock Pub & Kitchen listing

6. Rivales Taqueria and Craft Bar

Rivales Taqueria and Craft Bar in the Publix plaza at Forest Hill and South Shore Blvd runs a daily happy hour from 4 to 6 p.m. The bar focuses on handcrafted margaritas and cocktails.

What to try: A margarita with al pastor or pork belly tacos.

  • Address: 11924 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 28, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 345-3737
  • See the full Rivales Taqueria and Craft Bar listing

7. Lewis Prime Grill

Lewis Prime Grill at The Pointe at Wellington Green calls its happy hour Prime Social Hour, daily from 4 to 6 p.m. The steakhouse opened in May 2026 and comes from the family behind Okeechobee Steakhouse.

What to try: Shareable starters at the bar.

Tip: Stay for dinner: steaks come with add-on toppings.

  • Address: 10120 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Hours: Mon-Sat 4-10 p.m.; Sun 4-9 p.m.
  • See the full Lewis Prime Grill listing

8. DeVine Bistro

DeVine Bistro on State Road 7, in front of the Wellington Green mall, is a dinner-only American bistro led by executive chef Dave Palmateer. Happy hour runs Tuesday through Saturday from 5 to 6:30 p.m.

What to try: A drink at happy hour before pistachio-crusted salmon or Guinness-braised short ribs.

Tip: Dress is business casual, and it's closed Sundays and Mondays.

  • Address: 2465 S State Road 7, Suite 300, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 204-5432
  • Hours: Tue-Sat 5 p.m.-9 p.m.; Sun-Mon closed
  • See the full DeVine Bistro listing

9. Casa Tequila

Casa Tequila in Wellington Plaza runs a weekday happy hour with margaritas, and it's been serving classic Mexican food on Forest Hill Blvd since 2013.

What to try: A margarita and sizzling fajitas.

Tip: Wednesday is mariachi night.

  • Address: 12795 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 557-1378
  • Hours: Sun-Thu 11 a.m.-10 p.m.; Fri-Sat 11 a.m.-11 p.m.
  • See the full Casa Tequila listing

10. Kickback Neighborhood Tavern

Kickback Neighborhood Tavern in Wellington Plaza runs a daily happy hour, with elevated tavern food and weekly chalkboard specials.

What to try: A local beer on the dog-friendly covered patio.

Tip: Look for trivia nights and live music on weekends.

  • Address: 12771 W Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 795-0100
  • See the full Kickback Neighborhood Tavern listing

11. Mole Cantina Mexicana

Mole Cantina Mexicana in Wellington Green Commons, the Whole Foods plaza on State Road 7, offers a happy hour with margaritas made from 100% agave tequila.

What to try: A margarita with tableside guacamole on the patio.

  • Address: 2557 S State Road 7, Suite 150, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 355-5322
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 12 p.m.-9:45 p.m.; Fri 12 p.m.-10:30 p.m.; Sat 11 a.m.-10:45 p.m.; Sun 11 a.m.-9:45 p.m.
  • See the full Mole Cantina Mexicana listing

12. Kabuki Sushi Thai Tapas

Kabuki Sushi Thai Tapas on State Road 7 has a bar with a happy hour, indoor booths and outdoor tables.

What to try: Sushi rolls and dumplings with a drink.

  • Address: 2465 S State Road 7, Suite 100, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 323-4888
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 11:30 a.m.-10 p.m.; Fri-Sat 11:30 a.m.-11 p.m.; Sun 11:30 a.m.-10 p.m.
  • See the full Kabuki Sushi Thai Tapas listing

Tips for happy hour in Wellington

  • Confirm before you go. Happy hour times and specials can change, and some apply only at the bar. Call ahead or check the restaurant's website.
  • Pick your time: Lindburgers starts at 2 p.m., several spots start at 3 p.m., and Copper Blues runs latest, until 7 p.m.
  • Plan the night: pair a happy hour with a show at Palm Beach Improv or a free concert at the Wellington Amphitheater.
  • Get home safe: use a rideshare or a designated driver.
  • More: see the best date night restaurants and all Wellington restaurants.

Frequently asked questions

What restaurant in Wellington has the longest happy hour?

Lindburgers on Wellington Trace runs happy hour daily from 2 to 7 p.m.

Which Wellington happy hours start at 3 p.m.?

Spiro's Taverna on South Shore Blvd and Lemongrass Asian Bistro at the mall run happy hour daily from 3 to 6 p.m., and Chili's on State Road 7 runs a weekday happy hour from 3 to 6 p.m.

Is there a late happy hour in Wellington, FL?

Copper Blues Rock Pub & Kitchen at The Mall at Wellington Green runs happy hour daily from 4 to 7 p.m. DeVine Bistro's happy hour runs Tuesday through Saturday from 5 to 6:30 p.m.

Where can I get margaritas at happy hour in Wellington?

Rivales Taqueria (daily 4 to 6 p.m.), Casa Tequila (weekdays), Mole Cantina and Chili's all serve margaritas during happy hour.
