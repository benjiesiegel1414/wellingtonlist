13 Best Gyms and Fitness Studios in Wellington, FL

By Ben SiegelPublished November 7, 20267 min read

Looking for the best gym in Wellington, Florida? Since LA Fitness on State Road 7 closed, Wellington residents have been shopping around, and there are more choices than ever: big-box gyms with saunas and hot yoga, value gyms open nearly around the clock, coach-led HIIT and CrossFit, reformer Pilates studios, a kickboxing circuit, and the village's own clay-court tennis center and new aquatics center. Here are 13 gyms and fitness studios in and around Wellington, with addresses, hours and what each one is best for.

We picked these from the gyms, studios and public fitness facilities on Wellington List, covering every budget and workout style. Every spot below links to its full Wellington List listing. No business paid to be included, and the list is in no particular order of quality. Hours were last checked in November 2026 and can change, especially around holidays, so call ahead.

1. Crunch Fitness Wellington

Crunch Fitness opened in June 2022 in the fully renovated former Ultima Fitness space in Wellington Plaza on Forest Hill Blvd. At about 30,000 square feet, it's the biggest full-service gym in the village, with a group fitness studio, a HIIT Zone, a hot yoga studio and a cycling studio.

What to try: Hot yoga or a cycling class, then the dry sauna or a HydroMassage bed.

Tip: Ask about class schedules for the hot yoga and cycling studios before you join, since some classes fill up.

  • Address: 12799 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 461-8477
  • See the full Crunch Fitness Wellington listing

2. YouFit Gyms

YouFit on Wellington Trace is a location of the Florida-based value gym chain, with cardio machines, strength equipment, a functional training area, lockers and showers. When Ultima Fitness closed, its standard memberships moved to YouFit.

What to try: Late-night lifting: it's open until 11:59 p.m. Monday through Thursday.

Tip: YouFit participates in SilverSneakers, so eligible seniors should ask about it.

  • Address: 13865 Wellington Trace, Suite C, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 839-1925
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 5 a.m.-11:59 p.m.; Fri 5 a.m.-10 p.m.; Sat-Sun 8 a.m.-8 p.m.
  • See the full YouFit Gyms listing

3. Planet Fitness Royal Palm Beach

Planet Fitness on Southern Blvd in Royal Palm Beach, a short drive north of Wellington, stays open around the clock on weekdays, from 5 a.m. Monday until 10 p.m. Friday. It has cardio and strength equipment, free weights and a circuit training area.

What to try: A low-key, judgment-free workout at any hour during the week.

Tip: Higher-tier memberships add hydromassage beds, massage chairs and tanning.

  • Address: 10463 Southern Blvd, Royal Palm Beach, FL 33411
  • Phone: (561) 333-3833
  • Hours: Open 24 hours from Mon 5 a.m. to Fri 10 p.m.; Sat-Sun 6 a.m.-9 p.m.
  • See the full Planet Fitness Royal Palm Beach listing

4. Orangetheory Fitness Wellington

Orangetheory Fitness on State Road 7 runs coach-led, heart-rate-tracked interval classes that mix treadmill, rower and strength work. The studio is open daily from 5 a.m. to 9 p.m.

What to try: A full-body interval class if you like being told exactly what to do.

Tip: Holiday hours can vary, so check before you go.

  • Address: 2625 S State Road 7, Suite 610, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Hours: Daily 5 a.m.-9 p.m. (holiday hours may vary)
  • See the full Orangetheory Fitness Wellington listing

5. Thoroughbred CrossFit

Thoroughbred CrossFit is an official CrossFit affiliate in the Fortune Way business park. It's owned by Danielle Szpindor Viale, a three-time CrossFit Games team athlete, and runs one-hour coached classes where every workout is scaled to each member's level.

What to try: The beginner onboarding sessions, which teach the basic movements before regular classes.

Tip: Drop-ins are welcome if you already do CrossFit and are visiting for WEF season.

  • Address: 3133 Fortune Way, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 632-7518
  • See the full Thoroughbred CrossFit listing

6. Cat 5 Fitness

Cat 5 Fitness, also on Fortune Way, is a coached group training studio focused on strength, hybrid training, HIIT and circuits. It has free weights, functional training space and machines, and offers personal training.

What to try: An early-morning strength class. Weekday classes start at 5 a.m.

Tip: Saturday has just one short morning session, and it's closed Sundays.

  • Address: 3125 Fortune Way, Bay 16, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 704-4982
  • Hours: Mon-Fri 5 a.m.-7 p.m.; Sat 7:30 a.m.-9 a.m.; Sun closed
  • See the full Cat 5 Fitness listing

7. 9Round Kickboxing Fitness

9Round on Wellington Trace is a 30-minute kickboxing circuit with no set class times: you rotate through nine three-minute stations, from heavy bags to functional strength, with a trainer guiding you and heart rate tracking.

What to try: A quick lunch-hour workout that fits around your schedule.

Tip: First-timers can book a free intro session, and gloves are provided.

  • Address: 13860 Wellington Trace, Suite 15, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 660-6131
  • See the full 9Round Kickboxing Fitness listing

8. Club Pilates Wellington

Club Pilates on Forest Hill Blvd opened in Wellington in 2018 and teaches small group classes centered on the reformer, with classes from early morning into the evening on weekdays and shorter weekend hours.

What to try: An intro reformer class if you're new to Pilates.

  • Address: 10660 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 140, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 208-1233
  • Hours: Mon and Wed 6 a.m.-8:30 p.m.; Tue and Thu 6:30 a.m.-8:30 p.m.; Fri 6 a.m.-7:30 p.m.; Sat 7:30 a.m.-2:30 p.m.; Sun 9 a.m.-3 p.m.
  • See the full Club Pilates Wellington listing

9. JETSET Pilates Wellington

JETSET Pilates on South State Road 7 teaches a 50-minute signature reformer class built to be high intensity but low impact. Classes are booked ahead, including through ClassPass.

What to try: The JETSET Reformer class.

Tip: Bring grip socks; they're required on the reformers.

  • Address: 2535 S State Road 7, Unit 110, Wellington, FL 33414
  • See the full JETSET Pilates Wellington listing

10. Rooted Wellness Club

Rooted Wellness Club on Forest Hill Blvd is a boutique studio focused on women and families. The schedule centers on mat Pilates, including pelvic floor and core sessions, along with hatha, vinyasa and restorative yoga, strength and toning, and stretch and recovery.

What to try: Prenatal or postpartum fitness, or a baby-friendly class.

Tip: It also hosts women's self-defense workshops.

  • Address: 12777 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 1504, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 568-2124
  • Hours: Mon-Fri 8 a.m.-8 p.m.; Sat 8 a.m.-2 p.m.; Sun closed
  • See the full Rooted Wellness Club listing

11. Balance Fitness

Balance Fitness on Fairlane Farms Road is a personal training studio founded by Lynette Laufenberg. Its approach goes beyond workouts to habits around nutrition, sleep and stress.

What to try: One-on-one personal training if you want accountability.

  • Address: 3220 Fairlane Farms Rd, Suite 5, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 812-2647
  • See the full Balance Fitness listing

12. Wellington Tennis Center

The village-run Wellington Tennis Center on Lyons Road opened in 2015 with 21 Har-Tru clay courts, a clubhouse and a pro shop. Pros teach private and group lessons, and there's cardio tennis, leagues and junior programs from age 5.

What to try: A cardio tennis class for a workout that doesn't feel like one.

Tip: You can pay daily court fees or buy an annual permit.

  • Address: 3100 Lyons Rd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 791-4775
  • See the full Wellington Tennis Center listing

13. Wellington Aquatics Center

The Wellington Aquatics Center at Village Park opened in January 2026 with a heated Olympic competition pool, diving boards and a zero-depth recreation pool. It's a great option for lap swimmers, and Village Park next door has a 2.5-mile walking trail with exercise stations.

What to try: Lap swimming in the competition pool.

Tip: Residents pay lower daily rates than non-residents. See our aquatics center guide.

  • Address: 3410 120th Ave S, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 791-4770
  • Hours: Labor Day to Memorial Day: Mon-Sat 9 a.m.-7 p.m., Sun noon-7 p.m.; Memorial Day to Labor Day: Mon-Sat 8 a.m.-8 p.m., Sun noon-8 p.m.
  • See the full Wellington Aquatics Center listing

How to choose a gym in Wellington

  • Try before you sign. Most gyms and studios offer a free first visit, intro class or trial. Ask about it before you commit.
  • Read the contract. Ask about annual fees, enrollment fees and how to cancel, especially at big-box gyms.
  • Think about when you'll go. If you work out late at night, YouFit and Planet Fitness keep the longest hours. Class studios mostly run early mornings and evenings.
  • Check your insurance. Some Medicare plans include SilverSneakers, which YouFit accepts.
  • Outdoor options are free. Wellington's parks have trails, courts and fields. See our best parks in Wellington and the Diadem pickleball complex.
  • More: browse all gym and fitness listings and swim lessons in Wellington.

Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest gym in Wellington, FL?

Crunch Fitness in Wellington Plaza, 12799 Forest Hill Blvd, is about 30,000 square feet, with a HIIT Zone, a hot yoga studio, a cycling studio, dry saunas and HydroMassage beds.

Is there a 24-hour gym near Wellington, FL?

Planet Fitness on Southern Blvd in Royal Palm Beach is open 24 hours on weekdays, from 5 a.m. Monday to 10 p.m. Friday. YouFit on Wellington Trace is open until 11:59 p.m. Monday through Thursday.

Where did LA Fitness members in Wellington go?

The LA Fitness on State Road 7 in Wellington closed. Nearby options include Crunch Fitness on Forest Hill Blvd, YouFit on Wellington Trace and Planet Fitness in Royal Palm Beach, plus class studios like Orangetheory and Club Pilates.

Where can I do Pilates in Wellington?

Club Pilates on Forest Hill Blvd and JETSET Pilates on South State Road 7 teach reformer Pilates. Rooted Wellness Club on Forest Hill Blvd teaches mat Pilates, including pelvic floor and core classes.
