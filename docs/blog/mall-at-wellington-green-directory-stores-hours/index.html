The Mall at Wellington Green Directory: Every Store, Restaurant and Hours

By Ben SiegelPublished November 5, 20265 min read

Looking for The Mall at Wellington Green store directory? Here's every store, restaurant and attraction on the mall's official directory as of November 2026, sorted by category, plus mall hours, holiday events and tips for visiting. The two-level Mall at Wellington Green sits at 10300 Forest Hill Blvd. at State Road 7 in Wellington, Florida.

The Mall at Wellington Green at a glance

Address
10300 Forest Hill Blvd., Wellington, FL 33414 (at Forest Hill Blvd. and State Road 7)
Mall hours
Monday-Saturday 10 a.m.-8 p.m.; Sunday 11 a.m.-6 p.m.
Phone
(561) 227-6900 (mall office)
Department stores
Dillard's, JCPenney and Macy's, plus City Furniture and Ashley Homestore

CMX Cinemas Wellington 10, Palm Beach Improv, Copper Blues and Arcade Time

Hours are from the mall's website. Restaurants, department stores and the movie theater often keep their own hours, and the mall posts extended holiday hours each season, so check before you go.

Holiday events and Santa photos 2026

The mall's events calendar lists these holiday events for 2026:

  • Photos with Santa: starting Nov. 13, with Santa's arrival celebration for Kids Club on Nov. 14, 5-7 p.m.
  • Black Friday: Nov. 27, with the mall listing 8 a.m.-9 p.m.
  • Small Business Saturday: Nov. 28
  • Pet photos with Santa: Nov. 30 and Dec. 7, 5-7 p.m.
  • Sensory Santa: Dec. 6, 10-11 a.m., a calmer visit for children with sensory needs
  • Hanukkah celebration: Dec. 6, 3-6 p.m.
  • Storytime with Mrs. Claus: Dec. 16, 10:30-11:30 a.m.

Santa photo packages can be reserved online through the mall's website. For more around town, see our Wellington events calendar.

Tips for visiting

  • Timing: weekend afternoons and the weeks before Christmas are the busiest times to visit.
  • Getting there: the mall is at the corner of Forest Hill Blvd. and State Road 7, a few minutes east of Wellington Town Center.
  • Questions: call the mall office at (561) 227-6900.
  • Nearby: The Pointe at Wellington Green and Wellington Green Square have more restaurants and cafes nearby on Forest Hill Blvd. See our Wellington restaurant guide and best coffee shops.

Frequently asked questions

What stores are at The Mall at Wellington Green?

The mall's official directory lists about 150 stores, restaurants and attractions, including department stores Dillard's, JCPenney and Macy's, City Furniture, Ashley Homestore, the Apple Store, H&M, American Eagle, Hollister, Bath & Body Works, Victoria's Secret, Pandora and Foot Locker, plus CMX Cinemas Wellington 10, Palm Beach Improv and Copper Blues.

What are The Mall at Wellington Green hours?

Mall hours are Monday through Saturday from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., according to the mall's website. Restaurants, department stores and the movie theater may keep different hours, and extended holiday hours are posted each season.

Where is The Mall at Wellington Green?

The mall is at 10300 Forest Hill Blvd., Wellington, FL 33414, at the corner of Forest Hill Blvd. and State Road 7. The mall office phone number is (561) 227-6900.

When is Santa at The Mall at Wellington Green in 2026?

The mall's events calendar lists photos with Santa starting Nov. 13, 2026, with Santa's arrival celebration on Nov. 14, pet photos with Santa on Nov. 30 and Dec. 7, and a Sensory Santa visit on Dec. 6. Photo packages can be reserved online through the mall's website.

Is there a movie theater at The Mall at Wellington Green?

Yes. CMX Cinemas Wellington 10 is inside the mall.

What happened to Nordstrom at The Mall at Wellington Green?

The Nordstrom store has been closed since 2019. A developer has proposed replacing the space with about 620 apartments.
