Wellington Spring Soccer 2027 Registration: Dates, Fees and How to Sign Up

By Ben SiegelPublished November 11, 20265 min read

Registration for Wellington's Spring Soccer 2027 League is open. Wellington residents could sign up starting Monday, Nov. 9, 2026, and non-residents can register starting Thursday, Nov. 12 at 8 a.m. The league runs January through April for boys and girls from U6 to U16, and the fee is $100 for residents or $125 for non-residents, with a full uniform included. Here's how to sign up, what you need, and other Wellington parks news families should know this month.

Details are from the Village of Wellington's Nov. 2, 2026 announcement. Check the village's online catalog for division schedules and any updates.

Who can play

The Spring Soccer League is a recreational league with divisions for boys and girls from U6 to U16. Your child's division is based on their age as of Jan. 1, 2027. For example, a player who is 9 on Jan. 1, 2027 would generally play in the U10 division, since "U10" means under 10 on the cutoff date. Check the division chart in the village's catalog to confirm your child's age group.

Wellington residents get first access: resident registration opened three days before non-resident registration, so popular divisions can fill early.

How much it costs

  • Wellington residents: $100 per player
  • Non-residents: $125 per player
  • Included: a full uniform

Families in Royal Palm Beach, Loxahatchee Groves, The Acreage and unincorporated Lake Worth can still play at the non-resident rate.

How to register, step by step

  1. Set up a CivicRec account first. Online registration requires an existing CivicRec account with Wellington Parks and Recreation. If you don't have one, create it before you try to register. Without an existing account, the village requires two forms of ID to establish proof of residency.
  2. Gather documents. First-time registrants need a copy of the player's birth certificate.
  3. Register online or in person. Sign up through the village's online recreation catalog at wellingtonfl.gov/CivicRec, or in person at the Parks and Recreation office at Village Park, 11700 Pierson Rd.
  4. Pay the fee and watch for your team assignment and practice details from the league.

Tip: If you're new to Wellington, set up your CivicRec account and residency proof early. The same account works for other village programs, camps, aquatics and tennis.

Where games are played

Village Park on Pierson Road is Wellington's largest park, at about 114 acres, with sports fields, gyms and a walking trail. It's also home to the Parks and Recreation office and the new Wellington Aquatics Center. Check your team's schedule for field locations.

Also in Wellington parks this month

  • Scott's Place Playground closed Nov. 16-20: The barrier-free playground at 12190 Forest Hill Blvd. closes for maintenance Monday, Nov. 16 through Friday, Nov. 20, and reopens when the work is done, weather permitting. Regular hours are Monday through Saturday 7:30 a.m. to 9 p.m. and Sunday 7:30 a.m. to 7 p.m.
  • NinjaCross closed Nov. 16-19: The NinjaCross obstacle course at the Wellington Aquatics Center, 3410 120th Ave. S, closes for routine maintenance Monday, Nov. 16 through Thursday, Nov. 19. The rest of the Aquatics Center keeps its regular hours. Questions: (561) 791-4770.
  • Free Learn to Play Pickleball clinics: Free beginner clinics for ages 10 and up run at the Greenbriar Park courts, 2975 Greenbriar Blvd., from 9 a.m. to noon on Dec. 5, Jan. 16, Feb. 13, March 13 and April 10. No equipment is needed, but pre-registration through CivicRec is required and space is limited. Open play follows on a first-come basis.
  • Thanksgiving hours: Many village facilities close Nov. 26-27. See our Thanksgiving in Wellington guide.

Other youth sports options in Wellington

Soccer isn't the only option this winter and spring. The village-run Wellington Tennis Center on Lyons Road has 21 clay courts and offers junior programs for kids from age 5, plus private and group lessons. The Wellington Aquatics Center at Village Park has a heated competition pool and recreation pool, and several local swim schools teach year round. For indoor activity on rainy days, see our list of the best gyms and fitness studios.

Wellington's Parks and Recreation department lists all youth leagues, camps and classes in its online catalog, so it's worth browsing once your CivicRec account is set up.
