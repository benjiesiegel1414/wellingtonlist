24-Hour Pharmacies Near Wellington, FL: Who's Open Latest

By Wellington List staffPublished October 9, 20264 min read

Need a prescription filled late at night in Wellington, FL? Here's the honest answer up front: there is currently no 24-hour pharmacy in Wellington. Below are the pharmacies that stay open latest, the nearest true 24-hour pharmacy, and what to do if you need medicine after everything closes.

Quick answer

24-hour pharmacy in Wellington?
None right now. No CVS, Walgreens or Publix pharmacy in Wellington, Royal Palm Beach or Lake Worth is open overnight.
Open latest in Wellington
Walgreens, 10420 Forest Hill Blvd: pharmacy until 10 p.m., store until midnight
Nearest 24-hour pharmacy
Walgreens, 21880 State Road 7, Boca Raton (about 20 miles south)
Medical emergency
Call 911 or go to the ER at Wellington Regional Medical Center

Nearest 24-hour pharmacy to Wellington

The closest pharmacy we found that's open around the clock is the Walgreens at 21880 State Road 7 in Boca Raton, (561) 470-0647, roughly a 30-minute drive south on State Road 7. Its pharmacy closes only from 1:30 to 2 a.m. Some stores, like the CVS at 2077 N Military Trail in West Palm Beach, are open 24 hours but their pharmacy counter isn't, so always check the pharmacy hours, not just the store hours.

Tips for getting medicine after hours

  • Ask for an emergency supply. Florida law lets a pharmacist dispense a limited emergency supply of many maintenance medications if your doctor can't be reached, so call the pharmacy first thing in the morning.
  • Transfer the prescription. If your doctor sent a prescription to a pharmacy that has closed, a late-night pharmacy can usually transfer it once both are open, so plan ahead before a weekend or holiday.
  • Urgent care can help until 9 p.m. Baptist Health Urgent Care Express on Forest Hill Blvd is open daily 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. and can write prescriptions you can fill at the Walgreens down the road before it closes.
  • For emergencies, go to the ER. Allergic reactions, chest pain, trouble breathing and overdoses need 911 or the ER, not a pharmacy.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a 24-hour pharmacy in Wellington, FL?

Not currently. No CVS, Walgreens or Publix pharmacy in Wellington, Royal Palm Beach or Lake Worth is open overnight. The nearest 24-hour pharmacy is the Walgreens at 21880 State Road 7 in Boca Raton.

What pharmacy is open latest in Wellington?

The Walgreens at 10420 Forest Hill Blvd has the latest pharmacy hours in Wellington, until 10 p.m., with the store open until midnight. Confirm current hours before you go.

What time does Publix pharmacy close in Wellington?

Wellington Publix pharmacies are open Monday to Friday 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., Saturday 9 to 7 and Sunday 11 to 6.
