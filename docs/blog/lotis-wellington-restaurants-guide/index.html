Lotis Wellington Restaurants: What's Open Now and What's Coming

By Wellington List staffPublished October 6, 2026Updated October 14, 20265 min read

Lotis Wellington is the new mixed-use neighborhood on State Road 7, just north of Wellington Regional Medical Center. Its commercial heart, The Square, was planned around a lineup of nine restaurants, and some of them are already serving. Others have signed leases but are not open yet. Here is where every Lotis restaurant stands, with addresses, hours and phone numbers for the ones you can visit today.

Lotis restaurants that are open now

Cooper's Hawk Winery and Restaurant

The anchor of the dining lineup. Cooper's Hawk pairs a big American menu with its own label wines, a tasting room and a retail wine shop. Wine O'Clock happy hour runs Monday to Friday from 3 to 5:30 p.m., and Brunch and Bubbles is served Saturday and Sunday from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.

Address: 1387 S State Road 7, Wellington, FL 33414 · Phone: (561) 922-9463
Hours: Mon to Thu 11 a.m. to 9 p.m., Fri 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., Sat 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., Sun 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.

Turning Point

Turning Point is a breakfast, brunch and lunch restaurant founded in New Jersey in 1998, and Wellington is its first Florida location. Think pancakes, French toast, omelets, Benedicts, salads and wraps in a bright, upscale-casual room. It closes at 3 p.m., so plan around that.

Address: 1385 S State Road 7, Wellington, FL 33414 · Phone: (561) 800-3180
Hours: Daily, 7:30 a.m. to 3 p.m.

MOOYAH Burgers, Fries & Shakes

MOOYAH is a fast-casual, build-your-own burger spot with hand-cut fries and hand-spun shakes. It was the chain's first Palm Beach County location. Turkey and veggie patties and lettuce wraps make it easy to customize.

Address: 1347 S State Road 7, Wellington, FL 33414 · Phone: (561) 404-5757

Kura Revolving Sushi Bar

Kura held its grand opening on April 10, 2026. Plates travel past your booth on a conveyor belt, and you order extras from a tablet. Kids love it, and it is one of the easiest family dinners at Lotis.

Address: 1349 S State Road 7, Bay 101, Wellington, FL 33414

Foxtail Coffee

Foxtail Coffee Co. is a Florida-born coffee shop with espresso drinks, cold brew and pastries, plus a Kelly's Homemade Ice Cream counter. There is plenty of indoor seating, so it doubles as a remote work spot. See our full list of the best coffee shops in Wellington.

Address: 1385 S State Road 7, Suite 105, Wellington, FL 33414 · Phone: (561) 971-3672
Hours: Daily, 6 a.m. to 9 p.m.

Signed for Lotis, but not open yet

As of mid-2026, these restaurants have signed for Lotis Wellington but are not yet serving. Local reporting describes them as signed tenants rather than open doors, so check each brand's official site before you drive over.

  • Lazy Dog Restaurant & Bar. Lodge-style American restaurant with a dog-friendly patio and a dog menu. Until Wellington opens, the closest Lazy Dog is at 9636 Glades Rd in Boca Raton.
  • Frank Pepe Pizzeria Napoletana. The famous New Haven coal-fired pizza brand. The nearest open locations are in Broward County.
  • Naked Taco. Casual Mexican with tacos and margaritas.
  • Seasons 52. Seasonal grill and wine bar known for its mini indulgence desserts. Construction got underway in fall 2026, according to local social media posts, with opening expected in mid to late 2027.
  • Mazza Mediterranean. Named in the original lineup; its current status is unconfirmed.
  • Cluck Face Nashville Hot Chicken. Fast-casual Nashville hot chicken tenders, never frozen and breaded in-house, served country fried or Nashville hot as meals, sliders or bowls. Coming to Unit 3-105 at 1347 S State Road 7; as of late September 2026 the local franchisees were targeting a mid-November opening, with planned hours of 11 a.m. to 1 a.m. daily.
  • Garden Butcher. A clean-eating cafe focused on local and organic ingredients, with permits filed in July 2026 at 1385 S State Road 7.

Why are some Lotis restaurants delayed?

The project has faced construction liens and a dispute among its development partners in 2026, which has slowed tenant buildouts. Five commercial buildings are complete, and the developer has said the project is moving forward. We will update this guide as each restaurant opens. Know of an opening date? Tell us.

Tips for visiting Lotis Wellington

  • Brunch crowds are real. Turning Point and Cooper's Hawk both get busy on weekend mornings, so arrive early.
  • Make it a night. Dinner at Cooper's Hawk or Kura, then dessert at the Kelly's ice cream counter inside Foxtail.
  • Season matters. From January through April, State Road 7 traffic and restaurant waits pick up with the winter equestrian crowds.

Frequently asked questions

What restaurants are open at Lotis Wellington?

As of fall 2026, Cooper's Hawk Winery and Restaurant, Turning Point, MOOYAH Burgers, Kura Revolving Sushi Bar and Foxtail Coffee are open at Lotis Wellington on State Road 7.

Is Lazy Dog open in Wellington, FL?

Not yet. Lazy Dog has signed for Lotis Wellington but was not open as of mid-2026. The closest open Lazy Dog is at 9636 Glades Rd in Boca Raton.

Where is Lotis Wellington?

Lotis Wellington is on the west side of State Road 7 in Wellington, FL 33414, just north of Wellington Regional Medical Center. Restaurant addresses include 1347, 1349, 1385 and 1387 S State Road 7.

What are Turning Point's hours in Wellington?

Turning Point at 1385 S State Road 7 is open daily from 7:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. for breakfast, brunch and lunch.

When does Cluck Face open in Wellington?

Cluck Face Nashville Hot Chicken is coming to The Square at Lotis Wellington at 1347 S State Road 7, Unit 3-105. As of late September 2026, the franchisee was targeting a mid-November 2026 opening, with planned hours of 11 a.m. to 1 a.m. daily.
