    Who to Call in Wellington, FL: Phone Numbers for Potholes, Water, Trash, Code and More

    By Ben SiegelPublished November 23, 2026Updated November 25, 20265 min read

    Pothole on your street? Water off at 2 a.m.? Barking dog, dead street light, missed trash pickup? In Wellington, Florida, the right number depends on the problem, and it isn't always the village. This is a one-page directory of who to call for what in Wellington, plus how to report most problems online in a couple of minutes through the village's SeeClickFix Wellington app.

Village Hall is at 12300 Forest Hill Blvd. and is open weekdays, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

    The fastest way: report it online

    For most non-emergency problems, the village points residents to SeeClickFix Wellington, a free app and website that replaced the earlier GoWellington app on Nov. 1, 2026. You pick a category, drop a pin or enter an address, add a photo if you have one, and submit. The village's Public Works FAQ says to use the portal for:

    • Potholes and broken or unsafe sidewalks
    • Street light outages
    • Missed trash, recycling, bulk or yard waste pickup
    • Requests for a new garbage, recycling or yard waste container
    • Dead animals in the road or right-of-way
    • Algae or weed growth in canals and lakes, and illegal dumping
    • Code violations and requests to join the mosquito do-not-spray list

    Get it free from the app stores or at wellingtonfl.gov/seeclickfix; see our SeeClickFix how-to. One thing to know: under a 2021 Florida law, code complaints can't be anonymous, so you'll need to give your name and address for those.

    Things the village doesn't handle

    • Power outages and downed lines: Florida Power & Light. Stay away from downed lines and call 911 if there's danger.
    • Schools: the School District of Palm Beach County runs Wellington's public schools.
    • Trees or damage on private property: the owner's or HOA's responsibility, even after a storm.
    • Private roads, gates and community lakes: your HOA or property manager.
    • Homes outside village limits with a Wellington mailing address: Palm Beach County government, not the village.

    Tips for getting a faster fix

    • Give an exact location: the nearest address or cross street, and which side of the road.
    • Add a photo in the portal. It helps crews bring the right equipment.
    • Save your request number so you can follow up.
    • Check who owns it first. If it's inside a gated community, start with the HOA.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I report a pothole or broken street light in Wellington, FL?

    Submit a request through SeeClickFix Wellington, the village's free service request app (wellingtonfl.gov/seeclickfix), or call Village Hall at 561-791-4000.

    Who do I call for a water emergency in Wellington?

    Call Wellington Utilities at 561-791-4010 on weekdays, or the after-hours emergency line at 561-791-4037. Homes east of State Road 7 call Palm Beach County Utilities at 561-740-4600.

    Who do I call about a barking dog in Wellington?

    Animal complaints go to Palm Beach County Animal Care and Control at 561-233-1200, not the village.

    What is the Wellington Code Compliance phone number?

    561-753-2560. Under Florida law, code complaints generally can't be anonymous.

    Does Wellington have its own police department?

    No. The Palm Beach County Sheriff's Office provides law enforcement in Wellington, and Palm Beach County Fire Rescue handles fire and medical calls. Call 911 in an emergency.
