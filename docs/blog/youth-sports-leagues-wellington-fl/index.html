    Youth Sports in Wellington, FL: Every League, Season and Sign-Up Date

    By Ben SiegelPublished November 14, 20265 min read

    Looking for youth sports in Wellington, Florida? Between the village's own recreation leagues and eight local travel clubs, Wellington kids can play baseball, basketball, soccer, flag football, softball, lacrosse, volleyball, roller hockey, rugby, wrestling and tackle football. The catch is that every sport has its own registration window, and some open months before the season starts. We compiled the village's league calendar, age ranges, travel clubs and adult leagues into one guide so you know when to sign up.

    Wellington youth sports at a glance

    Where to register
    Village recreation leagues: the CivicRec online catalog (wellingtonfl.gov/CivicRec) or the Village Park front desk
    Open now
    Spring 2027 soccer (ages 4-15) and girls softball (ages 4-14), both starting in January
    Coming next (December)
    Spring baseball, girls basketball and spring lacrosse registration
    Home base
    Village Park, 11700 Pierson Rd.
    Travel and club teams
    8 local sports providers, from Wellington Wave Soccer to Western Communities Football League
    Questions
    Parks and Recreation, (561) 791-4005; Athletics Division, (561) 791-4741

    League dates and ages are from the Village of Wellington's League Seasons, Athletic Leagues and Sports Providers pages, checked Nov. 14, 2026. Registration months are approximate, and the village notes that Little League baseball sign-ups happen on specific dates within each window.

    Tip: mark your calendar a month before each window. Popular divisions can fill, and Wellington residents often get first access. For example, resident registration for spring 2027 soccer opened Nov. 9, three days before non-residents.

    Spring 2027 soccer: open now

    The village's recreational Spring Soccer League runs January through April for boys and girls from U6 to U16, based on age as of Jan. 1, 2027. The fee is $100 for Wellington residents and $125 for non-residents, with a full uniform included. First-timers need a copy of the player's birth certificate and an existing CivicRec account. Full details are in our spring soccer registration guide.

    Year-round youth programs

    Beyond leagues, the village offers skills programs and classes through its recreation catalog. The current list includes:

    • Ball sports: Basketball Fundamentals, Goals 4 Girls Basketball, Futsal Soccer, Future Soccer Superstar Training, Soccer Shots, Lacrosse Training and Sand Volleyball Training
    • For the youngest athletes: KinderSports
    • Cheer and gymnastics: recreational team cheerleading and tumbling
    • Martial arts and combat sports: TaeKwonDo (Monday and Wednesday evenings at Village Park), HapKiDo self-defense and wrestling
    • Fitness: Confidence thru Fitness and Motivation

    Sports camps and academies run on school breaks and in summer, including basketball, soccer, cheer and tumbling, fishing, horseback riding, volleyball, speed and agility, tennis, rugby, lacrosse and World Cup soccer camps. Availability varies by season.

    Other ways for kids to play

    • Tennis: the Wellington Tennis Center runs Junior Aces drop-in clinics on Saturdays ($30, ages 5-18) and after-school clinics by skill level. See our pickleball and tennis guide.
    • Swimming: the Wellington Aquatics Center has a 28-lane competition pool, swim lessons and the NinjaCross obstacle course. See our Aquatics Center guide and swim lessons.
    • Pickleball: free Learn to Play clinics for ages 10 and up at Greenbriar Park on Dec. 5, Jan. 16, Feb. 13, March 13 and April 10.
    • Skateboarding: the Village Park skate park is open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. or sunset. Helmets are required, and skaters under 11 need someone 18 or older with them.
    • Rowing: the Florida Rowing Center at the Lake Wellington boathouse runs summer camps for ages 12 to 18.
    • Open play: pickup basketball, futsal, roller hockey and sand volleyball are available at village parks on a first come, first served basis. Organized team practices need a permit from the Athletics Division at (561) 791-4741.

    Adult leagues

    • Men's basketball (18+): a 10-game season plus playoffs at Village Park, Sundays 8 a.m. to noon, up to 24 teams of 10 players. Players register individually and give a team name.
    • Men's flag football (18+): spring league at Village Park, April through July, limited to the first 16 teams (8 to 15 players each).
    • Adult soccer: run with the Beaches Adult Soccer League at Greenview Shores Park, with men's, coed, 7v7 and 11v11 options. Register at basl.com.

    How to sign up

    1. Create a CivicRec account before registration opens. If you don't already have one, Wellington requires two forms of ID to set up residency for the resident rate.
    2. Gather documents. First-time youth players usually need a birth certificate copy.
    3. Register online at wellingtonfl.gov/CivicRec, under the Sports and Athletics tab, or in person at Village Park.
    4. For travel clubs, call the club directly. Tryout dates and fees are set by each club.

    Tips for Wellington sports families

    • Check field status on rainy days. If village fields are closed for weather, they're closed for all uses. The village posts updates on its Weather and Field Status page.
    • Plan for the heat. Fall seasons start in August and September, so pack water and shade.
    • Look at the school calendar. Our school choice guide covers choice programs at Wellington-area schools, including Wellington High's academies.
    • After the game: find team-friendly spots in our pizza and kid-friendly restaurants guides.

    Frequently asked questions

    When does Wellington spring soccer registration open?

    Spring soccer registration opens in November for a January start. For spring 2027, resident registration opened Nov. 9 and non-resident registration Nov. 12, 2026; ages 4 to 15.

    When is Wellington Little League baseball registration?

    The village lists December registration for the spring season starting in February and June registration for the fall season starting in September, for ages 4 to 16. Sign-ups happen on specific dates within those windows.

    How do I register for Wellington youth sports?

    Register through the village's CivicRec online catalog at wellingtonfl.gov/CivicRec or in person at Village Park, 11700 Pierson Rd. You need a CivicRec account, and first-time players usually need a birth certificate copy.

    What travel sports clubs are in Wellington?

    The village lists Wellington Colts travel baseball, Wellington Roller Hockey Association, Wellington Wave Soccer Club, Wellington Wizards Rugby Club, Wellington Wolfpack Lacrosse, Wellington Wolves Travel Basketball, Wellington Wrestling Club and Western Communities Football League.

    Does Wellington have adult sports leagues?

    Yes: a men's basketball league and a spring men's flag football league at Village Park, and adult soccer with the Beaches Adult Soccer League at Greenview Shores Park.
