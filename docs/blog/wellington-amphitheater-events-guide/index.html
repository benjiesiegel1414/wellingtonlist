Wellington Amphitheater Guide: Free Concerts, Food Trucks, Movies and Parking

By Wellington List staffPublished October 6, 20264 min read

The Wellington Amphitheater is the village's free outdoor stage at Wellington Town Center, and it is one of the best deals in Palm Beach County. Most weeks bring free tribute band concerts, food truck nights, movie nights and seasonal festivals on the lawn beside Lake Wellington. This guide covers what happens there, how to plan a night out, where to park and what to bring.

Wellington Amphitheater at a glance

Address
12100 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
Cost
Free admission and free parking for most village events
Typical times
Food trucks around 5 p.m., music starting between 6:30 and 7:30 p.m.
Capacity
About 1,500 people on the lawn
Seating
Bring your own lawn chairs or blankets
Event line
(561) 753-2484 for rain-outs and schedule changes

What happens at the Wellington Amphitheater

Free concerts and tribute bands

The amphitheater is best known for its free concert series. Expect tribute bands covering everyone from ABBA and the Eagles to Billy Joel, along with local bands, school performances and holiday concerts. The stage has a professional sound and lighting system, so these shows feel much bigger than the price tag suggests.

Food truck nights

Food trucks line up along the Town Center promenade before many concerts, often a dozen or more, with everything from tacos and burgers to desserts. Grab dinner when you arrive, then stake out a spot on the lawn before the music starts.

Movie nights

Family movie nights on the lawn are a regular part of the schedule, especially in the cooler months. They are a great low-cost night out with kids. Pair one with an afternoon at Scott's Place, the barrier-free playground right next door.

Lakeside Market and seasonal festivals

The seasonal Lakeside Market sets up along the Town Center promenade, and the amphitheater hosts village celebrations through the year, including holiday events, Fourth of July festivities and community festivals. Check our Wellington events calendar for upcoming dates.

How to find the current schedule

The Village of Wellington publishes the official amphitheater calendar on wellingtonfl.gov, and performers and dates change from season to season. Before heading out, check the village calendar or call the event line at (561) 753-2484, which also announces weather cancellations. Outdoor events are sometimes moved or canceled for summer storms.

Parking at the Wellington Amphitheater

  • Parking is free in the Town Center lots around the amphitheater, the Wellington Aquatics Complex and the village municipal complex.
  • Arrive about an hour early for popular concerts. The closest spaces fill quickly.
  • Do not park in the neighboring Publix lot. Visitors report towing and ticketing there on event nights.

What to bring

  • Lawn chairs or a blanket. Seating is on the lawn, and there are no fixed seats.
  • Cash or card for food trucks. Most trucks take cards, but lines move faster when you know what you want.
  • Bug spray and a light layer. Evenings by the lake can bring mosquitoes, and winter nights can get cool.
  • Leave your dog at home for concerts. Visitors note pets are kept off the main lawn.

Make a night of it

The amphitheater sits at Wellington Town Center, beside Lake Wellington. Before a show, walk the lakeside promenade, let the kids loose at Scott's Place or cool off at the Wellington Aquatics Complex next door. For dinner beforehand, the restaurants on Forest Hill Boulevard and in the Lotis Wellington dining area are a short drive away.

Frequently asked questions

Are Wellington Amphitheater concerts free?

Most village events at the Wellington Amphitheater, including the tribute band concert series, food truck nights and movie nights, are free, and parking is free.

Where is the Wellington Amphitheater?

The Wellington Amphitheater is at 12100 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414, at Wellington Town Center beside Lake Wellington and Scott's Place playground.

Where do I park for the Wellington Amphitheater?

Park for free in the Town Center lots near the amphitheater and aquatics complex. Avoid the neighboring Publix lot, where visitors report towing on event nights.

Can I bring chairs to the Wellington Amphitheater?

Yes. Seating is on the lawn, so bring lawn chairs or a blanket.

How do I find out if an amphitheater event is canceled?

Check the Village of Wellington calendar at wellingtonfl.gov or call the event line at (561) 753-2484 for rain-out updates.
