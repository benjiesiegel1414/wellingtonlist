24-Hour Emergency Vets Near Wellington, FL

When your dog eats something it shouldn't at midnight or your cat suddenly can't breathe, you need to know where to go fast. Here are the 24-hour emergency vets near Wellington, FL, plus pet urgent care for less serious problems and 24/7 emergency care for horses.

24-hour emergency vets near Wellington

ACCESS Specialty Animal Hospital (Royal Palm Beach)

The closest round-the-clock animal ER to most of Wellington, just north on Southern Boulevard. ACCESS has a 24/7 emergency team on standby plus specialists for surgery, internal medicine and critical care.

Address: 10465 Southern Blvd, Royal Palm Beach, FL 33411 · Phone: (561) 774-8855 · Hours: 24/7 emergency

VCA Palm Beach Veterinary Specialists (West Palm Beach)

VCA Palm Beach Veterinary Specialists is a 24-hour emergency and specialty hospital on Forest Hill Boulevard, a straight shot east from Wellington.

Address: 3884 Forest Hill Blvd, West Palm Beach, FL 33406 · Phone: (561) 434-5700 · Hours: Open 24 hours, 7 days

Veterinary Emergency Group (Boynton Beach)

VEG is a walk-in emergency-only animal hospital open 24/7, including holidays. It's farther away, but a solid option if others are busy.

Address: 2246 N Congress Ave, Boynton Beach, FL 33426 · Phone: (561) 752-3232 · Hours: Open 24/7

Pet urgent care and regular vets

For problems that can't wait for a regular appointment but aren't life-threatening, such as minor cuts, ear infections, vomiting without other symptoms or limping, try Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care at 11081 Southern Blvd, Suite 100, open daily 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., (561) 407-7766. Many Wellington vets also hold same-day sick slots; see our list of veterinarians in Wellington. Banfield Pet Hospital on Forest Hill Blvd is open seven days a week, including Sundays.

24/7 emergency care for horses

Wellington is horse country, and colic doesn't wait. Palm Beach Equine Clinic at 13125 Southfields Road provides emergency veterinary service 24 hours a day, seven days a week, at (561) 793-1599. Keep that number saved in your phone and posted in the barn.

When it's an emergency

Go to a 24-hour animal ER right away for:

  • Trouble breathing, choking or pale or blue gums
  • Collapse, seizures or inability to stand
  • A swollen, hard belly or unproductive retching, especially in large dogs
  • Eating something toxic: chocolate, grapes or raisins, xylitol gum, rat poison, medications, or lilies for cats
  • Hit by a car, a deep wound or bleeding that won't stop
  • A toad or snake encounter. Cane toads are common in South Florida; wipe your dog's gums with a damp cloth and head to the ER.
  • Heatstroke signs in Florida summers: heavy panting, drooling, weakness

Call ahead while you're driving so the team can prepare, and bring any packaging if your pet ate something.

More after-hours help: late-night pharmacies, emergency dentists, hospitals and urgent care and emergency plumbers in Wellington.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a 24-hour emergency vet near Wellington, FL?

Yes. ACCESS Specialty Animal Hospital at 10465 Southern Blvd in Royal Palm Beach is the closest 24/7 animal ER, (561) 774-8855. VCA Palm Beach Veterinary Specialists in West Palm Beach and VEG in Boynton Beach are also open 24 hours.

Is there pet urgent care in Wellington?

Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care at 11081 Southern Blvd is open daily 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. for non-life-threatening problems.

Who handles horse emergencies in Wellington?

Palm Beach Equine Clinic at 13125 Southfields Road offers emergency veterinary service 24 hours a day, seven days a week, at (561) 793-1599.
