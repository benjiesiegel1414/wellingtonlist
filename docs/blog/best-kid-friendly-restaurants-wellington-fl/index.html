11 Best Kid-Friendly Restaurants in Wellington, FL

By Ben SiegelPublished November 9, 20265 min read

Looking for a kid-friendly restaurant in Wellington, Florida? Some places here go well beyond a kids' menu: sushi that rides past your table on a conveyor belt, an open-air cafe with a play area, hibachi chefs cooking at your table, and a play cafe built for little ones. Here are 11 family-friendly restaurants in Wellington, with addresses, hours, kids deals and tips.

We picked these from the restaurants on Wellington List that make eating out with kids easier, whether that means a kids deal, a play area, entertainment at the table or room for a big group. Every spot below links to its full Wellington List listing. No business paid to be included, and the list is in no particular order of quality. Hours were last checked in November 2026 and can change, especially around holidays, so call ahead.

1. Kura Revolving Sushi Bar

Kura Revolving Sushi Bar at Lotis Wellington is a hit with kids: plates ride past the booth on a conveyor belt, you order extras from a touch panel, and robots bring the drinks. Returning empty plates through the table slot counts toward the Bikkura Pon prize game.

What to try: Plates off the belt, plus ramen or udon for picky eaters.

Tip: Reserve ahead through the Kura Sushi Rewards app or website, especially on weekends.

  • Address: 1349 S State Road 7, Bay 101, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 507-1171
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 11:30 a.m.-9 p.m.; Fri 11:30 a.m.-9:30 p.m.; Sat 11 a.m.-9:30 p.m.; Sun 11 a.m.-9 p.m.
  • See the full Kura Revolving Sushi Bar listing

2. Juan's Marketplace

Juan's Marketplace on Polo Club Road is an open-air cafe, bar and market with a backyard feel, covered outdoor seating and a play area for children. Dogs are welcome too.

What to try: Breakfast, lunch or dinner outdoors while the kids play.

Tip: Seating is outdoors, so it may close in bad weather. Check its social media before you go.

  • Address: 11198 Polo Club Rd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 922-5180
  • Hours: Daily 9 a.m.-9 p.m.
  • See the full Juan's Marketplace listing

3. Lindburgers

Lindburgers on Wellington Trace has roughly 50 gourmet burgers, plus wings, hot dogs and milkshakes. Kids eat free on Mondays with an adult meal.

What to try: A burger and milkshake on a Monday night.

Tip: The loyalty program gives double points on Tuesdays.

  • Address: 13860 Wellington Trace, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 753-0555
  • Hours: Sun-Wed 11 a.m.-9 p.m.; Thu 11 a.m.-9:30 p.m.; Fri-Sat 11 a.m.-10 p.m.
  • See the full Lindburgers listing

4. Saito's Japanese Steakhouse

Saito's Japanese Steakhouse near The Mall at Wellington Green has hibachi tables where chefs cook at your table, which makes it a favorite for kids' birthdays. There's also a sushi bar and a regular dining room.

What to try: Hibachi chicken or steak with fried rice.

Tip: A gratuity is added automatically, so check the bill before tipping.

  • Address: 10240 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 140, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 296-8888
  • See the full Saito's Japanese Steakhouse listing

5. Lemon Drop Play Cafe

Lemon Drop Play Cafe on State Road 7 is a play cafe for kids from birth to age 7, with separate enclosed play areas for babies and toddlers and for ages 2 to 7. The cafe side serves coffee, teas, lemonades and breakfast bites.

What to try: Coffee for you and open play for the little ones.

Tip: Open play is sold as all-day passes, and it hosts birthday parties.

  • Address: 4095 S State Road 7, Unit Q2, Wellington, FL 33449
  • Phone: (561) 906-9119
  • See the full Lemon Drop Play Cafe listing

6. Skillets

Skillets in the Courtyard Shops on Wellington Trace seats about 195, which makes a big family breakfast much easier. The menu covers skillets, pancakes, crepes and gluten-free pancakes.

What to try: Pancakes or crepes for the kids and a skillet for you.

Tip: Go early on weekends in season.

  • Address: 13860 Wellington Trace, Suite A10, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 660-8304
  • Hours: Daily 7 a.m.-2:30 p.m.
  • See the full Skillets listing

7. Keke's Breakfast Cafe

Keke's Breakfast Cafe at The Pointe at Wellington Green leans into pancakes, waffles and French toast, which makes it an easy breakfast pick for kids.

What to try: Stuffed pancakes or chicken and waffles.

  • Address: 10120 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 190, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 444-2075
  • Hours: Daily 7 a.m.-2:30 p.m.
  • See the full Keke's Breakfast Cafe listing

8. Cooper's Hawk Winery and Restaurant

Cooper's Hawk at Lotis Wellington is a dinner the whole family can enjoy, with a kids' menu alongside steaks, pasta and burgers, plus weekend brunch.

What to try: Something from the kids' menu, and a scratch-kitchen entree for the adults.

Tip: The private dining room seats up to 48 for family celebrations.

  • Address: 1387 S State Road 7, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 922-9463
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 11 a.m.-9 p.m.; Fri 11 a.m.-10 p.m.; Sat 10 a.m.-10 p.m.; Sun 10 a.m.-9 p.m.
  • See the full Cooper's Hawk Winery and Restaurant listing

9. La Granja

La Granja on Forest Hill Blvd is part of a Florida chain founded in 1993, known for Peruvian-style rotisserie chicken. The casual setting suits families and groups, and family meals make takeout easy.

What to try: A rotisserie chicken family meal.

  • Address: 10590 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 383-8741
  • Hours: Mon-Sat 11 a.m.-10 p.m.; Sun 11 a.m.-9 p.m.
  • See the full La Granja listing

10. Chipotle Mexican Grill

Chipotle on State Road 7 is a fast, familiar option with kid's meals and family-size meals.

What to try: A kid's meal of tacos or a quesadilla.

Tip: Order ahead online to skip the line.

  • Address: 1000 S State Road 7, Suite 2, Wellington, FL
  • See the full Chipotle Mexican Grill listing

11. Denny's Royal Palm Beach

The Denny's on Civic Center Way in Royal Palm Beach is open 24 hours a day and set up for families, kids and groups, with breakfast served all day.

What to try: Pancakes at any hour.

Tip: Walk-ins are welcome.

  • Address: 300 Civic Center Way, Royal Palm Beach, FL 33411
  • Phone: (561) 793-7050
  • Hours: Open 24 hours, 7 days
  • See the full Denny's Royal Palm Beach listing

Tips for eating out with kids in Wellington

  • Go early. Dinner before 6 p.m. usually means shorter waits and a calmer dining room, especially in season.
  • Plan a deal night. Kids eat free at Lindburgers on Mondays with an adult meal.
  • Birthday ideas: hibachi at Saito's, a party at Lemon Drop Play Cafe, or a custom cake from one of the bakeries in our desserts guide.
  • Make a day of it: pair a meal with one of the things to do with kids in Wellington, like the Aquatics Center or Kids Empire at the mall.

Frequently asked questions

Which Wellington restaurants are best for kids?

Kura Revolving Sushi Bar at Lotis has a conveyor belt and a prize game kids love, Juan's Marketplace on Polo Club Road has a children's play area, and Saito's Japanese Steakhouse has hibachi tables where chefs cook at your table.

Where do kids eat free in Wellington, FL?

Lindburgers on Wellington Trace offers kids eat free on Mondays with the purchase of an adult meal.

Is there a restaurant with a play area in Wellington?

Yes. Juan's Marketplace on Polo Club Road has a play area for children and covered outdoor seating. For babies to age 7, Lemon Drop Play Cafe on State Road 7 combines a cafe with enclosed play areas.

Where can we have a kid's birthday dinner in Wellington?

Saito's Japanese Steakhouse near The Mall at Wellington Green is a popular pick because chefs cook at hibachi tables. Lemon Drop Play Cafe hosts birthday parties for younger kids.
