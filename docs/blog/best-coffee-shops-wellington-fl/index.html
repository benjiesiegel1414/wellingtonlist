The Best Coffee Shops in Wellington, FL

By Wellington List staffPublished October 4, 2026Updated October 8, 20266 min read

Looking for the best coffee shops in Wellington, Florida? Whether you want beans roasted that morning, a quiet table to work from or a quick latte before the school run, Wellington has more good coffee than most people realize. Here are the best coffee shops and cafes in Wellington, with addresses, hours and what to order, from local roasters to reliable chains.

Local coffee shops in Wellington

Candid Coffee & Anna Bakes

Two local businesses share one space at The Pointe at Wellington Green. Owners Bryan and Megan Jenkins started Candid Coffee roasting in Palm Beach County's warehouse district and selling at green markets; now they roast on site. Expect single-origin coffees like a Guatemalan with chocolate and almond notes, plus monthly rotating cold foam flavors. Johnson & Wales-trained baker Anna Ross handles the food: quiche by the slice, everything scones, feta pesto danishes, birthday cupcakes and gluten-free and vegan options. There is even a kids' latte.

Address: 10120 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 160, Wellington, FL 33414 · Phone: (561) 766-1742

Carmela Toast & Coffee Bar

Carmela is a South Florida cafe chain that began in Parkland in 2018 and brews coffee from its own farm in Costa Rica. The Wellington location in Wellington Green Square, next to Publix, leans into brunch with eight kinds of avocado toast (the Caprese is the best-seller), bowls, salads and flatbreads. Not a coffee person? Try the coconut lemonade.

Address: 10630 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 60, Wellington, FL 33414 · Phone: (561) 729-0522
Hours: Weekdays 7 a.m. to 4 p.m., weekends 8 a.m. to 3 p.m.

Foxtail Coffee

Florida-born Foxtail Coffee Co. anchors the coffee scene at Lotis Wellington on State Road 7, with espresso drinks, cold brew, seasonal lattes and pastries. It has the longest hours in town and plenty of indoor seating, plus a Kelly's Homemade Ice Cream counter inside. See what else is open nearby in our Lotis Wellington restaurant guide.

Address: 1385 S State Road 7, Suite 105, Wellington, FL 33414 · Phone: (561) 971-3672
Hours: Daily, 6 a.m. to 9 p.m.

Cumbres Coffee Roasters

Cumbres is a Colombian coffee roaster at The Pointe at Wellington Green that sells freshly roasted beans and serves cafe drinks. It's the place to stock up on beans for brewing at home.

Address: 10120 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414

Premier Cafe and Roasters

On the State Road 7 side of town at Wellington Reserve, Premier Cafe and Roasters is a reliable stop for everyday espresso drinks and cafe food.

Address: 1037 S State Road 7, Wellington, FL 33414

Taylor Made Cafe

Near the equestrian district on South Shore Boulevard, Taylor Made Cafe pairs coffee and espresso drinks with breakfast, sandwiches and comfort food. It's a handy stop before or after a day at the horse shows.

Address: 12160 S Shore Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414

Lemon Drop Play Cafe

Parents of little ones love Lemon Drop Play Cafe, which serves coffee and snacks next to an indoor play space. It's a lifesaver on rainy summer afternoons. For more ideas, see things to do with kids in Wellington.

Address: 4095 S State Road 7, Wellington, FL 33449

Reliable chains in Wellington

  • Starbucks, 2891 S State Road 7, Suite 100, plus other locations around the village.
  • Dunkin', 13501 S Shore Blvd, handy on show days near Wellington International.
  • Panera Bread, 10540 Forest Hill Blvd, with plenty of seating, Wi-Fi and food for longer work sessions.

Best coffee shops for working remotely

For a laptop session, Foxtail Coffee at Lotis has the longest hours and roomy seating, and Panera on Forest Hill Boulevard has Wi-Fi and plenty of tables. Weekday mornings are calmest. Avoid peak brunch hours on weekends, and in season (January through April), expect every coffee shop near the showgrounds to be busy on show days.

Tips for coffee in Wellington

  • Go early on weekends. Popular local spots get crowded by mid-morning.
  • Pair coffee with a walk. Grab a cup and head to the Town Center promenade along Lake Wellington.
  • Buy beans local. Candid and Cumbres both roast their own, and fresh beans taste best within a couple of weeks of roasting.
  • Check hours. Small cafes change hours seasonally, so call ahead if you're going late in the day.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best coffee shops in Wellington, FL?

Local favorites include Candid Coffee & Anna Bakes and Cumbres Coffee Roasters at The Pointe at Wellington Green, Carmela Toast & Coffee Bar in Wellington Green Square and Foxtail Coffee at Lotis Wellington.

Is there a coffee roaster in Wellington, FL?

Yes. Candid Coffee roasts its beans on site at 10120 Forest Hill Blvd, and Cumbres Coffee Roasters sells freshly roasted Colombian coffee at The Pointe at Wellington Green.

Which Wellington coffee shop is open latest?

Foxtail Coffee at 1385 S State Road 7 is open daily from 6 a.m. to 9 p.m., the longest hours of any local coffee shop.

Where can I work remotely with coffee in Wellington?

Foxtail Coffee at Lotis and Panera Bread on Forest Hill Blvd have the most seating and longest hours. Weekday mornings are quietest.
