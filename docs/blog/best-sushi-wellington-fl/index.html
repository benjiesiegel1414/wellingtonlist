10 Best Sushi and Japanese Restaurants in Wellington, FL

By Ben SiegelPublished November 6, 20265 min read

Looking for the best sushi in Wellington, Florida? You have more choices than you might think, from conveyor-belt sushi at Lotis and hibachi tables near the mall to all-you-can-eat rolls and small family-run Thai and Japanese kitchens. Here are 10 sushi and Japanese restaurants in Wellington, with addresses, hours and what to order.

We picked these restaurants from the sushi and Japanese places on Wellington List, covering different styles and price points around the village. Every spot below links to its full Wellington List listing. No business paid to be included, and the list is in no particular order of quality. Hours were last checked in November 2026 and can change, especially around holidays, so call ahead.

1. Kura Revolving Sushi Bar

Kura Revolving Sushi Bar opened at Lotis Wellington in April 2026. Plates travel past each booth on a two-level conveyor belt, you order extras from a touch panel, and robots deliver drinks. Sushi plates are a flat $3.90, and the menu has more than 100 items.

What to try: Pick plates off the belt, then try the ramen or udon. Kids love the Bikkura Pon prize game, which you play by returning empty plates through the table slot.

Tip: Reserve up to a week ahead through the Kura Sushi Rewards app or website.

  • Address: 1349 S State Road 7, Bay 101, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 507-1171
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 11:30 a.m.-9 p.m.; Fri 11:30 a.m.-9:30 p.m.; Sat 11 a.m.-9:30 p.m.; Sun 11 a.m.-9 p.m.
  • See the full Kura Revolving Sushi Bar listing

2. Saito's Japanese Steakhouse

Saito's Japanese Steakhouse on Forest Hill Blvd near The Mall at Wellington Green has hibachi tables where chefs cook at your table, plus a regular dining room and a sushi bar. It's a go-to for birthdays and group dinners.

What to try: Hibachi chicken, filet mignon or lobster; or tempura, katsu and sukiyaki from the kitchen menu.

Tip: A gratuity is added to bills automatically, so check your receipt before tipping.

  • Address: 10240 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 140, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 296-8888
  • See the full Saito's Japanese Steakhouse listing

3. Sushi Yama

Sushi Yama on Forest Hill Blvd offers all-you-can-eat sushi as well as a la carte ordering, along with a hibachi grill and other hot kitchen dishes.

What to try: All-you-can-eat sushi if you're hungry; a la carte if you just want a few rolls.

Tip: The full menu is posted on the restaurant's website.

  • Address: 10260 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 720-2091
  • See the full Sushi Yama listing

4. Kabuki Sushi Thai Tapas

Kabuki on State Road 7 is one of four Kabuki locations in Palm Beach County. The menu pairs sushi rolls, sashimi and hand rolls with Thai dishes and small plates, and it has a bar with a happy hour plus outdoor tables.

What to try: Specialty rolls with pad thai or tom kha soup, and dumplings or shumai to share.

Tip: Last seating is 30 minutes before closing, and the last delivery order is at 9 p.m.

  • Address: 2465 S State Road 7, Suite 100, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 323-4888
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 11:30 a.m.-10 p.m.; Fri-Sat 11:30 a.m.-11 p.m.; Sun 11:30 a.m.-10 p.m.
  • See the full Kabuki Sushi Thai Tapas listing

5. Fujisan Asian Bistro

Fujisan Asian Bistro is in the Forest Hill Blvd plaza at South Shore Blvd. It combines a sushi bar with Asian fusion kitchen dishes and offers sushi and sashimi platters for two.

What to try: Crispy rice with seared tuna, a dragon roll, gyoza and shrimp tempura.

Tip: It opens only for dinner on Mondays and Tuesdays, so lunch specials run Wednesday through Sunday.

  • Address: 11924 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 33, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 513-8888
  • Hours: Mon-Tue 4:30-9:30 p.m.; Wed-Thu 11:30 a.m.-9:30 p.m.; Fri 11:30 a.m.-10:30 p.m.; Sat noon-10:30 p.m.; Sun noon-9:30 p.m.
  • See the full Fujisan Asian Bistro listing

6. Lemongrass Asian Bistro

Lemongrass Asian Bistro at The Mall at Wellington Green pairs a full sushi bar with Thai dishes, ramen, wok stir-fries and rice bowls. Large combination platters work well for groups.

What to try: Sushi rolls with pad Thai or a curry, or a ramen bowl.

Tip: Happy hour runs daily from 3 to 6 p.m., and reservations are on OpenTable.

  • Address: 10312 Forest Hill Blvd, Suite 202, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 459-1598
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 11:30 a.m.-9 p.m.; Fri 11:30 a.m.-10 p.m.; Sat noon-10 p.m.; Sun noon-9 p.m.
  • See the full Lemongrass Asian Bistro listing

7. Sushi Moto Asian

Sushi Moto Asian on South Shore Blvd covers sushi, sashimi, hand rolls and specialty rolls, plus fried rice, soups and appetizers. It runs lunch and dinner specials and makes party platters.

What to try: A specialty roll and hand rolls, or a party platter for a get-together.

Tip: It's closed on Sundays.

  • Address: 12020 S Shore Blvd, Suite 700, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 792-9002
  • Hours: Mon-Thu 11 a.m.-9 p.m.; Fri-Sat 11 a.m.-10 p.m.; Sun closed
  • See the full Sushi Moto Asian listing

8. Mochi Thai and Japanese Restaurant

Mochi Thai and Japanese on Forest Hill Blvd is a small, family-run restaurant with a Thai side and a Japanese side to the menu. The dining room is compact, so takeout and curbside pickup are popular.

What to try: Sushi with a curry or drunken noodles, and the tempura cheesecake for dessert.

Tip: It's closed Tuesdays and closes between lunch and dinner on other days.

  • Address: 9152 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 469-6221
  • Hours: Mon, Wed-Sat 11:30 a.m.-3 p.m. and 5-9:30 p.m.; Sun 4-9:30 p.m.; Tue closed
  • See the full Mochi Thai and Japanese Restaurant listing

9. Sushi Fans Cafe

Sushi Fans Cafe is a casual sushi bar in the Wellington Marketplace plaza on Wellington Trace. It serves classic rolls and other Japanese and Asian dishes, has gluten-free options and adds brunch on Sundays.

What to try: California, spicy tuna and Philadelphia rolls.

  • Address: 13873 Wellington Trace, Suite B10, Wellington, FL 33414
  • See the full Sushi Fans Cafe listing

10. The Poke Company

If you want raw fish without a sit-down meal, The Poke Company on Forest Hill Blvd builds Hawaiian-style poke bowls.

What to try: A build-your-own poke bowl.

  • Address: 10120 Forest Hill Blvd, Wellington, FL 33414
  • Phone: (561) 323-2391
  • Hours: Sun-Thu 11 a.m.-9 p.m.; Fri-Sat 11 a.m.-10 p.m.
  • See the full The Poke Company listing

Tips for sushi night in Wellington

  • Hibachi for a celebration. Hibachi tables seat groups together and fill up on weekends, so call ahead for birthdays.
  • Lunch specials save money. Several places, including Fujisan and Sushi Moto, run lunch specials on weekdays.
  • Mixed groups. Kabuki, Lemongrass and Mochi all pair sushi with Thai dishes, which helps when not everyone wants raw fish.
  • More dinner ideas: see our guide to restaurants at Lotis Wellington or browse all Wellington restaurants.

Frequently asked questions

Is there conveyor belt sushi in Wellington, FL?

Yes. Kura Revolving Sushi Bar opened at Lotis Wellington, 1349 S State Road 7, in April 2026. Plates travel on a conveyor belt, extra items are ordered from a touch panel, and sushi plates are a flat $3.90.

Where is all-you-can-eat sushi in Wellington?

Sushi Yama on Forest Hill Blvd offers all-you-can-eat sushi along with a la carte ordering and a hibachi grill.

Where can I get hibachi in Wellington, FL?

Saito's Japanese Steakhouse at 10240 Forest Hill Blvd near The Mall at Wellington Green has hibachi tables, a sushi bar and a regular dining room. Sushi Yama also has a hibachi grill.

Which Wellington sushi restaurants also serve Thai food?

Kabuki Sushi Thai Tapas on State Road 7, Lemongrass Asian Bistro at the mall and Mochi Thai and Japanese on Forest Hill Blvd all serve sushi alongside Thai dishes like pad thai and curries.
