    Free and Low-Cost Classes for Seniors and Adults in Wellington, FL

    By Ben SiegelPublished November 15, 20264 min read

    Wellington offers a long list of free and low-cost classes for seniors and adults, many of them at the Wellington Community Center on Lake Wellington. Tai chi, the Arthritis Foundation's Walk With Ease program, an AARP Smart Driver course, technology help in three languages and monthly senior luncheons are all free, and fitness and dance classes like Zumba Gold and Strong in 30 cost $20 to $60 per session. We went through the village's online recreation catalog and compiled every adult and senior class, with prices, into one guide.

    Senior and adult classes at a glance

    Free programs
    Tai Chi, Walk With Ease, AARP Smart Driver, Technology Class, Feel Good Fridays, Lunch and Learn, Senior Special Events
    Fitness and dance
    Strong in 30, Zumba Gold, Zumba A.M., Zumba Step, Zumba Toning, line dancing and ballroom
    Typical cost
    Free to $60 per session
    Monthly senior events
    For ages 50+, 11:30 a.m. in the Community Center's Grande Ballroom, with free lunch and music

    Many at the Wellington Community Center, 12150 Forest Hill Blvd.
    Register
    wellingtonfl.gov/CivicRec, or call (561) 753-2484

    Class details and prices are from the Village of Wellington's online recreation catalog, checked Nov. 15, 2026. Sessions rotate, so check the catalog for current dates and times.

    Free programs for seniors

    Senior Special Events (ages 50+)

    Wellington's Community Services senior services team hosts monthly special events for residents 50 and older. Every event starts at 11:30 a.m. in the Grande Ballroom of the Wellington Community Center and includes a free lunch, music and more, according to the catalog. Registration is through the catalog.

    Lunch and Learn

    Free educational seminars on health and wellness topics. The catalog lists a series of sessions, so check for upcoming topics.

    Feel Good Fridays

    A free class for seniors provided by Baptist Health South Florida.

    Tai Chi

    A gentle form of physical activity the village recommends for seniors with uncertain balance or a fear of falling. Free.

    Walk With Ease

    The Arthritis Foundation's community program centers on walking, but also includes health education, stretching and strengthening exercises and motivation strategies. Free. Pair it with the 2.5-mile trail at Village Park; see our walking and biking trails guide.

    AARP Smart Driver Course

    For ages 50 and up, the updated Smart Driver course uses an easy-to-follow format with large print, full-color pages and videos. Completing it may qualify you for a car insurance discount, so check with your insurer. Free through the village.

    Technology Class

    TDM Technologies teaches seniors how to use everyday technology, with classes in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. Free.

    Fitness classes

    Strong in 30

    Low-impact strength training with seated and standing modifications to build lean muscle and strengthen bones. The village calls it great for seniors and beginners. $20 to $40 per session.

    Zumba classes

    • Zumba Gold ($40): basic Latin rhythms and simple steps for strength and flexibility, a lower-intensity version of Zumba.
    • Zumba A.M. ($40-$50): an hour of Latin-inspired dance fitness, great for beginners.
    • Zumba Step ($30): step aerobics combined with Zumba dancing.
    • Zumba Toning ($40): Zumba with light weights and toning sticks.

    Prefer free? The Mall at Wellington Green hosts a free Zumba class every Saturday from 10 to 11 a.m. in its District Studio, according to the mall.

    Dance classes

    • Fair Breeze Dancing Feet Line Dancing ($30-$50): a high-energy two-hour line dance class for all levels, with music from R&B and pop to country, blues, Caribbean and Afrobeats. No partner or experience needed; just bring sneakers.
    • Dancing with Lucie ($25-$60): beginner ballroom and line dance steps for social events and parties. Ballroom requires a partner; line dance doesn't.

    Other adult classes

    • Happy Day Creations cookie decorating ($20): 90-minute, beginner-friendly classes using royal icing, with all supplies included and 4 to 5 cookies to take home. Themes change monthly. The catalog lists a seniors' session on Friday, Dec. 11, 9 to 10:30 a.m., and a parent-and-child session on Saturday, Dec. 12, 10 to 11:30 a.m., at the Community Center.
    • Dog Obedience ($40): for dogs from 5 to 150 pounds. More in our dog-friendly Wellington guide.
    • Sports: adult leagues include men's basketball, men's flag football and adult soccer, plus open play pickleball and tennis. See our sports leagues guide and pickleball and tennis guide.
    • Water aerobics: the Wellington Aquatics Center sells a water aerobics pass.

    Where classes meet

    Many adult and senior programs, including the monthly senior events and cookie classes, are held at the Wellington Community Center, 12150 Forest Hill Blvd., on Lake Wellington. It's open Monday through Thursday 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m. to 1 p.m., and is closed Sundays. It closes Thanksgiving Day and the day after. Phone: (561) 753-2484.

    The Wellington Branch Library on Royal Fern Drive also offers free adult programs with a library card.

    How to sign up

    1. Create a CivicRec account at wellingtonfl.gov/CivicRec. Proof of residency gets resident rates where they apply.
    2. Browse the catalog by category (Senior Programs, Fitness and Wellness, Dance, Culinary Arts) or filter by the Adult or Senior age group.
    3. Register for free programs too. Even free classes and senior luncheons usually require sign-up so staff can plan.
    4. Need help? Call the Community Center at (561) 753-2484.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does Wellington have free classes for seniors?

    Yes. Tai Chi, the Arthritis Foundation's Walk With Ease, the AARP Smart Driver course, a technology class, Feel Good Fridays, Lunch and Learn seminars and monthly senior special events are listed as free in the village's catalog.

    What are Wellington's senior special events?

    Monthly events for residents 50 and older at 11:30 a.m. in the Wellington Community Center's Grande Ballroom, with free lunch and music. Register through the village catalog.

    Where is the AARP Smart Driver course in Wellington?

    The village lists the AARP Smart Driver course for ages 50 and up as a free program in its online recreation catalog. Completing it may qualify you for an insurance discount.

    How much are Zumba classes in Wellington?

    The village lists Zumba Gold at $40, Zumba A.M. at $40-$50, Zumba Step at $30 and Zumba Toning at $40 per session.
