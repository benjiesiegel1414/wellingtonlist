Schools in Wellington, FL: A Parent's Guide to Public, Charter and Private Options

By Wellington List staffPublished October 3, 20266 min read

Wellington has a reputation for strong schools, and it is one of the biggest reasons families move here. But between public, charter, magnet and private options, plus Florida's school choice scholarships, figuring out where your child can actually go is confusing. This guide lays out every type of school serving Wellington, Florida, how enrollment works, and what to look at when you compare.

Public schools in Wellington

Wellington's public schools are part of the School District of Palm Beach County, one of the largest districts in the country. Several Wellington schools consistently post some of the strongest results in the county, which is a big part of why demand for homes in their attendance zones stays high.

Elementary schools (PreK or K through 5)

  • Binks Forest Elementary, 15101 Bent Creek Rd
  • Elbridge Gale Elementary, 1915 Royal Fern Dr
  • Equestrian Trails Elementary, 9720 Stribling Way
  • New Horizons Elementary, 13900 Greenbriar Blvd
  • Panther Run Elementary, 10775 Lake Worth Rd
  • Wellington Elementary, 13000 Paddock Dr

Binks Forest and Equestrian Trails are frequently cited among the top-rated elementary schools in the area, and Wellington Elementary hosts a gifted program.

Middle schools (6 through 8)

  • Emerald Cove Middle, 9950 Stribling Way
  • Polo Park Middle, 11901 Lake Worth Rd
  • Wellington Landings Middle, 1100 Aero Club Dr

High schools (9 through 12)

  • Wellington High School, 2101 Greenview Shores Blvd. One of the better-ranked public high schools in the state, with strong academies, athletics and a long-running debate program.
  • Palm Beach Central High School, 8499 Forest Hill Blvd. Serves the eastern side of Wellington and surrounding communities.

How public school assignment works

In Palm Beach County, your home address determines your zoned elementary, middle and high school. Boundaries do not always follow neighborhood names, and two homes in the same subdivision can occasionally feed different schools, so always check a specific address with the district's school boundary locator before you buy or rent. Boundaries can also change when new schools open or enrollment shifts.

Beyond your zoned school, the district runs choice programs (often called magnet or academy programs) in areas like STEM, the arts, medicine, engineering and law. Students apply for these, and some have entrance criteria or lotteries. Applications usually open in the fall for the following school year, so if you are interested, start looking early.

Charter schools

Somerset Academy Wellington operates a K-8 school and a high school on Wellington Trace. Charter schools are public and tuition-free, but they run independently of the district and admit students by application, usually with a lottery when there are more applicants than seats. They often have waitlists, so apply as early as possible.

Private schools in and near Wellington

Inside the village, private options tend to be smaller schools, including St. David's Episcopal School on Forest Hill Blvd for the youngest grades, Wellington Collegiate Academy, The Wellington Preparatory School on Lake Worth Road, and Paolo Prep Academy, along with several Montessori and specialized programs.

Many Wellington families also commute to larger independent and religious schools in the area, including The King's Academy and Berean Christian School in West Palm Beach, and Rosarian Academy, a Catholic school in West Palm Beach for students through 8th grade. Tuition, class sizes and admissions vary widely, so visit in person and ask about the total cost, not just tuition.

Looking further ahead, Wingrove Academy, a nonprofit private PreK-12 school backed by Stephen Ross, is planned for the back of the K-Park property off Stribling Way, with a first phase targeted for August 2028 and need-based scholarships for Wellington residents.

Florida school choice scholarships

Florida expanded its school choice program in 2023 so that every K-12 student in the state can apply for a state-funded scholarship that can be put toward private school tuition and other approved education expenses. Award amounts vary, priority goes to lower-income families, and the application process runs through the state's scholarship funding organizations. If private school is on your list, check current eligibility and amounts before ruling it out on cost.

How to compare schools

  • Start with your address. Confirm your zoned schools with the district before anything else.
  • Look at more than one rating. State school grades, test scores and parent-review sites each tell part of the story.
  • Visit. Tours and open houses tell you more than any ranking, especially for private and charter schools.
  • Mind the deadlines. Choice programs, charter lotteries and private admissions usually close months before the school year starts.
  • Think about the commute. Drop-off traffic on Forest Hill Blvd, State Road 7 and Lake Worth Road is real, especially during the winter season.

Frequently asked questions

What public high schools serve Wellington, FL?

Wellington High School on Greenview Shores Blvd and Palm Beach Central High School on Forest Hill Blvd are the two public high schools serving Wellington, along with Somerset Academy Wellington, a charter high school.

How do I find out which school my Wellington home is zoned for?

Your home address determines your zoned public schools in Palm Beach County. Use the School District of Palm Beach County's school boundary locator to check a specific address.

Are there private schools in Wellington, FL?

Yes. Wellington has several smaller private schools, and many families also attend larger private schools nearby such as The King's Academy and Berean Christian School in West Palm Beach. Wingrove Academy, a private PreK-12 school, plans to open in Wellington in 2028.

Can I get a scholarship for private school in Florida?

Florida's school choice program lets every K-12 student apply for a state-funded scholarship that can be used toward private school tuition. Amounts vary and lower-income families get priority.
