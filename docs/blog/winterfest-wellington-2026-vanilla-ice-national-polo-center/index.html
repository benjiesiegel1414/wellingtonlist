    Winterfest 2026 in Wellington: Vanilla Ice, Free Parking, Shuttles and What to Know

    By Ben SiegelPublished November 26, 20265 min read

    One of Wellington's biggest nights of the year is back. The Greater Wellington Chamber of Commerce's 17th annual Winterfest with Vanilla Ice is Friday, Dec. 4, 2026, at the National Polo Center, with doors opening at 5 p.m. Admission and parking are free. Here's the lineup, where to park and catch the free shuttle, what's there for kids, what the VIP packages cost, and tips for a smoother night.

    Winterfest 2026 at a glance

    When
    Friday, Dec. 4, 2026; doors open at 5 p.m.
    Where
    National Polo Center, 3667 120th Ave. S., Wellington
    Admission
    Free
    Parking
    Free at Village Park and the Mall at Wellington Green, with free shuttles to the event
    Headliner
    Vanilla Ice (grand finale)
    VIP options
    "Ice Ice Experience" family night, $550 for up to six guests; Winterfest Holiday Lounge, $175 per person
    Host
    Greater Wellington Chamber of Commerce, wellingtonchamber.com

    Information is from the Town-Crier (Nov. 26, 2026) and the Greater Wellington Chamber of Commerce's 2026 Winterfest event materials, checked Nov. 26, 2026. Times and performers can change, so check the chamber's website before you go.

    What Winterfest is

    Winterfest is the chamber's holiday kickoff party, and it has grown into one of Wellington's largest events. The chamber says last year's Winterfest drew more than 10,000 people, its highest turnout to date, with a standing-room-only crowd for Vanilla Ice. Santa Claus is part of the show, too.

    "The event brings people together, creates memories and gives a community something to look forward to year after year," chamber Executive Director Michela Green told the Town-Crier. "Winterfest has become so much more than an event. It's a celebration of our community, our families, our businesses and the incredible spirit that makes Wellington special."

    The 2026 lineup

    According to the Town-Crier, the music runs in this order:

    PerformerWhat to expect
    Tom Blake and Gia MarieClassic country with a twist, to open the night
    DJ Johnny QuestA mix of songs spanning generations and genres
    Living the 80s Band'80s hits
    Michaela PaigeSinger-songwriter, back by popular demand
    Digital VibezInteractive dance performances
    Vanilla IceThe grand finale

    Exact set times hadn't been published as of Nov. 26. Since Vanilla Ice closes the show, plan to stay late if he's the reason you're going.

    For families and kids

    The chamber bills Winterfest as a family night. Its event materials describe:

    • A snowy "stables village" for kids and family activities
    • Holiday lights and a luxury car show
    • Seating on the polo field, up front by the stage
    • Food and drinks for every taste, from treats for kids to favorites for adults
    • Local business booths from chamber members and sponsors

    Food and drinks are sold on site, so bring a card or cash.

    Parking and the free shuttle

    Parking is free at Village Park (11700 Pierson Road) and at the Mall at Wellington Green, with complimentary shuttles to and from the polo grounds, the Town-Crier reported. With 10,000 people expected, the shuttle is usually the easiest way in and out.

    OptionAddressGood for
    Village Park11700 Pierson RoadClosest shuttle lot to the polo grounds
    Mall at Wellington Green10300 W. Forest Hill Blvd.Easy to reach from State Road 7; dinner or shopping before the show. See our mall holiday hours
    VIP parkingOn siteIncluded with some sponsor and VIP packages

    Expect heavier traffic on 120th Avenue South and Pierson Road from late afternoon. Give yourself extra time, and if you live nearby, it may be easier to stay off those roads that evening.

    VIP packages

    • Ice Ice Experience: a VIP family night for up to six guests, $550.
    • Winterfest Holiday Lounge: $175 per person.

    The chamber's event materials describe a VIP lounge with views of the Vanilla Ice performance from premium spots. General admission is free, so the VIP options are entirely optional. Book through the chamber.

    Tips for a better night

    • Arrive early. Doors open at 5 p.m., and lots fill as the night goes on.
    • Dress for the evening. December nights on an open polo field can get cool and damp.
    • Pick a meeting spot with your group and kids in case you get separated in the crowd.
    • Bring ear protection for little ones if you plan to sit near the stage.
    • Check the weather and the chamber's social media the day of the event for any changes.

    More holiday events in Wellington

    Winterfest opens a busy month. The village's Holiday Boat Parade lights up Lake Wellington on Saturday, Dec. 12, from 6:30 to 8 p.m., and there are tree lightings, a parade, the Jingle Bell Run and more. See our full 2026 Wellington holiday events guide, plus ways to give back this season. Polo fans can also check our 2027 polo season guide for what's next at the National Polo Center.

    Why it matters to local businesses

    For the chamber, Winterfest is also a showcase for local businesses. "For us at the chamber, it's also about supporting our local business community, creating opportunities and giving back," Green told the Town-Crier. Big free events like this bring thousands of people to one spot, which supporters say helps local vendors and sponsors. Neighbors who live near the polo grounds sometimes see it differently on event nights, with more traffic and noise, which is why the shuttle lots matter.
