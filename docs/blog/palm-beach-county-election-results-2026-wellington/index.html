Palm Beach County Election Results 2026: Wellington and the Western Communities

By Ben SiegelPublished November 4, 20264 min read

Here are Palm Beach County's results from the Nov. 3, 2026 general election for the races and questions that matter most in Wellington, Royal Palm Beach, The Acreage, Loxahatchee Groves and Westlake. All numbers are unofficial results from the Palm Beach County Supervisor of Elections, posted at 2:58 a.m. on Nov. 4 with all 814 precincts reporting. Vote-by-mail is still partly reported until after the signature cure and overseas ballot deadlines, so totals may still change slightly before certification.

Palm Beach County at a glance

Turnout
64.81%: 576,527 ballots cast out of 889,577 registered voters
Precincts
814 of 814 reporting; Election Day and early votes completely reported
Status
Unofficial. Vote-by-mail is partially reported
County questions
School tax, parks bond and library bond all received more than 50% yes (full results)

County Commission

District 6, which includes all of the western communities and most of the county west of State Road 7, according to the Town-Crier:

  • Katherine Waldron (DEM): 58.21% (50,844 votes)
  • Sara Baxter (REP): 41.79% (36,509 votes)

Katherine Waldron, a former state representative and Port of Palm Beach commissioner, is set to take office on Nov. 17, the Town-Crier reported. Sara Baxter, the current county mayor, was first elected to the District 6 seat in 2022.

District 4:

  • Marci Woodward (REP): 50.60% (47,441 votes)
  • Curtis Calabrese (DEM): 49.40% (46,314 votes)

State House, State Senate and Congress

District descriptions are from the Town-Crier's Nov. 4 results report.

  • State House District 93 (includes Wellington, Greenacres and parts of suburban Lake Worth): August Mangeney (DEM) 56.19% (40,626); Anne Gerwig (REP) 43.81% (31,669)
  • State House District 94 (includes The Acreage/Loxahatchee, Loxahatchee Groves, Westlake, parts of Royal Palm Beach and Palm Beach Gardens, and western Palm Beach County including the Glades): Elizabeth Holmes (DEM) 50.34% (38,361); Megan Weinberger (REP) 47.98% (36,560); Doug Barrow (NPA) 1.68% (1,280)
  • State Senate District 26: David Silvers (DEM) 58.04% (135,162); Rick Roth (REP) 41.96% (97,725). The seat is open because State Sen. Lori Berman is retiring.
  • U.S. House District 22 (Palm Beach County precincts; the district also includes parts of Broward, Hendry and Collier counties): Pia Dandiya (DEM) 56.22% (46,470); Casey Askar (REP) 43.78% (36,183). The Town-Crier reported Dandiya was elected to represent most of the western communities.

The new state legislators are set to be sworn in at the Legislature's organizational session on Nov. 17, according to the Town-Crier.

Indian Trail Improvement District

The Acreage's ITID board had three seats on the ballot, with 21 of 21 precincts reporting. Supervisors Patricia Farrell and Michael Johnson were incumbents, and Seat 1 was open, the Town-Crier reported.

  • Seat 1: Jay Sweet 55.09% (9,087); Kirk Ljongquist 44.91% (7,409)
  • Seat 3: Patricia "Patty" Farrell 54.57% (9,049); Jack Lloyd Bell II 45.43% (7,533)
  • Seat 5: Michael T. Johnson 55.57% (9,041); Christopher Karch 44.43% (7,229)

For background on the district, see how ITID handles drainage in The Acreage.

State constitutional amendments (Palm Beach County votes)

Florida constitutional amendments need at least 60% yes statewide to pass, so these county numbers don't decide the outcome on their own. Statewide results are reported by the Florida Division of Elections. The Town-Crier reported that Amendment 3, which increases homestead property tax exemptions, was approved statewide.

  • Amendment 1 (Article III, Section 19): Yes 40.75% (218,538); No 59.25% (317,734)
  • Amendment 2 (Article VII, Section 3): Yes 53.96% (291,790); No 46.04% (248,952)
  • Amendment 3 (homestead exemptions; Article VII, Sections 4, 6 and 9): Yes 60.00% (326,522); No 40.00% (217,637)

Amendment 3 matters for local budgets. Wellington staff estimated it could cost the village about $5.3 million in its first year; see why Wellington held its tax rate, and our explainer on what Amendment 3 means for your tax bill.

School Board and county questions

  • School Board District 4: Christina L. Romelus 61.62% (44,832); Daniel Zapata 38.38% (27,920)
  • School tax renewal: 76.87% yes
  • Parks bond (up to $280 million): 66.17% yes
  • Library bond (up to $175 million, Library District voters): 63.57% yes

Details on what each question does are in our county ballot questions guide.

Top statewide races (Palm Beach County votes)

  • Governor: David Jolly / Gwen Graham (DEM) 55.53% (317,692); Byron Donalds / Bryan Avila (REP) 43.25% (247,479); six other tickets and write-ins together about 1.2%
  • U.S. Senate: Angie Nixon (DEM) 54.58% (311,187); Ashley Moody (REP) 44.34% (252,798); Neil J. Gillespie (NPA) 1.07% (6,122)

These are Palm Beach County totals only. Statewide winners are decided by statewide totals.

What happens next

The county canvassing board will finish counting remaining vote-by-mail and overseas ballots, and results become official once certified. For every race on the ballot, including congressional, state legislative, judicial retention, Port of Palm Beach and Soil and Water races, see the Supervisor of Elections' full results page.

Frequently asked questions

Who won the Palm Beach County Commission District 6 race in 2026?

In unofficial results from the Palm Beach County Supervisor of Elections, Katherine Waldron (DEM) received 58.21% (50,844 votes) and Sara Baxter (REP) received 41.79% (36,509 votes). District 6 includes the western communities, and the Town-Crier reported Waldron is set to take office Nov. 17.

What was Palm Beach County's turnout in the Nov. 3, 2026 election?

Unofficial turnout was 64.81%, with 576,527 ballots cast out of 889,577 registered voters, according to the Supervisor of Elections.

Who represents Wellington in the Florida House after the 2026 election?

In State House District 93, which the Town-Crier says includes Wellington, August Mangeney (DEM) received 56.19% and Anne Gerwig (REP) 43.81% in unofficial results. District 94, covering The Acreage, Loxahatchee Groves, Westlake and parts of Royal Palm Beach, went 50.34% for Elizabeth Holmes (DEM) and 47.98% for Megan Weinberger (REP).

Are the 2026 Palm Beach County election results final?

Not yet. As of the morning of Nov. 4, results were unofficial, with all 814 precincts reporting but vote-by-mail still partially reported until after the signature cure and overseas ballot deadlines. Results become official after certification.
