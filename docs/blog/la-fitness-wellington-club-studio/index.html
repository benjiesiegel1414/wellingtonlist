LA Fitness Wellington Is Closed and Becoming Club Studio: What to Know

By Wellington List staffPublished October 14, 20264 min read

The LA Fitness at The Mall at Wellington Green closed on September 1, 2026 after more than two decades, but the building isn't staying empty. Parent company Fitness International plans to reopen it as Club Studio, its upscale boutique gym brand. Here's what we know about the change, what Club Studio offers, what happened to memberships and where to work out in Wellington in the meantime.

Quick answer

Is LA Fitness Wellington closed?
Yes. The LA Fitness at 10180 Forest Hill Blvd closed September 1, 2026.
What's replacing it?
Club Studio, Fitness International's luxury boutique fitness brand, confirmed by Wellington's planning director
When does Club Studio open?
No opening date has been announced
LA Fitness members
Members were given access to other LA Fitness clubs; the nearest is about 5 miles away in Lake Worth Beach

What happened to LA Fitness Wellington?

The Palm Beach Post reported in August 2026 that the roughly 35,000-square-foot LA Fitness on the edge of The Mall at Wellington Green would close September 1, 2026. The company didn't announce a reason, but Wellington planning director Tim Stillings confirmed that Fitness International plans to reopen the building as a Club Studio. The move is part of a wider shift: Fitness International has been closing or converting some LA Fitness locations around South Florida, including near Boca Raton, West Palm Beach, Stuart and Fort Lauderdale, as it pushes into higher-end clubs.

What is Club Studio?

Club Studio is Fitness International's premium brand, built around boutique-style classes and recovery rather than rows of machines. Its first Florida club opened at Miami Worldcenter in late 2025. Based on that 38,000-square-foot flagship, a Club Studio typically includes:

  • Boutique studios: a HIIT-style strength class (CS4), boxing, indoor cycling, heated yoga and reformer Pilates
  • Recovery: whole-body and localized cryotherapy, massage beds, compression and percussion therapy
  • Upscale extras: towel service, personal training, a medspa, a healthy cafe and market, and a retail shop

The Wellington club's exact layout, amenities and pricing haven't been announced, so features may differ. Expect membership to cost more than LA Fitness.

What about LA Fitness memberships?

According to the Palm Beach Post, Wellington members got access to all other LA Fitness locations starting September 1. The closest is in Lake Worth Beach, about 5 miles away. If you have questions about your contract, billing or transferring to Club Studio once it opens, contact LA Fitness member services directly.

Gyms in Wellington while you wait

  • Crunch Fitness Wellington, 12799 Forest Hill Blvd: full gym on the west end of Forest Hill
  • YouFit Gyms, 13865 Wellington Trace: budget-friendly gym on Wellington Trace
  • Planet Fitness Royal Palm Beach, 10463 Southern Blvd: open 24 hours on weekdays
  • Cat 5 Fitness, 3125 Fortune Way: group fitness classes
  • 9Round Kickboxing, 13860 Wellington Trace: 30-minute kickboxing circuits
  • JETSET Pilates, 2535 S State Road 7: reformer Pilates
  • Aerial Wellness and Rooted Wellness Club on Forest Hill Blvd: boutique wellness studios

Into racket sports? Diadem's 11-court indoor pickleball complex is coming to Forest Hill Blvd, just down the road.

Part of a bigger change at Wellington Green

The Club Studio conversion is one of several changes around The Mall at Wellington Green. The Palm Beach Improv opened on the mall's upper level in September, with Copper Blues Rock Pub & Kitchen next door, and a developer has proposed replacing the vacant Nordstrom with 620 apartments, covered in our mall redevelopment story. We'll update this page when Club Studio announces its opening date.
