    Questions

    Who reads Wellington List?

    People searching for Wellington, Florida businesses, restaurants, things to do and events: residents, families who just moved to the village, seasonal equestrian visitors and people planning a trip to the Mall at Wellington Green, the Town Center or the showgrounds.

    How much does it cost?

    A Featured listing is $19/month, or $49 once for lifetime at our founding price, and you can buy it online. For a directory, category, guide or events sponsorship, rates depend on the placement and how long you want to run. Email us with what you have in mind and we'll send current availability and pricing.

    Are sponsored placements labeled?

    Yes. Every paid placement is clearly marked as Featured or sponsored, so readers can trust the rest of the guide and your brand gets the credibility of appearing alongside it.

    Do I have to advertise to be listed?

    No. Basic listings are always free. Advertising is for businesses that want more visibility than a standard listing.

    How fast can a campaign go live?

    Featured listings usually go live within the hour after you finish the setup page. Other sponsorships usually go live within a few business days of receiving your artwork, copy and link.
