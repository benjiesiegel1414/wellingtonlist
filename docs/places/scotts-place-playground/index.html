About Scott's Place Barrier-Free Playground

Fenced, barrier-free playground built in 2010 so children of every ability can play together. It has a horse theme, wheelchair ramps, sensory equipment, a communication board, swings, slides and a reading corner, with a pavilion, restrooms and the Wellington Amphitheater right next door. Free and open daily from sunrise to sunset.

Scott's Place Barrier-Free Playground is at Wellington Town Center on Forest Hill Boulevard, next to Lake Wellington. Browse more parks and nature in our Wellington directory.

Good to know

  • Free and open sunrise to sunset
  • Next to the Wellington Amphitheater, so pair it with a free event night
  • Shaded areas make mornings the most comfortable time
