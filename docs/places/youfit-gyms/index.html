About YouFit Gyms

Budget-friendly gym with updated equipment, functional training areas and group exercise.

YouFit Gyms on Wellington Trace is a location of the Florida-based value gym chain. It offers cardio machines, strength equipment and functional training space, with amenities such as lockers and showers. YouFit's programs include personal training, HIIT-style workouts and SilverSneakers eligibility. When Ultima Fitness closed in Wellington, its standard memberships were transferred to YouFit.

YouFit Gyms is in the Wellington Trace shopping area, a cluster of local restaurants and shops in central Wellington near the Courtyard Shops. Browse more fitness and gyms in our Wellington directory.
