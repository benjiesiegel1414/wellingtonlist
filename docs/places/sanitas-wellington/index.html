About Sanitas Wellington

Primary care and walk-in medical center on Medical Park Blvd with family doctors, vaccinations, on-site lab work and imaging.

Sanitas Wellington is a primary care center on Medical Park Boulevard that serves adults and children. Family doctors provide annual checkups, screenings, women's and men's health, pediatric care, and sports and school physicals. A walk-in clinic handles time-sensitive issues such as infections, fevers, minor cuts and sprains. The center offers on-site blood work, x-ray, ultrasound, EKG and echocardiograms, plus chronic condition programs, nutrition support and telehealth visits.
