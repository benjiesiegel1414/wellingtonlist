About Wellington Town Square

Publix and CVS-anchored shopping center on Forest Hill Boulevard with restaurants, a nail salon and everyday services.

Wellington Town Square is a neighborhood shopping center on West Forest Hill Boulevard, owned and managed by Regency Centers. The center has about 108,000 square feet of retail space and is anchored by Publix and CVS. Restaurants in the plaza include Rivales Taqueria and Craft Bar and Fujisan Asian Bistro, and California Nails is among its shops. It is a convenient stop for groceries, prescriptions and a quick meal in central Wellington.

Wellington Town Square is on Forest Hill Boulevard, Wellington's main east-west corridor lined with the village's busiest plazas. Browse more shopping in our Wellington directory.
