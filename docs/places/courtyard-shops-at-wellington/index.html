About Courtyard Shops at Wellington

Publix-anchored neighborhood shopping center on Wellington Trace with restaurants, a gym, a nail salon and other local shops.

Courtyard Shops at Wellington is a grocery-anchored neighborhood shopping center on Wellington Trace near Greenview Shores Boulevard. Built in 1990, the center has roughly 129,000 square feet of space on an 18-acre site, with Publix as its anchor. Tenants have included restaurants, a frozen custard shop, a kickboxing gym, a nail salon and service businesses. Federal Realty Investment Trust sold the center in 2015 for $52.8 million.

Courtyard Shops at Wellington is in the Wellington Trace shopping area, a cluster of local restaurants and shops in central Wellington near the Courtyard Shops. Browse more shopping in our Wellington directory.
