About USA Sports Medicine Wellington

Sports-focused physical therapy clinic at Wellington Green Commons for injury rehab, post-surgical recovery and athletic performance.

USA Sports Medicine Wellington is a physical therapy clinic at Wellington Green Commons, the Whole Foods-anchored center next to the Mall at Wellington Green. It opened there in 2021 as part of a South Florida group of sports medicine clinics. The clinic focuses on sports injury treatment, post-surgical rehabilitation and non-surgical injury care, using manual therapy and therapeutic exercise. It also works with athletes and active adults on performance training, and patients can start care through direct access.

USA Sports Medicine Wellington is in the Wellington Green area beside The Mall at Wellington Green on State Road 7. Browse more health and medical in our Wellington directory.
