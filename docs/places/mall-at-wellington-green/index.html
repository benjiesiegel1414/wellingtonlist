About The Mall at Wellington Green

Wellington's regional shopping mall on State Road 7, with more than 140 stores, restaurants and entertainment spots on two levels, anchored by Macy's, Dillard's, JCPenney and City Furniture. The upper level near CMX Cinemas Wellington 10 is home to Palm Beach Improv and Copper Blues Rock Pub & Kitchen, which opened in October 2026.

The Mall at Wellington Green is an enclosed two-level regional shopping mall at Forest Hill Boulevard and State Road 7, the largest retail center in Wellington. It was developed by Taubman Centers and opened in October 2001, and it is now managed by Spinoso Real Estate Group. The mall has about 1.27 million square feet of space and more than 170 stores, with Dillard's, JCPenney, Macy's, Ashley Furniture and City Furniture among its anchors. CMX Cinemas operates a movie theater on site, and roughly 7,600 parking spaces surround the building. Former anchors include Burdines, Lord & Taylor and Nordstrom, which closed in 2019.

The Mall at Wellington Green is along the State Road 7 corridor on Wellington's eastern edge, near The Mall at Wellington Green. Browse more shopping in our Wellington directory.
