About Sicilian Oven

Wood-fired pizza and Italian dishes, a reliable family dinner pick.

Sicilian Oven Wellington is part of a South Florida group of Italian restaurants with about ten locations, including Lighthouse Point, Boca Raton, Coral Springs, Fort Lauderdale and Palm Beach Gardens. The kitchen is built around wood-fired pizza, served alongside a broader menu of Italian dishes. The Forest Hill Boulevard restaurant has a bar with craft cocktails and a happy hour. Guests can walk in and join a live online waitlist, order pickup or get delivery through DoorDash and Uber Eats, and the restaurant also handles catering, events and a Chef for a Day experience.

Sicilian Oven is in Wellington, Florida. It is one of 96 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's Pizza dining scene.
