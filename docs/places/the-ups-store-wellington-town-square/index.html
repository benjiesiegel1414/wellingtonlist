About The UPS Store (Wellington Town Square)

UPS Store at Forest Hill and South Shore handling shipping, packing, printing, mailboxes and notary service seven days a week.

The UPS Store at Wellington Town Square (store 2128) is at Forest Hill Blvd and South Shore Blvd, next to Publix and CVS. The franchise store ships UPS packages domestically and internationally, packs items and sells boxes and moving supplies, and also handles freight shipments. Its print center makes copies, business cards, brochures, banners, posters and blueprints. Other services include private mailboxes, notary service for documents such as wills, deeds and affidavits, passport photos, shredding, faxing and scanning. The store and its notary service are open seven days a week.

The UPS Store (Wellington Town Square) is on Forest Hill Boulevard, Wellington's main east-west corridor lined with the village's busiest plazas. Browse more local services in our Wellington directory.
