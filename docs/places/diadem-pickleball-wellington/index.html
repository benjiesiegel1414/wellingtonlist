About Diadem Pickleball Complex Wellington

Status: Coming soon. No official opening date yet; earlier reports targeted September 2026, and an account connected to the project has said November 2026.

An indoor pickleball complex coming to the former Circuit City site near The Mall at Wellington Green, with 11 air-conditioned courts, pickleball simulators, a golf simulator, a pro shop and Cat 5, an on-site restaurant and bar serving pizza, salads, beer and wine. It's the Wellington location of Diadem, the racket sports brand whose first complex is in Coconut Creek.
