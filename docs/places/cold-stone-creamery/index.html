About Cold Stone Creamery

Ice cream mixed to order on a frozen granite stone, plus shakes and ice cream cakes.

Cold Stone Creamery in Wellington is a franchise location of the ice cream chain, in Suite 110 at 10240 Forest Hill Blvd near The Mall at Wellington Green. Ice cream is mixed to order with candy, fruit, nuts or baked goods on a frozen granite stone and served in cups or waffle cones. The shop also sells shakes, pre-packed quarts, ice cream cupcakes and custom ice cream cakes that can be ordered ahead for pickup. Some non-dairy options are available.

Cold Stone Creamery is on Forest Hill Boulevard, Wellington's main east-west corridor lined with the village's busiest plazas. It is one of 96 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's Dessert dining scene.
