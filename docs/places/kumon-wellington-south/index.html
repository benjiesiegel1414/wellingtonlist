About Kumon Math and Reading Center Wellington - South

Local Kumon franchise center offering the Kumon after-school math and reading programs for children in Wellington.

Kumon Math and Reading Center Wellington - South is a franchise location of the Kumon after-school learning program, located on Isles View Drive in Wellington. Kumon centers teach math and reading through a self-paced worksheet method, with students working at the center and completing assignments at home between visits. Call or visit the center's Kumon page for class days, enrollment and current fees.
