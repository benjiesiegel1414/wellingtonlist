About Cluck Face Nashville Hot Chicken

Status: Coming soon to The Square at Lotis Wellington. As of mid-October 2026 the storefront signage is up, a Now Hiring sign is posted and the space is under construction; the franchisee has been targeting a mid-November 2026 opening. Confirm before you go.

Cluck Face is a fast-casual Nashville hot chicken restaurant coming to The Square at Lotis Wellington on State Road 7. The menu centers on extra-large chicken tenders, never frozen and breaded in-house, served country fried or Nashville hot as meals, combos, sliders or bowls, with heat levels from mild up to the top "Cluck It" level. The Wellington location is run by local franchisees Dave and Nancy Chapman, who also plan locations in Delray Beach and West Palm Beach.

Cluck Face Nashville Hot Chicken is in Wellington, Florida. It is one of 96 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's Chicken dining scene.
