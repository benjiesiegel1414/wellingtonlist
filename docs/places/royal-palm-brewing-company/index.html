About Royal Palm Brewing Company

Craft brewery and taproom on State Road 7 in Royal Palm Beach, the village's first brewery, with house-brewed beers that have won state competition medals.

Royal Palm Brewing Company is a craft brewery and taproom on North State Road 7 in Royal Palm Beach, about a 10-minute drive from Wellington. It opened in March 2018 as the village's first brewery. Its beers have won medals at the Best Florida Beer Championships, including three in 2026 for its Dark Mexican Honey Lager, Spruce Willis Spruce Tip IPA and Oktoberfest Marzen, and its Oktoberfest has twice been named Best Florida Beer. The taproom offers board games and snacks.

Royal Palm Brewing Company is a short drive from Wellington in Royal Palm Beach. It is one of 108 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's Craft beer dining scene.
