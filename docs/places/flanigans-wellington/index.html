About Flanigan's

The South Florida seafood, ribs and burgers institution, with a full bar.

Flanigan's is along the State Road 7 corridor on Wellington's eastern edge, near The Mall at Wellington Green. It is one of 69 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's American dining scene.
