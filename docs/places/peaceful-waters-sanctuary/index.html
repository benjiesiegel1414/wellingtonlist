About Peaceful Waters Sanctuary

A roughly 30 acre wetland preserve tucked into the back of Village Park, with about 1,500 feet of elevated boardwalk and a one mile trail. It is one of the best free birdwatching spots in Palm Beach County, with herons, wading birds and the occasional alligator.

Peaceful Waters Sanctuary is in Wellington, Florida. Browse more parks and nature in our Wellington directory.

Good to know

  • Free admission and parking
  • Enter through Village Park and follow signs to the back lot
  • Dogs and bikes are not allowed on the trails
