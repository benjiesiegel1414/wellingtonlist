About European Wax Center Royal Palm Beach

Waxing center on Southern Blvd in Royal Palm Beach offering face, body and Brazilian waxing for women and men.

European Wax Center Royal Palm Beach is a franchise waxing center on Southern Boulevard, a short drive north of Wellington. It offers waxing for the face, including brows and brow tinting, along with body waxing from arms and legs to back and chest. Bikini and Brazilian services come in several variations, and men's waxing is available. Regular clients can buy Wax Pass prepaid packages. The center is open seven days a week.
