About JoJo's Raw Bar and Grill

JoJo's Raw Bar and Grill is a laid-back neighborhood bar in the Wellington Trace plaza serving oysters, seafood, burgers and wings, with daily specials and trivia and poker nights. It's one of the spots where Wellington locals bump into friends.

JoJo's Raw Bar and Grill is in the Wellington Trace shopping area, a cluster of local restaurants and shops in central Wellington near the Courtyard Shops. It is one of 93 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's Seafood dining scene.

Good to know

  • Locals call it a fun neighborhood dive, known for burgers, wings and seafood
  • Trivia and poker theme nights are popular
  • Check the website for weekly specials and events
