About Turning Point

Turning Point is a breakfast, brunch and lunch restaurant at Lotis Wellington on State Road 7, the New Jersey-founded chain's first Florida location. Expect pancakes, French toast, omelets, eggs Benedict, salads, wraps and fresh juices in a bright, upscale-casual dining room.

Turning Point is in Wellington, Florida. It is one of 94 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's Breakfast dining scene.

Good to know

  • Weekend mornings are the busiest, so arrive before 9 a.m. or expect a wait
  • Turning Point serves breakfast and lunch only and closes at 3 p.m.
  • Free parking at Lotis Wellington; Foxtail Coffee and Cooper's Hawk are in the same plaza
