About DeVine Bistro

A polished bistro that is also a popular choice for private parties and celebrations.

DeVine Bistro is a dinner-only American bistro on the west side of State Road 7 just south of Forest Hill Boulevard, in front of the Wellington Green mall. Executive chef Dave Palmateer leads the kitchen, which serves seafood, steaks and pasta, with dishes such as pistachio-crusted salmon, Guinness-braised short ribs, seafood fra diavolo and filet mignon. The restaurant opens Tuesday through Saturday evenings, takes reservations, and runs a happy hour from 5 to 6:30 p.m. Dress is business casual.

DeVine Bistro is along the State Road 7 corridor on Wellington's eastern edge, near The Mall at Wellington Green. It is one of 90 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's American dining scene.
