About Copper Blues Rock Pub & Kitchen

Status: Opening Friday, October 16, 2026, according to local social media posts, on the upper level of The Mall at Wellington Green next to CMX Cinemas and Palm Beach Improv. Confirm with the restaurant before you go.

Copper Blues Rock Pub & Kitchen is a live music bar and restaurant opening on the upper level of The Mall at Wellington Green, next to CMX Cinemas and the Palm Beach Improv. The chain, which also has locations in Phoenix and Doral, books live music seven nights a week, with acoustic acts at happy hour and cover bands on weekends, usually with no cover charge.

Copper Blues Rock Pub & Kitchen is in the Wellington Green area beside The Mall at Wellington Green on State Road 7. It is one of 95 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's American dining scene.
