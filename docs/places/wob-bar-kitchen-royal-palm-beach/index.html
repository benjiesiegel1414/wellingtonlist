About WOB Bar & Kitchen

Craft beer bar and restaurant in Southern Palm Crossing with about 300 beers, 40 taps, Topgolf Swing Suites and a pet-friendly patio.

WOB Bar & Kitchen, formerly World of Beer, is a craft beer bar and restaurant in the Southern Palm Crossing plaza on Southern Boulevard in Royal Palm Beach. The roughly 7,000-square-foot space seats about 234 and carries around 300 beers plus 40 rotating taps, along with wine and spirits. It has two Topgolf Swing Suites for simulator games, plenty of TVs, a private room and a pet-friendly patio. Lunch and dinner are served daily, with a menu meant to pair with beer, such as burgers, bowls and a German pretzel.

WOB Bar & Kitchen is a short drive from Wellington in Royal Palm Beach. It is one of 108 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's American dining scene.
