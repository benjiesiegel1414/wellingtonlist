About The Tackeria

Tack shop in the Saddle Club Shops at South Shore Boulevard and Pierson Road, in the middle of Wellington's equestrian district.

The Tackeria is a tack and riding equipment store in the Saddle Club Shops at the corner of South Shore Boulevard and Pierson Road, about a mile north of Lake Worth Road. The location puts it close to Wellington International and many of the barns in the area's equestrian neighborhoods. The shop serves riders and horse owners looking for tack and riding gear, and it is one of several tack stores clustered in this part of town. Call the store for current hours and product availability.

The Tackeria is on South Shore Boulevard, close to the equestrian district and the Wellington International showgrounds. Browse more equestrian in our Wellington directory.
