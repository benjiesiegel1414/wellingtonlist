About Buckeye Plumbing

Family-owned plumber in the western communities offering emergency plumbing, backflow, drain cleaning, leak detection, sewer inspection and jetting, and tank and tankless water heaters.

Buckeye Plumbing is a family-owned plumbing company on Business Park Way off State Road 7, between Wellington and Royal Palm Beach. Kevin Gillum, originally from Ohio, founded it in 1983 with two trucks, and it has since grown to a fleet of about 70 trucks serving Palm Beach County, northern Broward and the Treasure Coast. The company started in new-construction plumbing and now handles service calls, backflow, water filtration, drain cleaning, leak detection and water heaters, with emergency response around the clock.
