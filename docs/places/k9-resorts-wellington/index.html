About K9 Resorts Luxury Pet Hotel Wellington

Dog boarding and doggie daycare off Fairlane Farms Road, with cage-free suites, daily playgroups and separate small dog daycare.

K9 Resorts Luxury Pet Hotel Wellington is a dog boarding and daycare facility on Fairlane Farms Road, part of the K9 Resorts franchise. Overnight guests can stay in cage-free luxury suites, executive rooms or traditional compartments. Daycare includes large dog, small dog and individualized options with daily playgroups and structured play. Staff are trained through the K9 Resorts program, and owners can book stays, upload vaccine records and message the team through a mobile app.
