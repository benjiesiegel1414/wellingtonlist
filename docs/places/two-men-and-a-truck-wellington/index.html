About TWO MEN AND A TRUCK Wellington

Franchise moving company with a Wellington office for local and long-distance moves, packing, storage and moving supplies.

TWO MEN AND A TRUCK Wellington is the local sales office of the moving franchise owned by Janelle and Joel Dowley, who also own the West Palm Beach franchise and opened the Wellington office on Fairlane Farms Road in 2016. Crews handle local and long-distance household moves, business moves, packing and unpacking, and load-only or unload-only help. The company also offers storage, including a climate-controlled vault, plus pickup and delivery and moving boxes and supplies. Its service area covers Wellington, Royal Palm Beach, Loxahatchee, Westlake, Lake Worth, Greenacres, Belle Glade and Pahokee.
