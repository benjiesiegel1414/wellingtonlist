About Cat 5 Fitness

Group fitness studio offering coached training classes.

Cat 5 Fitness is a coached group training studio in a bay on Fortune Way in Wellington's light industrial area off Forest Hill Blvd. Classes focus on strength training, hybrid training, HIIT and circuit workouts led by coaches. The facility has free weights, functional training space and weight machines, and personal training is available. Classes run early mornings through early evenings on weekdays, with a short Saturday morning session.
