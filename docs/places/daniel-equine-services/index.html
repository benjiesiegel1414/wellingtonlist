About Daniel Equine Services

Mobile equine veterinary practice in Wellington focused on sport horses, with lameness work, pre-purchase exams and surgical consults.

Daniel Equine Services is a mobile equine veterinary practice with a Wellington office and a second location in Ocala. The practice is led by a board-certified equine surgeon and focuses on performance horses, sale horses and complex cases. Services include sports medicine, lameness diagnosis and treatment, pre-purchase exams, surgical consultations, regenerative therapies, and wellness and emergency care. The office can be reached by phone or text during weekday business hours.
