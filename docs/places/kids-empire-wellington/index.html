About Kids Empire Wellington

Indoor playground at The Mall at Wellington Green with mazes, slides, ball pits and a toddler zone, plus birthday party packages.

Kids Empire Wellington is an indoor playground inside The Mall at Wellington Green. The play area is built for children ages 1 to 12 and includes a multi-level maze, winding slides, jungle gym passages, ball pits, obstacle routes, a dance floor and a separate toddler zone for ages 1 to 3. Admission is good for the whole day with same-day re-entry, and up to two adults come in free with each paying child. Socks and a signed waiver are required, and the park takes card payments only. Party packages and group outings for 10 or more kids are available.

Kids Empire Wellington is in the Wellington Green area beside The Mall at Wellington Green on State Road 7. Browse more things to do in our Wellington directory.
