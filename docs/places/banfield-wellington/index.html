About Banfield Pet Hospital

Banfield Pet Hospital in Wellington (33414) provides preventive care, vaccines, dental cleanings, spay and neuter surgery and Optimum Wellness Plans for dogs and cats. It is open seven days a week on Forest Hill Boulevard.

Banfield Pet Hospital is on Forest Hill Boulevard, Wellington's main east-west corridor lined with the village's busiest plazas. Browse more pets and vets in our Wellington directory.

Good to know

  • Open Sundays, which is rare for Wellington vets
  • Optimum Wellness Plans bundle routine exams and vaccines into a monthly payment
  • Book online or call ahead; walk-ins depend on availability
  • For after-hours emergencies, call an emergency animal hospital
