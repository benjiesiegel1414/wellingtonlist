About Village Park

Wellington's largest park at about 114 acres, with athletic fields, two gymnasiums, a skate park, a playground and a walking path of roughly 2.5 miles. It is the hub for youth sports leagues across the village.

Village Park is Wellington's largest park, covering about 114 acres on Pierson Road, and it serves as the main home for the village's youth sports leagues. It has two gymnasiums plus wrestling, cheer and multipurpose rooms, along with soccer, football and baseball fields, roller hockey rinks and pickleball courts. Outdoor features include a playground, a skate park and a walking trail of about 2.5 miles with exercise stations. Pavilions with grills, concession stands and restrooms support game days, and Peaceful Waters Sanctuary and the new Wellington Aquatics Center sit on the same grounds.

Village Park is on Pierson Road in the western part of the village. Browse more parks and nature in our Wellington directory.
