About iPlanet Nails & Lashes

Manicures, pedicures and lash services.

iPlanet Nails & Lashes is a nail and lash salon in Suite 110 at 12160 South Shore Boulevard in Wellington. Listings indicate the salon was formerly known as La Mer Nails & Spa. It offers manicures, pedicures and spa pedicures, gel and regular polish, and eyelash services. The salon is open seven days a week, with evening hours on weekdays, and appointments can be booked online.

iPlanet Nails & Lashes is on South Shore Boulevard, close to the equestrian district and the Wellington International showgrounds. Browse more beauty and spas in our Wellington directory.
