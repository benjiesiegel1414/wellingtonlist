About Wellington Aquatics Complex

The village's public pool complex at Town Center, used for lap swimming, lessons, swim teams and summer fun. Resident rates are available.

Wellington Aquatics Complex is at Wellington Town Center on Forest Hill Boulevard, next to Lake Wellington. Browse more things to do in our Wellington directory.
