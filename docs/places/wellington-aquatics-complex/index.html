About Wellington Aquatics Center

Wellington's public aquatics center at Village Park, opened in January 2026, with a 64-meter competition pool, diving boards, a zero-depth family recreation pool and Florida's first NinjaCross retractable obstacle course. Resident rates are available.

The Wellington Aquatics Center is the village's public pool complex at Village Park, which opened with a celebration on January 3, 2026. It has a full-size Olympic competition pool kept around 79 to 80 degrees, diving boards, and a zero-depth entry recreation pool kept around 83 to 85 degrees with interactive play features and shaded cabanas. It also has Florida's first NinjaCross retractable obstacle course over the water. Locker rooms, a concession stand and a multipurpose room round out the facility, and residents pay lower daily rates than non-residents.
