About Oxi Fresh Carpet Cleaning Wellington

Locally owned Oxi Fresh franchise cleaning carpet, upholstery, tile and grout and hardwood floors in Wellington, with low-moisture carpet cleaning.

Oxi Fresh Carpet Cleaning Wellington is a locally owned franchise location run by Dan Lynch and Kathleen Fix, serving the 33414 and 33449 ZIP codes and nearby neighborhoods. It uses a low-moisture carpet cleaning process with an enzyme pretreatment and an oxygen-based booster, which the company says lets carpets dry in about an hour and uses far less water than steam cleaning. Beyond carpets, it cleans upholstery, tile and grout and hardwood floors, handles commercial carpet and offers dryer vent cleaning. Pricing is set per room, and appointments can be booked online at any time.
