About O2B Kids Wellington

Play-based child care and preschool center in Wellington with VPK, after-school care and summer camp.

O2B Kids Wellington is a child care center and preschool on Professional Way in Wellington, operated under the O2B Kids name. It serves infants through children around age 5 with a play-based developmental approach, and offers Voluntary Pre-K in half-day and extended-day formats. Families can choose full-time or part-time schedules, and the center also runs after-school care and summer camp. Meals are provided.
