About PDQ

Fast-casual spot for hand-breaded chicken tenders, sandwiches and salads, with a drive-thru.

PDQ in Wellington is a fast-casual chicken restaurant on State Road 7, part of the Tampa-founded PDQ chain. The menu is built around hand-breaded buttermilk chicken tenders, crispy nuggets and chicken sandwiches, including a honey butter glazed sandwich on a brioche bun. Sides and extras include waffle fries, fresh salads, house sauces and hand-spun milkshakes. The restaurant has a dining room and a drive-thru, and offers pickup, delivery and catering.

PDQ is along the State Road 7 corridor on Wellington's eastern edge, near The Mall at Wellington Green. It is one of 108 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's Chicken dining scene.
