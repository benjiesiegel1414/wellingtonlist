About Tony Garcia State Farm

State Farm insurance agency offering auto, home, life and other coverage at Wellington Reserve.

Tony Garcia State Farm is a State Farm insurance agency in the Wellington Reserve office complex on State Road 7. Tony Garcia has served State Farm policyholders since 2002 and has earned company recognitions including Chairman's Circle and Legion of Honor. The agency writes auto, motorcycle, RV, homeowners, condo and renters insurance, plus business, life and health coverage including Medicare supplement plans. It also offers annuities, pet medical insurance and State Farm banking products, and service is available in English and Spanish.

Tony Garcia State Farm is along the State Road 7 corridor on Wellington's eastern edge, near The Mall at Wellington Green. Browse more local services in our Wellington directory.
