About Wellington Town Center and Lake Wellington

The civic heart of the village, set along Lake Wellington. Town Center brings together the amphitheater, community center, Village Hall and Scott's Place, a popular playground designed so children of all abilities can play together.

Wellington's Town Center is the village's civic campus on Forest Hill Blvd along Lake Wellington. It brings together Village Hall, the Wellington Community Center (a multi-use building of more than 28,000 square feet with rentable rooms and an open-air patio), the Wellington Amphitheater, a 9/11 memorial that includes a piece of the World Trade Center, and Scott's Place. Scott's Place is a barrier-free playground that opened in 2010, was donated by local residents in memory of their son, and was expanded in 2026 with new inclusive equipment, turf and shade. A roughly $8 million second phase of Town Center work added parking and more amphitheater lawn.

Wellington Town Center and Lake Wellington is at Wellington Town Center on Forest Hill Boulevard, next to Lake Wellington. Browse more things to do in our Wellington directory.
