About Jarocho Landscaping

Licensed and insured full-service landscaping company serving Wellington for over 20 years, with lawn care, landscape design, tree and palm work, irrigation repair and equestrian field maintenance.

Jarocho Landscaping is a Wellington-based landscaping company founded by Juan, with more than 20 years in the business. It maintains residential and commercial properties, with a particular focus on equestrian facilities and sports fields. Services include regular lawn care, landscape design and installation, tree and palm work, Bermuda grass installation and artificial turf. The company also installs and repairs irrigation systems, offers 24/7 emergency irrigation repair, and handles property cleanups.
