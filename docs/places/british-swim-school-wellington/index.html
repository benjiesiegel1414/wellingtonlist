About British Swim School at Wellington

Year-round swim lessons in small groups for babies, kids, teens and adults at an indoor pool on Fortune Way.

British Swim School at Wellington teaches swim lessons at its own pool on Fortune Way. Lessons last 30 minutes, run in small groups and enroll year-round. Levels start with parent-and-child water acclimation for babies from 3 months, move through water survival and stroke technique for children 3 and up, and include classes for teens 12 and up and adults. Group, semi-private and private formats are available, and the school publishes monthly tuition and an annual membership fee on its website.
