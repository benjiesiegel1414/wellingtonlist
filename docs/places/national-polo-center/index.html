About National Polo Center

The United States Polo Association's flagship venue, set on roughly 161 acres with seven tournament fields. Sunday polo during the winter season is a Wellington tradition, and the clubhouse hosts dining and private events.

National Polo Center is in Wellington, Florida. Browse more equestrian in our Wellington directory.

Good to know

  • Sunday matches are the classic way to experience polo
  • Group seating is available for larger parties
