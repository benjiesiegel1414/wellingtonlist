About Kura Revolving Sushi Bar

Conveyor belt sushi where plates come to you, a fun pick for kids and groups.

Kura Revolving Sushi Bar at Lotis Wellington is a location of the Japanese conveyor-belt sushi chain, which held its grand opening here in April 2026. Plates travel past each booth on a two-level conveyor under ventilated lids, and an Express Belt delivers items ordered from tableside touch panels, while robots bring drinks. The menu has more than 100 items, with sushi plates at a flat $3.90 plus ramen, udon, side dishes and desserts. Returning empty plates through a table slot counts toward the Bikkura Pon prize game, which is popular with kids. Reservations can be made up to a week ahead through the Kura Sushi Rewards app or website.

Kura Revolving Sushi Bar is in Wellington, Florida. It is one of 108 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's Japanese dining scene.
