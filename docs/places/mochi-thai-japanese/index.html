About Mochi Thai and Japanese Restaurant

Cozy family-owned Thai and Japanese restaurant in Kobosko's Crossing serving pad thai, curries and fresh sushi, with online ordering available.

Mochi Thai and Japanese Restaurant is in Wellington, Florida. It is one of 95 places to eat and drink in our Wellington restaurant guide, and part of the village's Thai and Japanese dining scene.

Good to know

  • Opens for dinner, so check hours before you go
  • Online ordering available for takeout
  • Vegetarian dishes available
