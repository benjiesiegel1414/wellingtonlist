Things to do in Wellington, FL

Parks, preserves, world class equestrian venues, shopping and easy day trips.

Advertisement Sponsor WellingtonList.comPromote your local business or service here, in front of Wellington residents every day. Become a sponsor

Wellington is best known as the winter equestrian capital of the world, but there is a lot more here for families and visitors. Start with the free boardwalk at Peaceful Waters Sanctuary, catch a free show at the Wellington Amphitheater, spend a winter Sunday at the National Polo Center, or climb the observation tower at the Wellington Environmental Preserve. For more ideas, see our events calendar.

17 places

Equestrian venue

Wellington International

The showgrounds at the center of Wellington's winter season and home of the Winter Equestrian Festival. For thirteen weeks each winter, show jumpers, hunters and equitation riders from across the United States and dozens of countries compete here, and spectators can watch much of the action.

Polo venue

National Polo Center

The United States Polo Association's flagship venue, set on roughly 161 acres with seven tournament fields. Sunday polo during the winter season is a Wellington tradition, and the clubhouse hosts dining and private events.

WellingtonDetails
Equestrian venue

Equestrian Village (Global Dressage Festival)

The home of the Adequan Global Dressage Festival, which brings international dressage competition to Wellington each winter. Evening freestyle performances set to music are a highlight for spectators who are new to the sport.

Equestrian PreserveDetails
Nature preserve

Peaceful Waters Sanctuary

A roughly 30 acre wetland preserve tucked into the back of Village Park, with about 1,500 feet of elevated boardwalk and a one mile trail. It is one of the best free birdwatching spots in Palm Beach County, with herons, wading birds and the occasional alligator.

Inside Village ParkDetails
Park and athletic complex

Village Park

Wellington's largest park at about 114 acres, with athletic fields, two gymnasiums, a skate park, a playground and a walking path of roughly 2.5 miles. It is the hub for youth sports leagues across the village.

Pierson RoadDetails
Nature preserve

Wellington Environmental Preserve

A 365 acre preserve at the Marjory Stoneman Douglas Everglades Habitat on the western edge of the village, with paved trails and a six story observation tower overlooking the Everglades landscape. Wide open and quiet, it is great for long walks and family bike rides.

Western WellingtonDetails
Outdoor venue

Wellington Amphitheater

The village's outdoor stage at Wellington Town Center, hosting concerts, movie nights, food truck events and seasonal festivals. Bring a blanket or lawn chair and make an evening of it by the lake.

Town CenterDetails
Community center

Wellington Town Center and Lake Wellington

The civic heart of the village, set along Lake Wellington. Town Center brings together the amphitheater, community center, aquatics complex, Village Hall and Scott's Place, a popular playground designed so children of all abilities can play together.

Town CenterDetails
Public pool

Wellington Aquatics Complex

The village's public pool complex at Town Center, used for lap swimming, lessons, swim teams and summer fun. Resident rates are available.

Town CenterDetails
Shopping mall

The Mall at Wellington Green

Wellington's regional shopping mall on State Road 7, with department stores, national retailers, restaurants and entertainment under one roof. The surrounding Wellington Green area is one of the busiest dining corridors in the village.

State Road 7Details
Shopping center

The Village Green

A newer shopping and medical complex along State Road 7, anchored by Trader Joe's and a Cleveland Clinic facility, just east of the K-Park site where Village Landing is being built.

State Road 7Details
Mixed-use town center

Village Landing (coming 2028)

Related Ross is building Village Landing, a 71 acre walkable town center on the former K-Park land, with shops, restaurants, offices, a 180 room Hilton hotel and a central lake with a boardwalk. It is expected to open in fall 2028.

K-ParkDetails
Private school

Wingrove Academy (opening 2028)

A new nonprofit PreK through 12 independent school being developed by ElevateED on the back portion of the K-Park site, with the first phase planned to open in August 2028.

K-ParkDetails
Drive-through safari park

Lion Country Safari

A drive-through safari park a short drive north of Wellington, where you tour animal habitats from your own car, then park for a walk-through area with rides, animal encounters and a water play zone. A classic day out for families.

Loxahatchee (nearby)Details
County park

Okeeheelee Park

One of Palm Beach County's largest parks, just east of Wellington on Forest Hill Blvd, with lakes, trails, a nature center, a golf course and a BMX track.

West Palm Beach (nearby)Details
Fairgrounds and living history park

South Florida Fairgrounds and Yesteryear Village

Home of the South Florida Fair each winter and events year round, plus Yesteryear Village, a living history park that recreates Florida life from the late 1800s to the mid 1900s.

Southern Blvd (nearby)Details
Botanical garden

Mounts Botanical Garden

Palm Beach County's oldest and largest public garden, with themed gardens, tropical fruit trees and a striking floating walkway over water.
